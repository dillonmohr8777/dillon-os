The Situation

WCK ran BambooHR, Airtable, Gmail based approvals, and a3innuva with no unified HR platform. That left data gaps, manual errors, and compliance exposure stretched across 24 countries and multiple entity structures, with no single source of truth for the workforce.

Why Align HCM

Align designed a purpose built Relief Contractor surge onboarding model: bilingual, field deployable, and configured for multi country compliance from day one. It was paired with proven Sage Intacct integration expertise. No other bidder offered either one.

The Outcome

WCK selected Align over a Big 4 consulting firm and a specialized HCM implementation partner, citing operational depth, mission alignment, and superior proposal specificity. Flexibility on commercial terms and rate structure sealed the partnership.

The Operating Reality

  • Field offices in Jordan, Ukraine, Egypt, and Israel running independently
  • Compliance across 24 countries and multiple entity structures
