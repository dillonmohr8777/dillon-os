# Align HCM SEO Blog Style Reference

Derived from analysis of 6 published SEO blogs in `SEO/AlignHCM/Blogs/` (April 2026 batch). Use this as the template whenever drafting new Align HCM SEO content.

---

## File Format

Each blog lives as a standalone `.md` file in `SEO/AlignHCM/Blogs/`.

### Required Frontmatter

```yaml
---
title: "Full blog title in quotes"
keyword: primary target keyword (no quotes, lowercase)
seo_score: 0.0
date: YYYY-MM-DD
status: draft | ready-to-publish | published
---
```

---

## Document Structure

```
# H1: Title (matches or closely mirrors title frontmatter)

## Meta Description
One sentence, under 160 characters. Contains keyword naturally. No em dashes.

## Content

[Opening hook paragraph -- no header, just text]

## H2: Main Section
### H3: Subsection (when needed)

## How Align HCM Helps [or similar CTA section]

## Internal Linking Opportunities
- [[wikilink-to-other-blog]]
- /page-path
```

## Word Count and Score Targets

- Target: approximately 950 words
- SEMrush content score target: 9.5+
- Observed scores in current batch: 8.7 to 9.2 (room to improve)

---

## Opening Hook Pattern

Lead with a direct, slightly provocative statement that reframes the reader's assumption. Examples from the batch:

- "The HCM you picked is not the HCM you get."
- "Mid market HCM software is the most competitive segment in the HR tech market. Every major vendor wants you."
- "Switching from ADP is a bigger decision than most vendors admit during the sales cycle."

No setup. No "In today's world." No "As companies grow." Just the point.

---

## Voice and Tone

Audience: HR directors, VPs of HR, CFOs, COOs at companies 200-5,000 employees. They know payroll. They know UKG, ADP, Workday by name. They do not need definitions of basic terms.

Tone qualities:
- Practitioner-level expertise, not vendor marketing
- Directional and opinionated (not hedge-everything consulting language)
- Uses "we" when referencing Align HCM
- Uses "you" for the reader
- Respects the reader's intelligence

What to avoid:
- "In today's dynamic business environment"
- "Leverage synergies"
- "Streamline your workflows"
- "Game-changer"
- "Holistic approach"
- Anything that sounds like a press release or a vendor landing page

---

## Writing Rules (Non-Negotiable)

- No em dashes. Ever. Use a period, comma, or colon instead. Restructure if needed.
- No starting sentences with: And, But, Or, It is, Do not, That is, This is.
- Use contractions (it's, you're, we've, don't).
- Bullet character only for lists. Never dash bullets.
- Numbered lists for sequential steps/phases.
- Bold for key terms or section labels inside lists, not for decoration.

---

## Section Patterns

### Comparison Lists (Platforms)
Use H3s for each platform. One to three sentences per platform. Lead with the differentiating use case, not the feature list.

Example pattern:
```
### UKG Pro
Best for [specific use case]. [One sentence on strength]. [One sentence on where it fits vs. where it doesn't].
```

### Numbered Phases or Processes
Use numbered H3s when walking through a sequential process. Label phases with time estimates when relevant (e.g., "Discovery (2 to 4 weeks)").

### Good vs. Bad / When to Do / When Not To
Binary framing works well for decision posts. Use short bullet lists under plain headers.

### Closing CTA Section
Always ends with a section explaining how Align HCM specifically helps. Keep it under 100 words. Specific, not generic. Reference real capabilities (UKG, Dayforce, ADP, mid-market, named processes).

## SEO Score Improvement Tactics

When a draft scores below 9.5:
- Add more semantically related terms (check SEMrush recommendations)
- Ensure keyword appears in H1, first paragraph, at least one H2, and meta description
- Increase content depth on the section SEMrush flags as thin
- Add a FAQ section if the query has question-based search intent
- Check that all recommended related terms appear at least once
