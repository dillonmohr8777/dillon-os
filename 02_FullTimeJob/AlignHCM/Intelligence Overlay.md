## Executive operating thesis

Align can compound authority by connecting HCM buyer education, implementation
expertise, named subject-matter experts, verified customer proof, and SmartCare's
post-go-live lifecycle into one measurable journey. The content engine is proven
at the production level; the next maturity step is query-to-opportunity and
answer-engine measurement.

## Current evidence snapshot

- Delivered work includes 18 finalized SEO blogs, vendor buyer guides, sales
  enablement, executive content, case studies, brand collateral, and SmartCare
  assets.
- The canonical project contains an attribution baseline, competitive dashboard,
  press watchdog, HubSpot property discovery, and recurring content workflows.
- Acceptance, current Search Console demand, AI citations, influenced pipeline,
  and downstream revenue must be refreshed from live systems before reporting.

## Demand and answer map

- **Core entities:** Align HCM, SmartCare, named experts, HCM platforms,
  implementation, payroll, workforce management, and post-go-live optimization.
- **Decision journeys:** platform selection, implementation risk, migration and
  readiness, service comparison, ongoing support, optimization maturity, ROI,
  and customer proof.
- **Evidence assets:** case studies, buyer guides, expert articles, assessments,
  calculators, webinars, videos, and primary research.
- **Exclusions:** unsupported vendor claims, invented customer outcomes, private
  sales data, and claims not approved by the relevant stakeholder.
