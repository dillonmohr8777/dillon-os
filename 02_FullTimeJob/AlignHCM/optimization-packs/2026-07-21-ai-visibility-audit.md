## Homepage issues (AI citation)

Fetched [alignhcm.com](https://www.alignhcm.com/):

• **Title is fine** (“HCM Implementation and SmartCare Support”). The **H1 is soft** (“We’re here to help you with your HR, payroll, and workforce management software”) and does not assert a quotable specialist claim.

• **Thin answers:** five service tiles with one-line blurbs. No FAQ, no “who we’re for,” no platform-by-platform short answers AIs can lift.

• **Proof is vague** (“hundreds of projects,” “100+ 5-star reviews”) without structured facts (platforms, industries, post-go-live model).

• **Strong assets are below the fold** (buyer guides, [Dayforce optimization](https://www.alignhcm.com/blog/dayforce-optimization-after-go-live)). Homepage does not compress them into citeable blocks.

• **Copy glitch:** “Speak to an expertASAP” looks unfinished and weakens trust signals.

• **Fix:** Rewrite H1 to “Align HCM: UKG, Dayforce, Workday, and ADP implementation and SmartCare post-go-live support,” add a 6–8 question homepage FAQ, and put SmartCare + one platform proof above testimonials.

## Pattern that already works

The Dayforce optimization post ([alignhcm.com/blog/dayforce-optimization-after-go-live](https://www.alignhcm.com/blog/dayforce-optimization-after-go-live)) is the model: definition, priority table, roadmap, FAQ, SmartCare CTA. That is why Align gets cited there and not on UKG/Workday/ADP specialist queries.

## AI lead review rubric

Score each inbound AI lead 0–2 per row. Pursue ≥6/8. Park or nurture below.

| Factor | 0 | 1 | 2 |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Landing page | Vague homepage / wrong platform | Relevant blog or partner page | Exact service page (impl, SmartCare, platform) matching intent |
| Offer fit | Vendor shopping only / wrong product | Implementation or optimization curiosity | Clear post-go-live pain, punch list, capacity gap, or active platform live |
| Company size | &lt;100 or mega-enterprise with big SI already locked | Mid-market stretch either side | Align ICP mid-market (roughly hundreds to low thousands of employees) |
| Source | Pure brand vanity / competitor name collision | Generic “HCM consulting” | Query maps to a priority service (UKG/Dayforce/SmartCare/ADP/Workday payroll) |

**Quick kill rules**

• SmartCare EHR / healthcare portal intent → not Align.

• “ADP implementation specialist” job seeker → not a buyer.

• Workday payroll with official SI already selected and no client-side need → low odds unless they ask for independent optimization/support.
