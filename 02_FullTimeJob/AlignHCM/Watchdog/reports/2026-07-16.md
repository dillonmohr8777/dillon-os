Site is up and indexed by Google. Traffic is flat to declining (288 views so far this week vs a 613 peak in mid May) and form submissions have slid from 12-16/week in May to 3 this week. There are 8 open issues; the top 3 are structural SEO problems that are actively costing ranking equity.

## Critical issues (fix first)

### 1. All case studies live on the default HubSpot domain, not alignhcm.com
Every case study (GTAA, Redberry, Kimberly-Clark, AWP, Resorts World, Alsco, Driscoll's, Vibra, Beumer, Arrow, InVision) is served from `242825734.hs-sites-na2.com/case-studies/...`. All the authority, backlinks, and keyword relevance those pages earn accrues to a HubSpot system subdomain instead of alignhcm.com. Case studies are the strongest commercial-intent content the company has.
**Fix:** move structured case-study content onto the connected `www.alignhcm.com` domain (Settings > Website > Domains, or rebuild as site pages under /case-studies), then 301 the hs-sites URLs.

### 2. Duplicate UKG buyer's guide is cannibalizing itself
Two live posts with the same title "The Strategic Buyer's Guide to UKG": `/blog/the-strategic-buyers-guide-to-ukg` (61 views/30d) and `/blog/ukg-buyers-guide` (31 views/30d). Google has to pick one and splits signals between both.
**Fix:** keep the stronger URL, merge content, 301 the other.

### 3. Resolved after audit: the site returned 403 to non-browser crawlers
Two independent fetch paths both got HTTP 403 on every page including robots.txt and sitemap.xml. Googlebot clearly gets through (site is indexed), but generic and AI crawlers (GPTBot, ClaudeBot, PerplexityBot) appear blocked. In 2026 a large share of HCM software buying research happens in AI assistants; being unfetchable means Align HCM cannot be cited in those answers. It also blocks SEO audit tools.
**Resolution:** the block was removed, robots.txt is reachable, and llms.txt was published through the terminal-only HubSpot Files and URL Redirect APIs. Keep the automated probes active and reopen this issue immediately on any 403 regression.

## High-priority issues

### 4. Legacy WordPress-era URLs still indexed on the apex domain
Google still indexes `alignhcm.com/welcome/` (old tagline "HCM Simplified") and `alignhcm.com/news/`. Stale pages compete with current pages for brand queries.
**Fix:** 301 all legacy apex paths to their current www equivalents.

### 5. Blog publishes volume but almost nothing ranks
About 50 posts; outside the top 6, most get under 10 views/month and blog bounce runs 95-100%. Meanwhile the Workday buyer's guide holds readers for 11+ minutes, which is an exceptional engagement signal going to waste.
**Fix:** shift from net-new volume to consolidation: pillar page per platform (Workday, UKG, Dayforce, Paylocity, HiBob, ADP) with the buyer's guide as the pillar, implementation/optimization posts interlinked as the cluster, and a lead magnet CTA inside each guide.

### 6. Untitled tracked page with meaningful traffic
Content ID `238611079877` shows 53 views/30d but returns no title or URL metadata. Likely a landing page missing SEO metadata.
**Fix:** open it in HubSpot (search the content ID), set htmlTitle and meta description or unpublish it.

## Housekeeping

7. **Analytics pollution:** the sandbox domain (`...sandbox.hs-sites-na2.com`), two Netlify microsites, and HubSpot meeting pages all mix into portal analytics. The watchdog now excludes them from KPIs.
8. **Slug typo:** `/blog/paylocity-vs-paycheck-best-platform` spells Paychex as "paycheck". If the post ranks at all, leave it; otherwise fix slug with a 301.
9. **404 page:** 213 views on `/404` in the trailing default window, but only 3 in the last 30 days. Historical link cleanup appears to have worked; watchdog monitors this daily with an alert threshold of 5/day.

## What's working (protect these)

## Ranking growth plays (beyond fixes)

1. Case study migration (issue 1) plus Case Study schema markup and links from each partner page to the matching case study.
2. Partner pages get traffic but 90-100% bounce: add proof (case study cards), a platform-specific CTA, and FAQ schema targeting "Workday implementation partner", "UKG optimization consultant" style queries.
3. Refresh and expand the Workday buyer's guide (best engagement on site) and promote it via the 5 LinkedIn profiles.
4. Fix AI-crawler access (issue 3), then track AI-referral landing sessions as their own segment.
5. Internal linking pass: every blog post should link to one service page and one case study minimum.
