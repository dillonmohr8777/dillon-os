# Brand Guidelines

**Summary:** canonical Align HCM visual tokens, verified 2026-08-10 against the
shipped Aug 2026 editorial PDF set and the production carousel/motion tokens.

## Logo

- Exact mark, reverse variant (white wordmark + orange X and dots):
  `assets/align-hcm-logo-reverse.png` (268x108, transparent background,
  extracted from the shipped editorial PDFs; not a recreation).
- Place on navy `#0A1628` or another dark panel, sized generously in mastheads.
  No recolors, no boxes behind it, no low-contrast placements.

## Fonts

- Web, graphics, motion: Inter + DM Sans + Syne (production tokens).
- Alternates seen in collateral: Plus Jakarta Sans, Poppins, Barlow.
- Office documents (xlsx/docx): Arial.

## Signature effects

- CTA gradient: `linear-gradient(135deg, #F05A28 0%, #FF6B35 100%)`.
- Glass panels on navy: backdrop-blur 20 to 24px, 1px white border at ~12% alpha.
- Ambient glow blobs: ~120px blur, corner placement, low opacity.
- Accent underline animation on a headline word: scaleX 0 to 1, 0.6s, origin left.

## Tone

Professional, consultative, expert-level HCM knowledge. Never salesy. Speak to
decision-makers and implementation leaders who already understand UKG, Workday,
and payroll operations.

## Writing rules

- **No em dashes anywhere.** Ever. Use periods, commas, colons, or restructure.
- Use contractions.
- Bullet character only, never dashes as list markers.

## SmartCare messaging

Ongoing expert access. Platform optimization. Post-go-live support.
Stabilize → Essentials → Accelerate → Transform maturity ladder.
