What I'd build, and why I know it works

Empeon runs zero paid search, sits 93% on organic, and has thin AI-assistant visibility — the same channel map I already instrumented and grew at another HCM company. Four moves, each one I've shipped before.

01

Instrument before spending

Full UTM coverage and session-level source capture first, so every dollar is provable. The gap in my last program is the first thing I'd close here.

Evidence: the two-views test above
02

Own the AI-assistant channel

AEO/GEO so Empeon is the answer ChatGPT gives for healthcare payroll and HCM. I have a 98-scored buyer that arrived exactly this way.

Evidence: Therapeutic Associates trail
03

Open paid search deliberately

A channel at zero against ADP, Paycom and Paylocity is upside, not a weakness — entered after tracking exists, not before.

04

LinkedIn content → named pipeline

Map social content to specific companies in the CRM, the way I traced eight named accounts to LinkedIn — plus agentic workflows to run it daily at low cost.
