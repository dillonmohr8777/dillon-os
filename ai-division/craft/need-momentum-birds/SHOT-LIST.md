# Need Momentum: Make room for the next customer

**Current canonical storyboard.** Draft direction, awaiting story/transition review; no artwork generated, 30s animation implemented, or video exported in this pass. Supersedes the earlier 15s shot list for this launch; `briefs/15s-hero-brief.md` records that historical treatment. The existing 15s composition is unchanged.

**Premise:** A small-business owner's scattered work becomes a connected sequence: understand a customer's request, prepare the response, plan the follow-up, and return attention to the person. A cardinal picks up the first task; doves join as work finds its direction. The people remain the point of the story.

**Audience / delivery assumptions:** Small-business owners; staged 1920x1080, 16:9, 30 fps master. Exactly 900 frames / 30 seconds. Distribution, service scope, offer, destination and audio budget are unverified. The illustrative workflow is a creative proposal, not a verified product demonstration or customer result. No performance figures, guaranteed bookings, free offer or invented URL.

**Emotional movement:** Recognizable overload → one manageable action → human confidence → coordinated work → room for customers. Brand role: introduce Need Momentum's AI division as practical assistance guided by people. Takeaway: connect the work that supports a customer relationship.

## Visual and motion lock proposal

Blue and cream are the dominant paper fields. Exact blue is pending an original brand token; do not sample a screen preview and label it approved. Keep the authentic logo colors unchanged. Doves remain cream/white; cardinal red is a small natural motif, not an orange field. Retain the existing Outfit Bold / Source Sans 3 fonts as a working assumption until brand font evidence is reconciled.

One family of cut edges, soft directional contact shadows and photographed fibers. Camera stays above or just oblique to the paper stage; a maximum 5% push or pull within a scene. Physical sliding, overlapping and short stepped settles carry the movement. No whole-image Ken Burns sequence passed off as a flock animation; no wing warps on baked birds. Birds use independent cutouts with subtle rotation and translation; a wing-cycle asset is optional, not required. No robot mascot, dashboard montage, fake testimonials, generated logos, or repeated logo posters.

Layers: shared background → stationary people/tableau when needed → task papers and route → independent birds → contact shadows → final typography → original logo. Foreground wipes must cover the frame before changing backgrounds. Keep headline region inside x=120–1800 and y=100–900; plan crop variants only after master review. The current 16:9 scenes require layout adaptation for 9:16, not automatic cropping.

**Audio proposal:** Human, conversational read of the six main headlines (S06: “Need Momentum. Introducing our AI division. Let's put AI to work.”), with space between ideas. Dry paper slips and one soft pencil sound at the human edit. A light pulse may gather with the flock; no cinematic triumph suggesting promised outcomes. No audio asset selected, licensed or generated. All meaning must remain clear muted. Captions remain a separate delivery layer if voiceover is produced.
