# Need Momentum - scrapbook birds QA gates

Any failed gate is **hold**. These gates apply before a render leaves local staging.

- [ ] **Field:** full frame reads as warm cream `#FBF8F4`; paper grain/deckle stays subtle and does not obscure copy.
- [ ] **Accent budget:** signal orange `#E27113` is limited to route/annotation details and measures at or below ~8% of the frame; it is not body text on cream.
- [ ] **IP boundary:** no Anthropic, Claude, or Fable names, marks, logos, characters, screenshots, exact compositions, or trade dress; no generated third-party brand marks.
- [ ] **Type-safe areas:** render type only in Remotion using Outfit Bold for headline/lockup and Source Sans 3 for captions/notes; keep all copy inside the central 80% title-safe area (at least 10% from each edge), with no crop or wrap.
- [ ] **Plate integrity:** birds, paper edges, tape, and shadows read as physical collage; prompts, source provenance, and SHA-256 hashes are recorded before any generation; approved Momentum mark is sourced and composited last, never generated or recolored.
- [ ] **Motion/render:** 30 fps, 450 frames, 1920x1080; entrances and settles are readable, no glow/crossfade/flash, and the final flock holds before the lockup.
- [ ] **Human layer:** a human reviews the prompt set, contact sheet/open-mid-close-final frames, exact copy, contrast, logo provenance, rights, and the orange-area check before any future publish. Automated send/post/publish/spend remains blocked.
