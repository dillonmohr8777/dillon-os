# IMMOHRTAL guides: the homepage world in Read mode

## Scope and comprehension

- Mode: Read.
- Routes: The ten indexable guide articles under `/insights/`.
- Job: Make complex website, search, and AI-worker topics understandable without losing primary-source support.
- Primary action: Understand the answer, inspect its sources, continue to a related guide, or start a focused website conversation.

## Reading contract

- Keep the homepage navigation, orbit hero, exact typography, positioning strip, closing statement, and footer so each guide belongs to the same brand world.
- Move the article body into a calm proof-paper field with a useful sticky contents rail, a prominent direct answer, readable measure, clear tables, and strong Unbounded section headings.
- Let the reading structure lead. Do not force article paragraphs into marketing cards or add decorative motion that competes with comprehension.
- Preserve visible authorship, publication data, official sources, related reading, canonical metadata, Article schema, and crawlable static HTML.

## Responsive and accessibility contract

- Collapse the contents rail above the article on smaller screens while keeping DOM order aligned with reading order.
- Keep tables intentionally scrollable, body copy at a readable mobile size, links descriptive, and focus visible.
- Reduced motion shows the complete final layout with no hidden article content.
