# IMMOHRTAL inner sales pages: the homepage system, extended

## Scope and conversion

- Mode: Persuade.
- Routes: About, Better Website, Get Found, AI Workers, Pricing, Work, Contact, and Guides index.
- Job: Carry the homepage's visual conviction into clear, indexable explanations and one direct conversation path.
- Primary action: Open the contact page or Dillon's email channel.
- Authority: The homepage Particle Proof Conveyor, its exact fonts, navigation rail, proof fields, browser frames, and closing statement.

## Structural contract

- Begin with the same floating navigation rail and an orbit-stage hero with one dominant outcome statement.
- Follow with the shared positioning strip, then alternate dark observatory and pale proof fields according to the content's job.
- Use asymmetrical thesis-and-support grids, generous section separation, hairline fact groups, and ordered steps only where sequence matters.
- Present website work as inspectable browser frames in a horizontal rail, not as a generic card grid.
- On Pricing, use the Particle Proof Ledger sequence: lead with the two approved bundle anchors, show each search visibility service and bundle, show each paid media service and bundle, separate every custom-scoped engagement, then explain how to choose the smallest sufficient plan before the shared system links and closing statement.
- End every route with the same IMMOHRTAL closing statement and compact homepage footer.

## Responsive and accessibility contract

- Keep all content within 320px without accidental overflow.
- Preserve 46px mobile actions, visible focus, keyboard navigation, reduced motion, and content-visible no-script behavior.
- Self-host and verify Unbounded Variable, Manrope Variable, and Foundry Mono. System display fallbacks are not an acceptable shipped state.
- Motion may reveal content during normal scrolling, but content remains visible by default and fully present under reduced motion.
