Relay + Proof

Design, discovery, and handoff belong together

A website is not finished when it looks polished. Customers need to understand the offer, search systems need to discover and interpret the important pages, and the business needs to receive useful context when someone acts.

That is why these services connect. Web design creates the public experience. Technical SEO gives it a clean structure. Content and schema make the business relationships explicit. HubSpot and CRM agents improve the handoff after interest becomes action.

The crew changes with the job

  • Scout finds the friction

    Live-page inspection, crawl paths, source checks, mobile behavior, and evidence collection.

  • Atlas connects the system

    Page architecture, entity relationships, service paths, lifecycle stages, and operating context.

  • Forge builds the change

    Responsive interfaces, reusable templates, production code, structured pages, and performance repairs.

  • Relay moves the context

    Forms, CRM routing, research packets, approvals, and exact handoffs between systems and people.

  • Proof tries to stop the release

    Accessibility, responsive behavior, links, schema, console output, evidence, and production verification.
