## Qualification floor

Approach a prospect only when all five conditions are true:

1. The company is a real service business that fits IMMOHRTAL's website, visibility, content, CRM, or bounded-agent offer.
2. The public observation is current enough to be useful and can be reproduced from a source URL.
3. The observation creates a plausible business problem without claiming private outcomes.
4. One IMMOHRTAL page directly supports the proposed conversation.
5. The recipient's role plausibly owns or influences that problem.

## Disqualifiers

- A personal address with no clear business relationship.
- A role unrelated to the observed problem.
- A trigger obtained from private, gated, or sensitive information.
- A message that requires an invented compliment, benchmark, result, or customer comparison.
- A prospect whose only fit is paid media. IMMOHRTAL is positioned first as a website and AI implementation studio.
- A market where the planned outreach has not passed the applicable consent and legal review.
- Any account already assigned to a client, partner, or active conversation without exact routing and ownership checks.

## Offer discipline

The first offer should be small enough to evaluate without a sales call:

- A three-point page review.
- An exact crawlability finding and reproduction path.
- A one-service content architecture sketch.
- A one-form handoff map.
- A bounded workflow and approval map.

Do not promise rankings, AI citations, traffic, leads, revenue, automation savings, or a full strategy before the underlying evidence and scope exist.
