# Website page to outreach routing map

Canonical origin: `https://www.immohrtalmarketing.com` (the apex is acceptable as a short outreach URL only while its one-hop permanent redirect remains verified)

Use one link in a first-touch email. The link must support the exact observation and offer. Do not default every prospect to the homepage or contact page.

## First-touch routing

| Outreach angle | Primary link | Use when | Evidence required before linking |
|---|---|---|---|
| Distinctive website redesign | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/web-design/` | The current site undersells a specific service business or reads like a template | Current page capture and a precise design, hierarchy, or clarity observation |
| Improve the existing site | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/web-design-optimization/` | The identity is credible but a live page has bounded usability, accessibility, loading, or CTA friction | Reproduction path, viewport or device, timestamp, and affected task |
| Technical SEO | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/technical-seo/` | A crawl, HTML, link, status, canonical, redirect, sitemap, or robots issue is reproducible | Exact URL, response or DOM evidence, and a caveat that impact is not yet proven |
| AEO and GEO | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/aeo-geo/` | A real customer question or entity fact is unclear, disconnected, or unsupported | Question, source page, missing information, and no promise of AI placement |
| Content and schema | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/content-schema/` | Services, locations, people, proof, or guides need a coherent public architecture | Specific inconsistency or disconnected content path; no schema-ranking claim |
| Website to CRM | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/hubspot-crm-agents/` | The public form path or a publicly named CRM creates a relevant handoff question | Public form evidence; no inference about private CRM state |
| Business agents | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/business-agents/` | One repeated, bounded workflow is supported by a current public signal | Observable signal and a clear prepare-versus-approve boundary |
| Broad problem, unclear service | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/services/` | The public evidence spans two or more connected service areas and forcing one diagnosis would be premature | A concise statement of what is known and what still needs inspection |
| Broad educational resource | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/insights/` | The prospect asks for the complete guide library or wants to evaluate several related topics | All ten guide cards, descriptions, routes, authorship, and citations verified live |
| Proof of design range | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/work/` | The prospect explicitly asks to see work or visual range | Live project cards and outbound project links verified before use |
| Founder accountability | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/about/` | The prospect asks who performs the work or how approval is handled | Founder name, portrait, public-safe claims, and all links verified |
| Start a scoped conversation | `https://immohrtalmarketing.com/contact/` | The prospect has replied with interest or asked for the next step | Mailto recipient, subject, explanatory copy, and desktop/mobile draft-opening behavior verified; no hosted form is claimed |

## Whole-site routing rules

1. The homepage is the brand overview, not the default cold-outreach link.
2. Service pages support commercial intent. Guides support evaluation and education.
3. `Work` is evidence of design range, not evidence of an unapproved outcome or metric.
4. `Contact` is a next step after interest, unless the prospect specifically asked how to start.
5. A page may be linked only after its canonical, title, main heading, mobile layout, internal links, academic citations where applicable, and CTA have been checked on the live domain.
6. The linked page must say IMMOHRTAL Marketing Solutions and use the new canonical origin. Any former public brand or origin is a blocker.
