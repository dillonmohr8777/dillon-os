# IMMOHRTAL outreach launch kit

Status: **STAGED, NOT SENT**

Prepared: 2026-08-25

Canonical website: `https://immohrtalmarketing.com`

## What this kit is

This is a review-ready outreach system for IMMOHRTAL Marketing Solutions. It translates the verified website positioning, service architecture, and keyword research into prospect segments, focused offers, concise messages, and exact page routing.

It does not contain purchased lists, scraped personal data, invented personalization, client claims, testimonials, performance figures, or conversion claims.

## What this kit is not

- No email, LinkedIn message, form submission, Slack message, or other outreach was sent.
- No mailbox, social account, CRM, contact database, or sending platform was accessed.
- No outreach domain, DNS record, mailbox authentication, or legal footer was assumed to be ready.
- No message may be used until the live-readiness gate in `pre-outreach-readiness.md` passes.

## Files

- `segment-offer-matrix.md`: who to approach, what public signal makes the outreach relevant, and which bounded offer fits.
- `message-library.md`: concise email and LinkedIn drafts with evidence placeholders and one clear ask.
- `page-routing-map.md`: the one best IMMOHRTAL page to use for each outreach angle, plus follow-up resources.
- `pre-outreach-readiness.md`: live domain, page, CTA, tracking, deliverability, legal, and approval checks that must pass before use.

## Recommended operating sequence

1. Complete and document every launch gate.
2. Select one segment and one observable, recent, public trigger.
3. Verify the prospect, role, company, URL, and trigger manually.
4. Choose the single best IMMOHRTAL page from the routing map.
5. Fill only verified placeholders in the matching message.
6. Read the message as plain text and remove any sentence that does not establish relevance or support the ask.
7. Stage the exact recipient, subject, body, link, evidence date, and opt-out language for Dillon's approval.
8. Send only after the exact staged message and recipient are approved through the active outreach workflow.

## Current decision boundary

The website can support outreach once its new public domain and all linked pages are proven live. This pack does not treat a successful build, deploy receipt, or local preview as proof that a recipient can reach the final page.
