Source: live SEMrush Keyword Magic Tool, United States database

## Operating rule

Use one primary query and a small set of close supporting queries per page. The 200-row CSV is a site architecture and editorial roadmap, not permission to repeat every phrase on every page. Natural topical coverage, useful answers, internal links, credible citations, and technical crawlability outrank keyword density.

## What gets baked into the website

- Service pages own commercial terms and explain the offer in buyer language before technical acronyms.
- Each editorial guide owns one clear informational cluster, a direct definition near the top, extractable steps or comparisons, and FAQs where the content supports them.
- Internal links connect every guide to its service hub, one adjacent guide, and the relevant conversion route using descriptive anchors.
- External citations point to academic research, standards bodies, or primary platform documentation. No affiliate roundups or circular agency-blog citations.
- Titles, descriptions, canonical URLs, headings, image alternatives, Article schema, breadcrumb schema, and organization or person entities stay aligned with the assigned query intent.
- Keyword variants are used only when they read naturally. Awkward measured variants remain research evidence and are not forced into public copy.
