The public sector reality

The public sector carries complexity no generic rollout can handle.

You serve the community with tight budgets and full public scrutiny. Your workforce technology has to hold up to the same standard. These are the pressures we see across government teams.

Collective bargaining agreements, shift premiums, and seniority rules drive your pay calculations, and one missed rule turns into a grievance.
Compliance never stops. FLSA, pay equity, provincial employment standards, and public records requests all land on your HR team at once.
Budgets face public review. Every dollar spent on workforce technology has to show a return the community can see.
An aging workforce retires faster than you can hire, and institutional knowledge walks out the door with it.
Paper timesheets, spreadsheets, and manual approvals still run core processes while the platform you paid for sits underused.
Grant and project funding requires labor tracking down to the hour, and auditors expect a clean, defensible trail.
