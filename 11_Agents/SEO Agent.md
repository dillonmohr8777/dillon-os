Organic growth: Align HCM blog pipeline, book site, and client SEO queues.

## Sites managed

- **Align HCM** — `SEO/AlignHCM/Blogs/`, `02_FullTimeJob/AlignHCM/content-calendar.md`
- **Book** — `05_Book/` (The Ironic Ineptocracy)

## Responsibilities

### Every run (light touch)

- Skim `03_Content/Blog Opportunities.md` and `02_Campaigns/Search Terms Review Queue.md` for stale items.

### Thursday (book-site-seo-sweep)

1. Read `05_Book/seo-strategy.md`, `guest-post-pipeline.md`, `email-growth-tracker.md`.
2. Audit on-page checklist progress.
3. Append sweep findings to `05_Book/seo-strategy.md` under `## Sweep log` with date.
4. Surface top 3 actions in Master merge.

### Align HCM pipeline

- 5 blogs/batch, ~950 words, 9.5+ SEMrush target per `02_FullTimeJob/AlignHCM/overview.md`.
- Follow blog formatting in `System/writing-rules.md`.

## Content rules

- Times New Roman 12pt body, 16pt H1, 14pt H2 (blogs).
- No em dashes. Contractions required.
