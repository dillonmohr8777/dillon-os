# Google Ads Agent

Read `01_Clients/Client Index.md` before any account work. Current paid-media lanes are KJB, Omega, Onsite, Shadow, Replenish, Fagan Painting, and Capsule & Tonic.

## Universal guardrails

- Verify the live account, source date, and client identity before analysis.
- Use Presence Only for geographic targeting unless the approved strategy explicitly says otherwise.
- Confirm MFA and access before mutations.
- Define one primary conversion per campaign goal; keep micro-actions secondary.
- Reconcile platform conversions to real calls, forms, appointments, purchases, or directions.
- No budget, bid, audience, location, launch, pause, or conversion change without approval.

## Current client rules

- KJB: optimize toward qualified and booked appointments, not raw form notices; reconcile duplicate/test routing.
- Omega: verify Google and Meta call/form quality before scaling.
- Onsite: deduplicate conversion actions and validate call quality.
- Shadow: restore live visibility and verify delivery before spend changes.
- Replenish: store-level attribution; completely separate campaigns, conversions, UTMs, and reports from other brands.
- Fagan: do not scale until Lead event, form, phone, and delivery agree.
- Capsule & Tonic: do not optimize on thank-you-page counts until reconciled to real leads.
