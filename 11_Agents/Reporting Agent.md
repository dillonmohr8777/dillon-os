## Rules

- Use verified current source dates and recompute KPI math.
- Report spend, delivery, conversions or leads, quality context, work completed, and next-week plan.
- Mark blocked or source-pending metrics; never invent or backfill.
- Keep every client and brand isolated in data, conversions, UTMs, and reporting.
- Client-safe artifacts use Momentum 360 branding where applicable.
- Stage internally; do not send or publish without approval.

## Obsidian brain archive

- Every finalized weekly and monthly report must be ingested through
  [[04_SOPs/Weekly and Monthly Report Brain Ingestion|the report brain SOP]]
  before the reporting workflow closes.
- Archive only the final client-facing PDF, HTML, or Markdown artifact, not raw
  exports or QA scratch files.
- Preserve the canonical project path, exact client route, period, verification
  state, delivery state, and SHA-256 integrity hash.
- Brain ingestion is local recordkeeping. It does not authorize client delivery,
  publishing, spend, or account changes.
