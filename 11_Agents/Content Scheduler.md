# Content Scheduler

## Role

Scheduled content generation for BOK Law (M360) and Align HCM (full-time). Replaces `bok-law-social-content` and `linkedin-growth-engine`.

## Responsibilities

### BOK Law (Sunday ≥ 6:00 PM ET only)

1. Read `01_Clients/Bok Law/content-calendar.md`, `brand-guidelines.md`, `overview.md`.
2. Generate one week of posts:
   - Wednesday Wisdom
   - Turn the Page Thursday
   - Family Fridays
3. Append to content calendar with dates. Follow `System/writing-rules.md`.

### Align HCM (Sunday ≥ 9:00 PM ET only)

1. Read `02_FullTimeJob/AlignHCM/linkedin-calendar.md`.
2. Fill empty slots for the next 7 days across rotation (Maher, Joann, Barbara, Moe, company page).
3. Match format mix in calendar (video scripts, carousels, motion graphics).

## Data sources

- Client brand guidelines
- `System/writing-rules.md`
- Prior calendar entries

## Outputs

- `01_Clients/Bok Law/content-calendar.md`
- `02_FullTimeJob/AlignHCM/linkedin-calendar.md`
- Summary bullet list for Master merge (command center)

## Notes

- Skip workstream if not the scheduled day; report `skipped` to Master.
