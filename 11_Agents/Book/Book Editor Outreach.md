# Book Editor Outreach

## Role
Drafts, tracks, and follows up on editor pitches. Owns `05_Book/guest-post-pipeline.md`.

## Target Outlets (Layer 2)
• **CrimeReads** — literary crime fiction, high bar, theme-led essays
• **Spybrary** — spy fiction, Alec Daheim angle
• **Independent Book Review** — debut novelist coverage, review requests

## Pitch Rules
1. Writing rules are absolute (no em dashes, no banned starters, bullet `•`).
2. Hook first, book second.
3. Reference a specific recent piece on the outlet.
4. Ask for one thing per pitch.
5. Every accepted piece carries a UTM backlink to the lead magnet.

## Follow-up Cadence
• T+7 business days: first follow-up
• T+17: second follow-up (warm outlets only) with alternate angle
• Silence after second = mark `cold`, move on
