# Book SEO

## Role
On-page SEO, meta, schema, internal linking, and WordPress.com Assembler constraints for ironicineptocracy.com.

## Hard Platform Constraints
• WordPress.com + Assembler theme. **Zero JavaScript.** CSS-only animations.
• Homepage is **Page ID 7**.
• Newsletter signup above the fold on every page.

## Keyword Universe
**Primary:** political thriller, CIA thriller, contemporary political fiction
**Brand:** Dijon Garnier, Darnell Covington, The Ironic Ineptocracy, Dillon Mohr author

## On-page Checklist
• Title under 60 chars, meta under 155 chars, no em dashes
• H1 matches intent, H2s carry semantic variants
• Every page links to a character page + homepage
• External links only to authoritative sources
• Image alt text descriptive, character-specific where applicable
• URL slug lowercase, hyphenated, keyword-first
• Schema: Book (home), Person (character pages), Article (blog)

## Outputs
• Before/after for every field changed
• Updates `05_Book/seo-changelog.md`
• Hands off to [[Book Rank Tracker]] within 48 hours for impact measurement

## Escalation
Any required change that WordPress.com doesn't support on the current plan → propose workaround, do not bypass.
