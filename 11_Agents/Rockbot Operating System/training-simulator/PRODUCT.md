# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Inferred from Dillon's instruction to execute immediately: static HTML, CSS,
and JavaScript with no external dependencies or deployment target. The
simulator remains a local teaching artifact.

## Users

- Dillon Mohr, demonstrating how his marketing operating system works.
- Grok Bot, watching a sanitized walkthrough and turning it into a repeatable
  routine.
- Codex acting as Marketing Chief, verifying that the learned routine follows
  the contract and does not exceed its authority.

## Product Purpose

Provide a safe, realistic workstation in which every recurring Dillon workflow
can be demonstrated end to end without exposing private communications,
credentials, payment data, client secrets, or live provider state. Success is a
saved Grok Bot routine that can replay a second synthetic fixture and produce
the expected artifact, evidence receipt, approval state, and next safe action.

## Positioning

Unlike a generic task demo or dashboard, this simulator makes the operating
chain itself executable: sense, route, prioritize, build, verify, approve,
deliver, read back, and learn. Each stage exposes its evidence and authority
boundary so Grok learns both how to act and when to stop.

## Operating Context

The simulator represents synthetic versions of Dillon's Gmail and Slack
intake, meeting notes, client registry, canonical queue, repository preflight,
website and content production, paid-media evidence, reporting, approval desk,
delivery readback, and Dillon OS knowledge capture. A trainer chooses one
routine, moves through its stages, and ends with a machine-readable receipt.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Load the 54-routine recording manifest and present one bounded walkthrough at
  a time.
- Support keyboard and mouse operation, obvious current state, and resettable
  synthetic fixtures.
- Label every person, company, metric, message, provider, and delivery event as
  synthetic.
- Simulate Gmail, Slack, ad platforms, CRM, deployment, and approval readback;
  never connect to live providers.
- Never display or request passwords, MFA, passkeys, recovery codes, card data,
  API keys, tokens, cookies, or raw private communications.
- Never imply that a simulated send, publish, spend, deployment, queue write, or
  account change happened externally.
- Grok Bots may produce local artifacts and proposals. Codex acting as
  Marketing Chief remains the final verifier and only canonical queue writer.

## Brand Commitments

The interface speaks in Dillon's operating language: direct, evidence-backed,
decisive, warm, and free of generic assistant phrasing. Status words remain
precise: complete, partial, blocked, drafted, staged, deployed, sent, and
verified are never interchangeable.

## Product Principles

1. Demonstrate the work, the proof, and the boundary together.
2. One routine, one trigger, one finish line, one replayable receipt.
3. Synthetic detail should feel operationally real without resembling a real
   person's private data.
4. Local completion and external delivery are visibly different states.
5. A routine is learned only after an independent replay passes.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard operation, visible focus, semantic controls, high contrast, reduced
motion support, and a layout that remains usable at desktop and narrow mobile
widths are required.
