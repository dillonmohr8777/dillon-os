## Gate 3: 120-image Grok generation matrix

Before generating, create `IMAGE-MATRIX.json` containing exactly six unique
prompt records for each of the 20 locked businesses. Every master is a vertical
4:5 editorial illustration designed for responsive cropping. The six roles are:

1. `hero-mission`: wide sense of the industry's people, setting, and purpose.
2. `operational-system`: process, records, schedules, tools, roles, or connected
   work represented as a coherent system.
3. `material-detail`: a tactile macro study of the industry's characteristic
   materials, instruments, craft, or environment.
4. `human-service`: a composed, non-identifiable service interaction relevant to
   the industry, never presented as the real business's staff or customers.
5. `process-panorama`: an environmental/process scene with clear foreground,
   midground, and background layers for restrained 2.5D motion.
6. `outcome-field`: confidence, readiness, relief, completion, or coordinated
   service expressed without an unverified performance claim.

Shared art language:

- premium editorial campaign illustration;
- visibly fine etched, engraved, halftone, grainy, or screen-printed texture;
- layered ribbons, contour fields, and connected operational motifs where they
  make sense;
- clear negative space and deliberate crop-safe composition;
- each business's verified palette translated into deep field, paper, ink, and
  restrained signal roles;
- text-free, logo-free, watermark-free, and fake-interface-free;
- industry specific without falling into a generic category stock scene.

Negative constraints for every prompt:

- no real employee, customer, owner, property, vehicle, office, storefront, or
  recognizable private person from the target business;
- no copied first-party photography, no stock-photo staging, no generated words,
  no signage text, no logo, no watermark, no gibberish, no fake UI, no synthetic
  testimonial, no unsupported before/after result, no duplicated subject, no
  extra limbs, no melted geometry, no cross-client brand element;
- avoid spectacle, weapon-forward scenes, patriotic cliches, gratuitous sci-fi,
  and category-template compositions.

Attach the exact verified logo as a locked identity reference and approved Align
art examples as style/grain references where the generator supports references,
but instruct the model never to render or alter the logo inside the illustration.
Place the exact logo in site code as a separate untouched asset.

For every generated master, record generator/provider identity, model, prompt,
negative prompt, input-reference hashes, generation timestamp, native dimensions,
output SHA-256, business, role, and review status in
`IMAGE-GENERATION-RECEIPT.json`. Generate all masters with GrokBot. Responsive
WebP/AVIF derivatives do not increase the master count.

Reject and regenerate any image that is generic, factually misleading, visibly
photographic stock, text-bearing, logo-bearing, malformed, off-industry, too
similar to another output, or insufficiently grainy. All 120 master hashes must
be unique and must not match any previously stored/generated/stock/first-party
