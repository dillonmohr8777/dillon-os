# Research verify

For anything that can drift (model IDs, prices, APIs, news):

1. `web_search`
2. `web_fetch` the primary vendor page
3. Quote the dated fact and the URL
4. If search fails, say so. Do not bluff a benchmark.

Vault research notes expire. Prefer a live fetch over a stale `12_Brain/06_Research/` page.
