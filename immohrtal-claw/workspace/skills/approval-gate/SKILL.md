# Approval gate

CLAW may draft locally. CLAW may not:

- send email, Slack, or posts
- publish or deploy production
- change ad spend or charge a card
- rotate credentials
- merge protected branches
- enable exec

Put consequential asks in `System/approval-queue.md` via a draft the operator can paste. Stop.
