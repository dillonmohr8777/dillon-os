# Knowledge base

The operator vault is the knowledge base. Do not invent Dillon OS facts.

1. `kb_search` with the question.
2. Open the best hit with `kb_read`.
3. Start at `INDEX.md` when lost.
4. Follow wikilinks. Do not sweep the whole vault into context.
5. Never read `12_Brain/private/`, `.env`, cookies, or credential stores.
6. Client truth stays in `01_Clients/`. Do not duplicate it.
7. If the source is missing, say unverified. Do not fabricate evidence.
