# Pro Fence & Deck Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Pro Fence & Deck needs a verified business-identity and conversion foundation
before a deeper growth system can be trusted. SEO research and licensed stock
concepts may continue locally, but production claims, CTAs, AEO, reporting, and
publishing remain blocked by missing exact business facts and real project proof.

## Current evidence snapshot

- The canonical record identifies an active SEO and value-add video planning
  lane.
- Current visual materials are licensed generic category B-roll for fences,
  gates, decks, planning, craft details, and outdoor living.
- They are not evidence of Pro Fence & Deck projects, crews, customers, or
  outcomes.
- Production logo, website, CTA, contact route, service area, offer, warranties,
  pricing, real media, analytics, and pipeline are not verified.

## Truth and creative boundaries

Never present stock or generated material as client work. Do not add a logo,
phone number, website, service area, offer, price, warranty, financing, award,
performance claim, customer statement, or public CTA until verified. Keep all
concepts local and reviewable until the exact delivery route and preview are
approved.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Confirm canonical website, business name, logo provenance, contact route,
   service area, offer, services, and owner.
2. Obtain approved real project imagery and claim permissions.
3. Capture live site, GBP, Search Console, analytics, review, and lead-path
   baselines after exact access is mapped.
4. Mine real customer questions and won/lost reasons.
5. Produce the first evidence-backed keyword, page, AEO, video, and measurement
   backlog.
