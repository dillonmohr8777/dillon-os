# Brand Guidelines

## Naming
- Use **Replenish** branding in ads, not Fresh Blends.

## Ad copy rules (from Mia)
- Delete all calorie references.
- Delete "only 3 ingredients."
- Keep "self-serve in about 60 seconds."
- Delete "IQF" and "Flash Frozen Fruit."
- Delete "no staff, no mess."
- Add "smoothies" where appropriate.
- No phone-call conversions, no call extensions, no forwarding numbers.

## KPIs
- **Primary:** increase in daily drink sales.
- **Secondary:** increase in branded searches for "7-Eleven smoothies" in South Florida (Google Trends).

## Location insight
The 56th Street location sits near a school with a strong Gen Z / Gen Alpha customer base. That context should inform creative and targeting for that store.
