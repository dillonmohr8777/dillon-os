# Fresh Blends / Replenish — Brand Research

## Business Snapshot
Automated, self-cleaning blended-beverage technology out of **Delray Beach, FL** (Fresh Blends North America, Inc., 955 NW 17th Ave, Delray Beach FL 33445 — 561 area code matches Mia's number). Core product is a robotic countertop blender that turns flash-frozen, chef-curated ingredient cups into a fresh smoothie/shake in ~60 seconds with no prep, staff, or cleanup, using patented in-cup blending (Hydroblend) and automated self-clean/sanitize (Hydroclean). Category: **fresh smoothies / blended beverages via automated blending hardware + frozen ingredient cups** (not bottled CPG, not powdered supplements).

**Fresh Blends vs Replenish — the relationship (best read, high confidence):** Two brand faces of the *same* company/technology platform, split by go-to-market.
- **Fresh Blends** = the **commercial / B2B** brand: the "Fresh Blender" self-serve kiosk (built in partnership with Welbilt/Multiplex), deployed in convenience retail (7-Eleven), foodservice, gyms, military, travel, etc. 4,000+ units, 60M+ drinks sold. This is the foodservice/operator-facing side.
- **Replenish** (getreplenish.com) = the **consumer / home (DTC)** brand: the "**Genesis**" countertop self-cleaning blender + subscription "blend box" of frozen ingredient cups delivered to your door. Pitched historically as "the Keurig for smoothies." Evolved from earlier startup **LivBlends** (YC, founded ~2013). Replenish's own site labels its operator program "**Powered by Replenish**."
- **Confirmation of shared entity:** contact Mia Lange is Managing Partner and uses *both* mia@getreplenish.com and mia@freshblends.com (per internal contact-info.md); both brands are Delray Beach; shared tech (Hydroblend/Hydroclean), shared awards narrative (2026 NRA Kitchen Innovations Award; 2025 TIME America's Top GreenTech). The exact corporate/holding-entity structure (single Inc. vs. parent + sub) is not nailed in public records *(unverified)*, but operationally they are one business with two brand names.

**IMPORTANT scope correction:** Dillon's *live* B2C work is **local foot-traffic to 5 South Florida 7-Eleven-branded smoothie kiosks**, advertised under the **Replenish** name (per Mia's rule). It is local "smoothie near me" intent, **not** national DTC ecommerce of a home blender. The getreplenish home/DTC subscription product is a separate GTM and is **currently out of scope** at this budget. Treat "B2C" = local in-store drink sales, not ecommerce checkout.

## Products & Pricing
- **Fresh Blends / Fresh Blender (commercial):** self-serve kiosk; menu of smoothies, shakes, frappes, plus a functional line (protein shakes, protein coffees, energy smoothies, caffeinated refreshers). All-natural, non-GMO, no HFCS, no artificial colors/sweeteners; FDA-registered, GFSI-certified, OU Kosher, USDA Organic-compliant facility. Operator economics cited: ~85% labor savings, ~20% margin lift. Drink retail price set by the host retailer (the 7-Eleven kiosks) *(specific per-drink price unverified)*.
- **Replenish Genesis (home/DTC):** Genesis countertop blender + companion freezer + subscription "blend box" of recyclable frozen ingredient cups (≤6-month frozen shelf life). Flow: choose plan → build blend box → frozen delivery → drop cup in, blend, done.
- **Pricing:** No public price points captured — both .com sites returned 403 to automated fetch and pricing is gated behind site/checkout. **Pull actual drink prices and any DTC subscription tiers from the sites or Slack/Gmail.** *(prices unverified)*

## Target Audience / ICP
- **Live (kiosk B2C):** South Florida convenience-store shoppers seeking a fast, healthy on-the-go drink; "smoothie near me" / gas-station-snack intent. One location (56th Street) sits near a school with a strong **Gen Z / Gen Alpha** skew — treat as its own micro-persona for creative.
- **Replicate-able home DTC (out of scope now):** health-conscious, time-pressed families/professionals wanting fresh smoothies at home without prep/cleanup.
- **B2B operator side (deferred):** convenience retail, foodservice, fitness, military, healthcare, education, travel/entertainment buyers — explicitly out of scope at current budget.

Convenience + clean-label health + tech novelty. Positioning pillars: "fresh, real, blended in ~60 seconds," "no staff, no mess," all-natural / no junk ingredients. **Per Mia's ad rules these must be adapted** — see constraints below.

## SEO / Paid Media Notes (B2C local vs ecommerce)
- This is **local-intent B2C**, not ecommerce. No Shopping feed / no cart, so **Shopping and conversion-value bidding don't apply** the way they would for DTC. The DTC getreplenish store *would* warrant Shopping/PMax-with-feed if it ever comes into scope.
- Current mix: **Search** ("Smoothie Near Me" local intent), a **Gas Station / Kiosk concept** Search campaign, and **PMax for foot traffic** at $200/location/mo (~$6.50/day/store). B2B partner-lead campaigns are intentionally excluded.
- KPIs: **primary = daily drink-sales lift; secondary = branded search lift for "7-Eleven smoothies" in South Florida (Google Trends).** Note conversions can't be tracked at the register directly — leans on store-visit / proxy signals + sales data from client.
- Hard rules: **no phone-call conversions, no call extensions, no forwarding numbers** (don't route callers to the 7-Eleven stores).
