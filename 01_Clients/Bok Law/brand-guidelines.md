# Brand Guidelines

## Tone
Empathetic, professional, community-oriented. Never aggressive or adversarial. Never salesy.

## Hard rules
- No legal advice in posts. Always general education, never case-specific guidance.
- No language that could read as a solicitation or retainer pitch.
- Posts should feel like a thoughtful colleague sharing perspective, not a firm marketing itself.

## Tone by series
- **Wednesday Wisdom:** empathetic, educational. Lead with a human situation, not a statute.
- **Turn the Page Thursday:** warm, hopeful, motivational. Life-transitions framing.
- **Family Fridays:** lighthearted, relatable, Pittsburgh-community-flavored.
