## Target Audience / ICP
- **Divorce / custody clients:** Pittsburgh-area adults (largely 30s–55s) navigating separation, custody, or support — often emotionally distressed, comparison-shopping, value-conscious; mediation appeals to those wanting a lower-conflict, lower-cost path.
- **Mediation clients:** Couples seeking an amicable, out-of-court resolution; cost-sensitive (flat-fee messaging resonates).
- **PFA / safety clients:** Individuals needing urgent protection — sensitive, high-trust tone required.
- **Estate planning / probate clients:** Families and older adults planning ahead, or executors handling a loved one's estate.
- **Guardianship / adoption clients:** Families expanding or caring for vulnerable relatives.
- Geography: North Hills of Pittsburgh and surrounding Allegheny/Butler county communities (local intent matters heavily).

## Brand Voice & Positioning
- **Positioning:** Compassionate, family-centered firm that "helps families navigate periods of transition" — emphasizes guidance over combat, with mediation as a differentiator vs. adversarial competitors.
- **Tone for legal:** Warm, reassuring, plain-language, educational. Avoid hype, guarantees, and outcome promises (PA RPC 7.1). Empathy-forward, professional, trustworthy.
- **Differentiators to lean on:** dual offering (litigation AND mediation), flat-fee mediation pricing, female-founded/female-led team, the Donuts & Divorce podcast, free consults, flexible (Zoom/phone) access.

## Content / SEO Notes
- **Compliance/ethics (PA Rules of Professional Conduct):**
  - No promises or implied guarantees of results (RPC 7.1 — false/misleading communications).
  - Avoid language that creates an attorney-client relationship or gives specific legal advice in posts; use "general information, not legal advice" framing.
  - Be careful with testimonials/client stories — get consent; never disclose case details.
  - Avoid "specialist/expert" claims unless certified (RPC 7.4 considerations).
- **Local SEO intent:** Optimize for "divorce mediation North Hills," "Pittsburgh family law attorney," "child custody lawyer Pittsburgh," "flat fee divorce mediation." Keep NAP (name/address/phone) consistent across directories; encourage Google reviews.
- **Repurpose the podcast:** Each Donuts & Divorce episode = multiple social posts (audiograms, quote cards, FAQ clips).

## What Dillon Does For Them — Weekly Social Cadence
Recurring themes observed: **Wednesday Wisdom**, **Family Fridays**, **Saturday Solutions**. Suggested content pillars per theme:

### Wednesday Wisdom (educational / authority)
- Plain-language legal explainers: "What is APL vs. alimony?", "How custody schedules work in PA."
- Myth-busting: common divorce/mediation misconceptions.
- Estate-planning tips (wills, POA, guardianship basics).
- Quote/insight cards pulled from the Donuts & Divorce podcast.
- Always tag with disclaimer ("general info, not legal advice").

### Family Fridays (warm / human / brand)
- Compassion-forward content: co-parenting tips, supporting kids through transition.
- Team spotlights (attorneys, firm culture, female-led story).
- Community involvement / local Pittsburgh ties.
- Encouraging, reassuring messaging ("you don't have to do this alone").

### Saturday Solutions (problem → solution / conversion-leaning)
- Scenario-based posts: "Thinking about divorce but want to avoid court? Mediation may be for you."
- Flat-fee mediation value messaging.
- FAQ answers; consultation CTAs (free consult, Zoom availability).
- PFA / safety resources where appropriate (sensitive tone).
