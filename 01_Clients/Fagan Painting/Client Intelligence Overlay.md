# Fagan Painting Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Fagan's Dillon-owned lane has transitioned from Meta management to coordinated
AEO, GEO, and SEO. The growth system should make real Pittsburgh-area services,
projects, locations, homeowner questions, and estimate paths easy to discover
and cite while preserving clean lead ownership and attribution.

## Current evidence snapshot

- The July 27 strategy transition assigns Facebook to Benson/Legacy Paint
  Holdings and keeps AEO/GEO/SEO with Dillon in coordination with Phil and Mac.
- Existing evidence covers service/location pages, FAQs, internal links,
  structured content, website forms, phone intent, and historical paid media.
- Historical qualified website inquiries could not be conclusively assigned to
  Meta; a later window contained two unique website leads and one likely
  duplicate, but receipt, disposition, and downstream outcomes were unknown.
- The latest AEO/GEO package defines a 90-day answer and local-authority sprint;
  it is not evidence of achieved rankings, citations, or estimate results.

## Demand and answer map

Prioritize verified service and location combinations, preparation, process,
timing factors, project fit, estimate expectations, comparisons, maintenance,
commercial/HOA needs, and local conditions. Do not invent pricing, radius,
warranty, availability, project minimums, certifications, or results.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Export current Search Console queries/pages and technical crawl results.
2. Verify GBP/site entity parity and current review/project-proof inventory.
3. Run the fixed AI citation query set and record cited competitors and sources.
4. Complete one real form and call-path reconciliation through disposition.
5. Choose the first service pages and FAQs by qualified-demand potential and
   evidence strength, then coordinate implementation ownership.
