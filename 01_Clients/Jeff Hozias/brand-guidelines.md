# Brand Guidelines

## Palette
Navy / red / gold (Howard Hanna Rand Realty).

## Tone
Approachable expert. Community-focused. Educational, not salesy. Long-form GBP posts read more like blog entries than promo bursts.

## Content pillars
- Rockland County market updates.
- Buyer tips and seller tips (specific, tactical).
- Community spotlights (neighborhoods, schools, local events).
- Seasonal real estate advice.

## Ad narrative
Anti-Zillow, anti-Zestimate positioning. Real local expertise over algorithmic guessing. "Not a Zestimate. Not a guess." is the approved north-star hook for seller-side copy.
