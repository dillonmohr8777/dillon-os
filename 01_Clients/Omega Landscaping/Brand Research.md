## Target Audience / ICP
- **Primary:** Colorado Springs-area homeowners (suburban, mid-to-upper income) wanting outdoor-living upgrades — patios, driveways, retaining walls, full backyard transformations.
- **Secondary:** Contractors and developers (the site references serving "homeowners, contractors, and developers").
- **Geographic ICP:** North Colorado Springs, Monument, Black Forest, Falcon, Peyton — higher-lot, newer-build areas where drainage/grading and large hardscape projects are common.
- **Buyer triggers:** drainage/water problems, cracked or failing old concrete, new-build raw yards, freeze-thaw damage, desire for low-water turf/rock landscaping.
