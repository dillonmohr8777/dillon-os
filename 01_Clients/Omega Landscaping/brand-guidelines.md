# Brand Guidelines

## Tone
Professional, visual-forward, seasonal awareness, community-oriented. Lean on real project photos and credibility markers.

## Preferred proof framing
"Helped over 3000 homeowners elevate their outdoor living" over "15 years of experience." Volume and customer impact over tenure.

## Content pillars
- Project showcases (real jobs, before/after).
- Seasonal landscaping tips.
- Concrete and hardscape capabilities.
- Colorado Springs neighborhood references.
