# Omega Landscaping and Concrete Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Omega should join high-intent Colorado Springs landscaping and concrete demand
to precise service/location pages, GBP proof, calls/forms, named lead records,
project qualification, estimates, and won work. Platform conversion counts are
intermediate signals until a real opportunity is identified.

## Current evidence snapshot

- Google Ads evidence through July 26 showed one active Performance Max
  campaign, $271.93 spend, 1,492 impressions, 45 clicks, 3.02% CTR, and one
  Google-counted event awaiting named-lead validation.
- Earlier evidence recorded search-term exclusions, lead-quality review,
  GoHighLevel/Meta integration needs, and planned high-intent Search.
- A local GBP content batch exists for retaining walls, patios, drainage,
  landscape details, and Colorado landscape design.
- Current project quality, CRM match, revenue, and live account state after the
  evidence cutoff are not established.

## Demand and answer map

Build from verified services, service areas, project types, materials, process,
timing factors, drainage/terrain needs, permitting responsibilities, estimates,
proof, and exclusions. Do not invent pricing, licenses, warranties, availability,
service radius, financing, or project outcomes.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Identify the July 20–26 counted event across call, form, inbox, CRM, and GHL.
2. Record project type, service area, qualification, owner, response, estimate,
   and outcome in the authorized system.
3. Export search terms and classify bad intent and high-value service language.
4. Audit GBP/site/entity/service-area parity and current project proof.
5. Decide PMax, Search, landing-page, and content priorities from qualified
   project evidence, not platform count alone.
