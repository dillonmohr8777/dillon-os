# Brand Guidelines

## Positioning
Membership support org for independent commercial cleaning business owners. NOT a franchise. Emphasize independence, growth support, and the breadth of services (contracts, insurance, invoicing, fast funding, payroll, compliance, marketing).

## Tone
Practical, business-owner-to-business-owner. Lean into the "we handle the hard stuff so you can run your business" angle. Fast-funding (3–5 days) is a concrete proof point worth featuring.

## Primary offer
Free assessment as the lead-gen entry point.
