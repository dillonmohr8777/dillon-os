# Bar Crawl USA — Brand Research

Business verified. Name, website (barcrawlusa.com), owners (Andy & Caroline Zirger), and Chattanooga base/multi-city footprint all confirmed across the company site, Eventbrite, LinkedIn, Instagram, and a founder interview. Owner contact info matches the brief. This is the right business.

## Business Snapshot
- Events/entertainment company that produces themed, walkable **organized bar crawls** (food + drink + DJ block-party format) across the US. [1][2]
- Founder Andy Zirger started in Atlanta (~2014–2015 "Ugly Sweater Bar Crawl" in Buckhead) as a side hustle, then expanded the brand to Bar Crawl USA. Caroline Zirger co-owns and runs ops/events. [3][7]
- Self-reported scale: "400+ events in 20+ cities" since 2015. Different pages cite 2014, 2015, 2016, and 2017 start dates — the brand's "Since 2015" tagline is the canonical one. (Exact event/city counts unverified beyond their own marketing.) [1][6]
- Chattanooga presence since 2019, centered on the North Shore area along the Tennessee River. [2]
- Has a branded iOS app ("Bar Crawl USA," App Store). (App currency/maintenance unverified.) [App Store]
- Internal note: engaged via Momentum 360 at $950/mo, start 2026-02-16; Dillon is Account Manager. [internal: overview.md]

## Services & Pricing (public)
Recurring themed crawl series, roughly quarterly: [8]
- **Taco & Tequila Crawl** (spring flagship) — free/included signature tacos via taco tokens, discounted margaritas, DJ, walkable 5+ bars.
- **St. Patrick's Shamrock Crawl** (March).
- **Boozy Brunch Crawl** (mimosas/bloody marys).
- **Boos & Booze Halloween Crawl** (fall).
- **Ugly Sweater Bar Crawl** (December — the original event).

Public ticket pricing (Eventbrite, Taco & Tequila): **GA from ~$25**, **VIP ~$40**. GA = lanyard with 4–5 taco tokens, event map, access to 5+ participating bars. VIP = welcome shot, event t-shirt, extra taco token, 1-hour early start. (Prices vary by city/venue and rise as events fill.) [4][5]

## Target Audience / ICP
- Primary: adults **21–35**, social/group-oriented, in mid-to-large US metros; people who buy "experience" outings (festivals, themed nights). [inferred from event format/pricing]
- Group buyers: friend groups, birthdays/bachelorette parties, work/social outings (group ticketing fits the lanyard model).
- Secondary stakeholders: participating bars/restaurants (B2B partners who get foot traffic) and local sponsors.

## Brand Voice & Positioning
- Voice: **local, high-energy, fun, community/festival vibe — never corporate.** Leans on city-specific references and landmarks. [internal: brand-guidelines.md]
- Visual: red/white/blue gradient, energetic, food-forward photography. [internal: brand-guidelines.md]
- Positioning vs. competitors: "festival feel meets bar crawl," **all venues within walking distance**, fast-service food stations (no long lines), and a claim of being among the few crawl companies with **5-star Google & Facebook reviews**. (Star claim is their own marketing — see SEO notes; treat as unverified.) [1][6]

## SEO / Market Notes
- **Cities (mix of confirmed + internal pipeline):** Chattanooga TN (base), Atlanta metro (Midtown, Buckhead, Beltline, Roswell, Alpharetta, Duluth, Lawrenceville, Macon GA), St. Pete & Sarasota FL, Cincinnati OH, plus internal PMax build list: Greenville SC, Portland ME, Indianapolis IN, Soulard/St. Louis MO, Columbia SC. [1][8][internal: active-campaigns.md] Brief's "~20 cities" matches the brand's "20+ cities" claim.
- **Seasonality is the whole game** — demand spikes around fixed event windows: St. Patrick's (March), Cinco de Mayo / Taco & Tequila (April–June), Halloween (Oct), Ugly Sweater (Dec). Ad spend, landing pages, and SEO content should ramp 4–8 weeks ahead of each window per city.
- High-value local intent queries: "[city] bar crawl," "[city] taco crawl," "[city] st patricks bar crawl / pub crawl," "ugly sweater bar crawl [city]," "bachelorette bar crawl [city]."
- **Compliance constraint that shapes SEO/ads:** ZERO alcohol-related language allowed in Google Ads copy (banned-terms list), location targeting must be **Presence Only**, copy only from the pre-approved library. [internal: active-campaigns.md / brand-guidelines.md]

## What Dillon Does For Them (scope + priorities)
- **Google Ads:** full-funnel management — Search RSA + **Performance Max per city**, $14.25/day default budgets, Maximize Conversions (with tCPA guardrail after a Soulard overspend). Resolve disapprovals; enforce Presence-Only + alcohol-compliance rules. [internal]
- **20-city landing pages:** Taco & Tequila landing-page package (delivered as .docx) for the per-city event series. Priority: keep these template-consistent, fast, and conversion-focused (ticket CTA above the fold).
- **WordPress publisher tool:** React + WP REST API tool that clones the Elementor template (Page ID 15281) and auto-publishes city pages at scale. Priority: reliability and on-page SEO defaults baked into the template.
- **On-page / local SEO:** city-page optimization, schema (Event), local landmark/venue references, internal linking, and seasonal content cadence.
- **Suggested priority order:** (1) keep paid compliant + pacing clean through each event wave, (2) harden the publisher so every new city page ships SEO-ready, (3) layer local SEO + Event schema so organic captures the seasonal spikes paid can't fully cover.

## Opportunities (concrete plays)
1. **Event schema (JSON-LD) on every city/event page** — date, location, offers/price, organizer. Drives rich results and Google "events" eligibility right when seasonal intent peaks.
2. **Per-city Google Business Profiles or a verified primary GBP + event posts** — capture "near me" + map-pack intent; currently appears thin/unverified.
3. **Reviews engine** — automate post-event review requests (the brand already leans on its 5-star claim) to build verifiable Google/Facebook ratings that strengthen both ads (seller ratings) and local SEO.
4. **Seasonal content calendar tied to the 4–5 fixed event windows** — publish/refresh city pages 4–8 weeks ahead of each theme so organic ramps with paid.
5. **Compliance-safe ad asset library expansion** — pre-cleared food-forward / "fiesta" / "festival" angles per theme so disapprovals (Halloween/Fall Cocktail issue) stop recurring.
6. **Eventbrite ↔ landing-page UTM + conversion tracking audit** — ensure every PMax/Search click attributes to ticket purchases cleanly across 20 cities (ties directly to the tCPA/pacing work).
