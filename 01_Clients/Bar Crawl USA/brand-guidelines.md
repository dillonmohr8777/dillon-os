# Brand Guidelines

## Palette
- Red, white, blue gradient. Fun, energetic, event-driven.

## Tone
- Local, high-energy, community event vibe. Never corporate. Lean on city-specific references.

## Hard compliance rules (repeat from active-campaigns.md because of blast radius)
- ZERO alcohol-related language anywhere in Google Ads copy, sitelinks, extensions, headlines, or descriptions. Pre-approved copy library is the only source. No improvisation.
- Location targeting is always Presence Only, never Presence or Interest.

## Banned terms
beer, wine, liquor, cocktail, drink, booze, shots, happy hour, bar specials, get drunk, party hard, alcohol, spirits, brew, pour, cheers, toast, nightlife, drinking, drunk, intoxicated, wasted, hammered, buzzed, tipsy, and any synonym or near-synonym.

## Approved concepts
City-specific taco and tequila event language (without the word "tequila" in ad copy where policy pushes back), VIP packages, FAQs, local landmark references, food-forward photography.
