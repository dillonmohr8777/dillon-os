# NKCDC — Brand Research

> Identity VERIFIED. NKCDC is a real, established Philadelphia nonprofit community development corporation (501(c)(3), EIN 22-2610536, IRS ruling year 1985, ~40 years operating). Corroborated across the org's own domain (nkcdc.org), GuideStar, Cause IQ, ProPublica Nonprofit Explorer, Charity Navigator, PACDC member list, and local press (Kensington Voice). Address: 2771 Ruth St, Ste 1, Philadelphia, PA 19134. Contacts on file (Anthony Miller, Tess Watts, Mikey Boyd, all @nkcdc.org) are consistent with a staffed org of this size. Note: nkcdc.org returns HTTP 403 to automated fetches, so on-page program copy below is drawn from search snippets of nkcdc.org pages plus third-party sources, not direct page scrapes.

## Business Snapshot
- **Mission (verified):** "NKCDC advances social equity & economic empowerment by nurturing and creating opportunities for residents to live in, and actively shape, their neighborhoods of choice." Self-described as community-driven, trauma-informed, and equity-focused.
- **Neighborhoods served (verified):** Kensington, Fishtown, and Port Richmond (core service area). Housing counseling extends city-wide and across Berks, Bucks, Chester, Delaware, Montgomery, and Philadelphia counties. Nourish food program also reaches Fairhill and Juniata.
- **Org type:** 501(c)(3) community development corporation (CDC). EIN 22-2610536. PACDC member.
- **Scale:** Revenue ~$3.9M, assets ~$10.0M (most recent reported via Cause IQ/GuideStar — exact fiscal year unverified).
- **Leadership:** Executive Director **Dr. William McKinney** (verified via GuideStar/Cause IQ).
- **History note:** First org in the region to use vacant-land management as a revitalization strategy (1996, w/ Pennsylvania Horticultural Society); received a HUD Best Practices Award (verified via nkcdc.org history snippet).

## Programs / Services (what they'd advertise)
Verified program set (from nkcdc.org program pages + findhelp + press):

Homebuying help, homebuyer workshops, mortgage/rent/utility assistance, eviction & foreclosure prevention, free home repair applications, financial literacy, weatherization/energy assistance. Owns/rents 80+ affordable 1–2BR apartments in Kensington (unverified exact unit count — single source).
- **Free Business Tax Prep** — current paid-media offer in market (per internal notes; landing page at businesstaxprep.fshtechnologies.org). Not prominently surfaced on public program pages in search — likely a seasonal/grant-funded initiative (treat program details as unverified beyond internal thread).
- **Economic / Business Development** — small business + artist support, commercial corridor support, start-up/existing business assistance, property inventory.
- **Taking Care of Business (TCB)** — City-funded commercial corridor cleaning/stewardship; maintains 48+ blocks, 5 days/week, year-round.
- **Vacant Land Resources** — cleaning, greening, and management of vacant lots; resident guidance on dealing with vacant lots.
- **Community Engagement Center** — Kensington Avenue hub for exhibitions, community meetings, workshops, satellite services (health, housing counseling, legal aid, utility support).
- **Nourish** — food & nutrition program; meal-kit delivery + nutrition education (Kensington, Fairhill, Juniata).
- **Cure Violence Kensington** — community violence intervention; public-health approach to gun violence using outreach workers with lived experience.
- **Community gardens / Urban Agriculture** — transforming open lots into community spaces.

## Target Audience / ICP (multi-audience — typical of a nonprofit)
A CDC serves several distinct audiences; campaigns must segment, not blend:
1. **Program participants — residents (primary demand-gen):**
   - *Homeowners/renters* needing housing counseling, foreclosure/eviction help, home repairs, utility assistance — low-to-moderate income, Kensington/Fishtown/Port Richmond + wider PA counties. High-intent, often crisis-driven (eviction, foreclosure, behind on bills).
   - *Aspiring first-time homebuyers* — workshops, financial literacy.
   - *Small business owners / entrepreneurs / artists* — corridor businesses needing tax prep, growth support.
   - *Families* — food/nutrition (Nourish), community gardens.
2. **Donors / funders** — individual donors, foundations, City of Philadelphia, corporate (Benevity-listed). Want impact narratives, transparency, community trust.
3. **Volunteers & community members** — event attendees, garden/cleanup participants, engagement-center visitors.
4. **Talent / job seekers** — active job board (housing counselor, corridor manager, Cure Violence roles); recruiting is a real audience.

ICP for *paid media* depends on which program is funding the buy. The current build is narrowly scoped to small-business owners eligible for **Free Business Tax Prep** (per Anthony's 2026-04-09 direction to drop multi-service framing).

## Brand Voice & Positioning
- **Voice (verified tone + internal brand notes):** Warm, welcoming, access-oriented, community-trust-forward. Trauma-informed and equity-focused language. Resident-empowerment framing ("neighborhoods of choice," "actively shape"). Never commercial or salesy.
- **Positioning:** A trusted, long-tenured (~40 yr) neighborhood institution, BY and FOR Kensington/Fishtown/Port Richmond — not a top-down service provider. "Community development should benefit residents."
- **Ad implication:** Lead with "free," "no catch," local/trusted, and plain-language eligibility. Avoid jargon. Spanish-language consideration likely relevant for Kensington/Fairhill (unverified — confirm bilingual need).

## SEO / Campaign Notes
- **Google Ad Grants ($10k/mo in-kind search):** Caps at $2 CPC (smart bidding exempt), keyword quality-score floors, 5% CTR account minimum, must run 2 active ad groups/campaign, etc. Best fit for **informational/high-intent service queries** the org already answers on-site — housing counseling, foreclosure help, free home repairs, first-time homebuyer Philadelphia, free tax prep. Grant traffic should route to dedicated, conversion-ready landing pages (the org's history of slow page delivery is a known risk — see notes.md).
- **Audience segmentation is mandatory:** residents vs. donors vs. job seekers have opposite intents. Never co-mingle in one campaign. Donor/awareness work belongs on **paid social (Meta)**; high-intent service capture belongs on **search (Grant + a small paid Google layer for restricted terms)**.
- **Geo:** Tight radius targeting on Kensington/Fishtown/Port Richmond zip codes (19125, 19134, 19122, 19133) for resident programs; wider PA-county targeting only for housing counseling.
- **Language:** Test Spanish ad groups for Kensington/Fairhill resident programs (unverified need).
- **Nonprofit nuance:** Conversions are form-fills/appointment requests/calls, not purchases. Preserve the `ref=...` attribution param NKCDC uses (per notes.md) on all click-throughs.

## What Dillon Does For Them
**Scope: ICP research + keyword strategy + campaign architecture (Google Ads + Meta Ads).**

1. **ICP research (delivered/ongoing):** The multi-audience map above. Current paid build is correctly narrowed to Free Tax Prep small-biz ICP per Anthony. Keep a documented "program → audience → channel" matrix so future scope additions are deliberate, not creep.
2. **Keyword strategy:** Build keyword sets per program (start: Free Tax Prep; expandable to housing counseling, foreclosure/eviction help, free home repairs, first-time homebuyer). Separate brand vs. non-brand vs. crisis-intent. Crisis terms (eviction/foreclosure) convert highest — prioritize.
3. **Campaign architecture (recommended structure):**
   - **Google Search (paid, current):** Free Tax Prep — single-offer focus, exact/phrase match, tight geo, one landing page (businesstaxprep.fshtechnologies.org). Keep this as the launch unit.
   - **Google Ad Grants (recommended add):** Separate Grant account for evergreen service capture — Housing Counseling, Free Home Repairs, Foreclosure/Eviction Help, Homebuyer Workshops, Free Tax Prep. 1 campaign/program, 2+ ad groups each, dedicated LPs. $10k/mo free inventory the client is likely leaving on the table.
   - **Meta (paid):** Awareness + lead-gen by program; donor/volunteer retargeting; Spanish creative test. Warm, community-trust creative (not salesy).
4. **Google Ad Grant fit assessment:** **HIGH.** Recommend Momentum 360 either (a) confirm whether NKCDC already holds a Grant account, and if not (b) scope a Grant setup as a follow-on. The Grant + paid Google split lets paid budget cover Grant-restricted/competitive terms while the Grant covers everything else for free. This is the highest-leverage play available.
