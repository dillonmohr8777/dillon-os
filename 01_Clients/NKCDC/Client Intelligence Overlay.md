# NKCDC Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

NKCDC's Phase Two should organize growth around a small number of real Economic
Development programs and participant outcomes. SEO, AEO, content, ads, email,
SMS, partners, and events are supporting capabilities—not substitutes for a
clear offer, eligible audience, operational owner, conversion path, capacity,
and downstream program result.

## Current evidence snapshot

- Phase One focused on Free Business Tax Prep.
- The review-ready Phase Two strategy broadens the opportunity to programs,
  demand generation, conversion/nurture, authority/discovery, and measurement.
- Proposed audiences include small businesses, entrepreneurs, eligible program
  participants, local vendors, partners, and leadership.
- Leadership has not yet selected the two or three pilot priorities; eligibility,
  dates, owners, capacity, budget, permissions, and baseline outcomes remain
  explicit unknowns.

## 90-day operating path

Month 1 chooses priorities and builds verified offers, audiences, paths,
tracking, and follow-up. Month 2 launches only the approved channels and first
expert assets. Month 3 compares qualified participation and downstream program
outcomes, improves the strongest system, and decides the next quarter.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Facilitate the leadership decision on two or three priorities.
2. For each, verify eligibility, geography, capacity, dates, owner, offer,
   destination, follow-up, and outcome.
3. Capture current audience, registration, attendance, referral, and completion
   baselines.
4. Mine participant and partner language; then produce the search/content map.
5. Build the pilot scoreboard and review cadence before launch.
