# Brand Guidelines

## Tone
Nonprofit / community service. Warm, welcoming, access-oriented. Never commercial or salesy. The Free Business Tax Prep program is the only message in market right now.

## Audience
Small business owners in NKCDC's service area who qualify for free tax preparation. Framing should emphasize "free," "no catch," and community trust.
