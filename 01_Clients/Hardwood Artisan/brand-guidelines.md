# Brand Guidelines

## Tone
Craftsman, trustworthy, practical. Lean on specific jobs, specific neighborhoods, and real finishing techniques. Avoid generic home improvement language.

## Content pillars
- Wood floor refinishing — process, before/after, durability.
- Neighborhood / town references across Oklahoma service area.
- Seasonal project timing and scheduling availability.
