# Bercos Popcorn Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Bercos needs a launch-intelligence system, not speculative promotion. Product
catalog, availability, fulfillment, pricing, geography, brand story, GBP state,
analytics, checkout, lifecycle tools, and the approved working relationship must
be verified before keyword or campaign plans can be trusted.

## Current evidence snapshot

- The last communication evidence said the Shopify site remained with the
  development team and was expected by the end of July.
- Historical work lanes include Shopify launch readiness, GBP reinstatement,
  SEO, GA4, Search Console, email/SMS integration, pixels, and retargeting.
- No current finished-site handoff, live catalog audit, analytics baseline,
  customer-language corpus, or confirmed ongoing scope is present in the vault.

## Demand and answer map

Do not infer product types or customer segments from the brand name. Research
must start with the real catalog, approved markets, customer reviews, support
questions, purchase occasions, gift behavior, repeat-purchase patterns, and
order economics. Only then should clusters such as product, flavor, occasion,
gift, local, comparison, dietary, shipping, or wholesale be accepted.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Reconfirm the relationship, site owner, completion state, and review route.
2. Capture the Shopify catalog, navigation, checkout, policies, feeds, analytics,
   consent, and technical SEO state.
3. Verify GBP status and exact real-world business facts.
4. Establish product-level query, conversion, revenue, and repeat-purchase
   measurement.
5. Produce the first source-backed launch and 90-day growth backlog.
