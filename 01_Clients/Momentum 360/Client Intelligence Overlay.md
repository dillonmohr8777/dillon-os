# Momentum 360 Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Momentum 360 needs an agency operating system that makes ownership, client
truth, attribution, delivery quality, approvals, and learning visible across
many workstreams without collapsing them into a blended narrative. Shared tools
should standardize method; each client retains its own evidence and outcomes.

## Current evidence snapshot

- Active work spans client delivery, HubSpot, call attribution, reporting, AI
  enablement, training, websites, paid media, SEO, creative, sales, and new
  product concepts.
- Agency chatbot and CRM routes have been live; call data has reached the
  attribution layer; AI product and training materials exist.
- A paid-media reporting contract, client-level dashboards, CallRail/HubSpot
  completion notes, and decision bundles are present.
- Pricing, cohorts, platform administration, final product rollout, and some
  attribution/landing-page decisions remain approval gates.

## Strategy-system coverage

| System | State | Evidence | Gap | Next move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Demand intelligence | Developing | Cross-client search, ads, content, and sales work exists | Shared learning is not consistently compiled without blending data | Aggregate patterns only after client-level normalization |
| AEO/GEO | Developing | Site factory and trust gate exist | Client-specific baselines and outcomes vary | Require overlay, source date, and result per client |

| Pipeline | Developing | HubSpot, forms, chat, CallRail, and Voice Assist routes exist | Canonical stage, owner, deduplication, and revenue reconciliation incomplete | Finalize agency measurement contract and exceptions |
| Reporting | Operational | Paid-media contract and weekly surfaces exist | Client outcomes and data freshness still require independent source checks | Add freshness, definitions, gaps, and owner to every report |
| Workflow | Operational | AI, automation, training, site factory, and agent assets exist | Too many open loops can lose decision ownership | Maintain one canonical queue and bounded role cards |
| Governance | Operational | Human gates and client separation are established | Enforcement needs routine audits | Run client coverage, approval, and evidence checks weekly |

## Agency knowledge flow

```text
client evidence -> separate client overlay -> verified decision or experiment
-> reusable method stripped of client-sensitive data -> shared concept/template
-> next client application with fresh evidence
```

Never move raw client metrics or conclusions into the shared layer without
scope, source, date, definition, and privacy review.

## Next evidence sprint

   workstream.
2. Validate the HubSpot/call/form canonical lead and opportunity definitions.
3. Generate the agency scoreboard from separate client rows with freshness and
