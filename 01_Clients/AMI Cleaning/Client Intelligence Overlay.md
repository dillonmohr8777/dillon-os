# AMI Cleaning Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

AMI's new website creates the foundation for organic and campaign demand, but
growth should remain gated until a real inquiry reaches the correct HubSpot
record and named owner. The first win is not more traffic; it is a reliable
commercial-cleaning inquiry path with source, service fit, geography, and
disposition intact.

## Current evidence snapshot

- The WordPress site is live and Search Console began recording impressions on
  July 3.
- Work has included website launch, HubSpot audit, contact upload, email
  planning, SEO, Search Console, and form routing.
- Secure HubSpot routing and production-lead ownership remain open.
- A purchased-list test was discussed historically; list permission, quality,
  segmentation, deliverability, and business outcome are not established.

## Strategy-system coverage

| System | State | Evidence | Gap | Next move |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Keyword and demand | Developing | Early Search Console signal and commercial-cleaning offer | No query, service, geography, or outcome baseline | Export queries and validate them against real sales language |
| Intent architecture | Developing | New site and lead-capture path | Priority services and decision pages are not mapped here | Build service, industry, geography, proof, process, and FAQ map |

| Workflow | Blocked | WordPress and HubSpot routes identified | Secure access and failure escalation remain incomplete | Validate the production form receipt and notification chain |

## Demand and answer map

Research should begin with the exact services, markets, contract types,
industries, recurring-versus-project work, buying roles, and exclusions that AMI
can actually serve. Useful question families include scope, frequency, facility
type, quality control, onboarding, staffing, insurance, estimates, and service
area—only after AMI verifies the underlying facts.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Submit or observe one authorized production test through the complete routing
   chain without exposing personal data in the vault.
2. Capture Search Console queries/pages and current indexability.
3. Interview the lead owner for qualification criteria, objections, won/lost
   reasons, and profitable service types.
4. Produce the first client-specific keyword and page brief.
5. Start the scoreboard with inquiries, qualified opportunities, proposals, and
   wins rather than email volume or impressions alone.
