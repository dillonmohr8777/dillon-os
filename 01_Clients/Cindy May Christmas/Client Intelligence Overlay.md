# Cindy May Christmas Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

The approved package should become a distinctive, evidence-based brand and
recipe destination with clean discovery, newsletter capture, and commerce
handoffs. The immediate work is implementation discipline: preserve approved
copy and assets, resolve named dependencies, instrument the paths, and avoid
inventing brand claims or platform details.

## Current evidence snapshot

- The approved website package includes brand, recipe/method, asset, social,
  newsletter, and Shopify planning.
- Remaining known dependencies include the brand-video destination, newsletter
  connection details, photo mapping, and platform prerequisites.
- No current live traffic, search demand, subscriber, commerce, product,
  conversion, or revenue baseline is verified in the vault.

## Demand and answer map

Potential demand must be derived from approved recipes, techniques, brand story,
real products, customer questions, and seasonal use—not guessed from the name.
Each instructional page should state the outcome, ingredients or materials,
steps, timing, substitutions or limits, authorship, related methods, and next
action only where those facts are approved.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Reconfirm the approved package and identify every unresolved placeholder.
2. Assign owners and dates for video, newsletter, photography, Shopify, and
   production destinations.
3. Implement the canonical page and internal-link structure in staging.
4. Add analytics, consent, newsletter, and commerce events with test receipts.
5. Establish launch search, subscriber, engagement, and revenue baselines.
