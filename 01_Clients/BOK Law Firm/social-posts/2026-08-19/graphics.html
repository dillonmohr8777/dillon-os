Therapy Should Not Feel Like a Mystery

Child therapy during family change can stay clearer when parents know what to ask.

Kids rely on routine when one home becomes two.

Big feelings are allowed, even when words are hard.

A calm conversation can help you decide if support fits.

Family Friday

Kids Need Room to Feel the Change

A quiet evening at home can matter more than a packed weekend plan.

Keep one small routine the same in both homes.

Let kids have questions without rushing the answers.

Celebrate ordinary time, not a perfect schedule.

♥

Steady moments help children feel safe during family change.

Saturday Solutions

Questions to Bring Before Child Therapy

A short list can make the first conversation feel less overwhelming.

What will sessions look like for my child?

How are parents involved without putting kids in the middle?

What signs suggest extra support may help?

♥

These questions are for learning, not for building a court strategy.
