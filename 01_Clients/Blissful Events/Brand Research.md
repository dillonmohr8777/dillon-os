# Blissful Events — Brand Research

## Business Snapshot
- **DBA:** Blissful Events. **Legal name:** Blissful Aura Entertainment LLC.
- **What they do:** Independent promoter of Bollywood / South Asian live music concerts, primarily in the New Jersey / Atlantic City market. Books touring Indian playback and Bollywood pop artists into casino theaters and event centers.
- **Primary contact:** Ambika (role unconfirmed — likely marketing/coordination point of contact).
- **Organizer of record:** Praveen Vig (no public LinkedIn/press footprint surfaced — see Open Questions).
- **Engagement status:** **COMPLETED.** One-time $500 project for the April 11, 2026 "Power Duo" concert. Not retainer — re-engagement only if/when they book a new event.
- **Markets / cities:** Atlantic City, NJ (Borgata, Caesars) anchored; audience drawn from the NJ / NY / Philadelphia tri-state South Asian diaspora.
- **Event type:** Single-night marquee Bollywood concerts featuring big-name touring artists at premium casino venues.

- **Mika Singh & Kanika Kapoor — "The Power Duo" (Live in Concert)** — Borgata Hotel Casino & Spa (Event Center), Atlantic City, NJ. **April 11, 2026, 8:00 PM.** Organized by Blissful Aura Entertainment; marketed as the "first-ever Bollywood concert at the #1 casino MGM Borgata." Tickets from $79 via endlessevent.com (this is the Dillon engagement). *Verified.*

## Target Audience / ICP
- **Core:** South Asian diaspora (Indian / Punjabi / broader desi) across the NJ–NY–Philadelphia tri-state.
- **Age/region:** Roughly 25–55, family and friend-group attendees; Hindi/Punjabi-speaking households; high-income enough to travel to AC and buy $79–$349 tickets.
- **Psychographics:** Bollywood music fans, casino-night / nightlife crowd, nostalgia + party-anthem listeners. The "Power Duo" framing (party king + party-anthem queen) targets dance/celebration intent over seated-concert intent.
- **Geo for paid media:** AC drive-radius from Edison/Iselin/Jersey City/Newark NJ corridors, NYC outer boroughs, and Philadelphia/South Jersey.

## Brand Voice & Positioning
- **Voice:** High-energy, hype-driven, exclusivity-forward. Press language leaned on superlatives ("first-ever," "Party King," "queen of party anthems," "best of a DJ night and concert combined").
- **Positioning:** Premium-venue Bollywood night out — pairing a recognizable big-name lineup with a marquee casino brand (Borgata) as a trust/prestige signal. Sells the *experience* (call-and-response, big chorus moments) more than the seat.

## Online Presence
- **Ticketing / discovery:** **endlessevent.com** — a self-serve Bollywood/Indian event ticketing + discovery platform (organizer.endlessevent.com for listing; iOS & Android "Endless Event" apps; no convenience fee pitch to organizers). Blissful's event lived at the endlessevent.com event page.
- **Cross-listed on aggregators:** Sulekha Events (events.sulekha.com), Festivals.com, plus general AC events calendars. YouTube promo asset exists for the Power Duo show.

## Paid Media Notes
- This is **event-ticketing performance marketing**, not always-on brand — short, deadline-bound flight ending at event date (April 11).
- **Meta (Facebook/Instagram):** event-ad RSVP objective, geo + South-Asian/Bollywood interest + language targeting, pixel/conversion tracking to the endlessevent.com ticket flow.
- **Google PMax (Local):** drive AC-radius and tri-state ticket-intent traffic.
- **Mechanics that fit this client:** urgency/scarcity ("first-ever," "tickets going fast," tiered pricing), RSVP-then-retarget, and **lookalikes built off past ticket buyers / RSVP list** — strongly recommended if they reuse the endlessevent buyer data for the next show.

## What Dillon Did / Could Do Again
- **Did (completed):** Facebook Event Ads (RSVP, targeting, tracking), Google Local PMax, FB event management, competitor research, social gap analysis. One-time, $500.
- **Could do again (re-engagement):** This is the highest-value play — Blissful runs *episodic* concerts, so each new event = a repeatable ~$500+ media-setup engagement. Pitch a "per-event growth package" so the buyer audience, pixel, and creative templates carry over and compound across shows.

## Opportunities (future events)
