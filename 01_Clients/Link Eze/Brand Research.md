# Link Eze (LinkEZE) — Brand Research

> **Identity confirmed.** LinkEZE sells ATV/UTV ground-engaging implements (landscape rakes, scrape blades, cultivators, disc harrows, 3-point hitch lift systems) for small-scale farming, food plots, land/property maintenance, and trail work — i.e., "tractor-style" attachments for ATVs/UTVs that don't need a tractor. It is a consumer-facing brand operated by **Uniparts USA, Ltd.**, the US arm of **Uniparts Group**, a global manufacturer of 3-point linkage (3PL) systems. Site: linkeze.biz (Shopify). A mirror exists at linkeze.info.
>
> *Note: linkeze.biz and Tractor Supply product pages return HTTP 403 to automated fetch (Shopify/CDN bot protection), so exact live prices could not be scraped. Product/positioning facts below come from search-indexed page snippets; specific dollar prices are marked (unverified) and should be pulled from the live site/feed.*

## Business Snapshot
- **What they sell:** Category "0" / light-duty ground-engaging implements engineered for ATVs and UTVs — landscape rakes, scrape/grading blades, S-tine cultivators, disc harrows, chisel plows, tool bars, and 12V electric-actuator 3-point hitch lift systems. Universal 2" receiver-hitch mount; "no tractor needed." [1][2][6][8]
- **Use cases:** Small farms, hobby/backyard properties, food-plot prep, driveway grading, trail maintenance, gravel/soil work. [3][8]
- **B2B vs B2C:** Primarily **B2C / DTC ecommerce** (and retail via Tractor Supply). The *parent* Uniparts is B2B/OEM; LinkEZE is the consumer brand that productizes Uniparts' 3PL expertise for the powersports/landowner market. [3][6]
- **Parent company:** **Uniparts USA, Ltd.**, 1431 Marvin Griffin Rd, Augusta, GA 30906 — a warehousing/distribution arm of **Uniparts Group**, an India-based global manufacturer (operations since 1984; ~8 facilities across India, USA, Germany; products reach ~25 countries). Uniparts is a leading global maker of 3-point linkage systems and precision-machined parts for the off-highway market. [4][6][7]
- **Contact on file:** Biru Gupta, biru.gupta@unipartsgroup.com — consistent with the Uniparts Group parent affiliation.

## Target Audience / ICP
- ATV/UTV owners who want tractor-like ground work without owning a tractor.
- Small/hobby farmers, food-plot hunters, rural landowners, ranchers, large-acreage homeowners. [3][8]
- DIY land/driveway maintenance buyers; value durability + easy install + universal fit.
- Demographic skew: rural/exurban US, male-leaning, powersports + ag/hunting overlap.

## Brand Voice & Positioning
- Practical, rugged, capability-focused: "tractor-level capability for compact, all-terrain vehicles," "no tractor needed," "one-stop shop for lawn/land preparation." [1][6]
- Leans on parent credibility: "50+ years of global leadership in 3PL systems" (Uniparts heritage) as a trust/quality differentiator vs. generic import attachments. [6]
- Emphasis on engineered durability (steel, powder coat), universal 2" receiver fit, and electric-actuator convenience.

## SEO / Shopping / Market Notes
- **Branded vs generic:** "LinkEZE" branded terms are partly shared with Tractor Supply (TSC ranks for LinkEZE SKUs) — branded Search + Shopping defense matters so TSC doesn't intercept their own demand.
- **High-intent generic terms:** "ATV 3 point hitch," "UTV landscape rake," "ATV cultivator/disc harrow," "ATV food plot implements," "scrape blade for UTV," "no tractor 3 point hitch." Strong Shopping/PMax fit (visual, considered-purchase, $-priced).
- **Feed considerations (Shopify → Google Merchant Center):**
  - Ensure GTIN/MPN, brand=LinkEZE, accurate `google_product_category` (Vehicles & Parts → Vehicle Parts / Lawn & Garden) on every SKU.
  - Titles should front-load fitment + type + size (e.g., "LinkEZE 60 in. ATV/UTV Landscape Rake — Universal 2" Receiver").
  - Surface compatibility ("fits ATV/UTV w/ 2" receiver") and key specs (width, tine count, actuator) in title/description and custom labels.
  - Custom labels for margin tier, bundles vs single, promo/clearance for bid segmentation.
  - Watch duplicate-content/feed risk across linkeze.biz vs linkeze.info.
- **Price-display restriction:** If MAP/price-gating applies (as TSC implies), confirm GMC pricing matches landing page to avoid disapprovals.
