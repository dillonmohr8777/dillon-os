## Important Note on PMax Asset Groups

Google's system requires square and vertical format videos to reach Excellent ad strength for Performance Max campaigns. All currently available LinkEZE YouTube videos are horizontal (16:9) format.

To push these PMax asset groups from Average to Excellent, you'll need to upload square (1:1) or vertical (9:16) versions of your product videos to YouTube and add them here. Google also noted it may auto-generate video formats in most cases.
