## Recommended 60-minute agenda

1. **5 min — Outcome:** confirm decisions required today.
2. **10 min — Tori walkthrough:** listen and capture intent before critique.
3. **10 min — Product model:** roles, first user, success loop, retailer/dispensary distinction.
4. **12 min — Prototype:** directory → profile → contact request → dashboard → admin review.
5. **10 min — Visual direction:** react to attributes before individual colors.
6. **8 min — MVP boundary:** confirm in/out and classify change requests.
7. **5 min — Commitments:** owners, assets, next deliverables, next review.

## Discovery questions

### Product and users

- Who feels this problem most acutely today?
- What is the first valuable outcome a new member should achieve?
- What would make someone trust a Bridge profile?
- Which role supplies network value first?
- What behavior would make the directory feel empty or unsuccessful?

### Verification

- What does Tori mean when she says “verified”?
- Is verification organization-level, person-level, or both?
- Which licenses/evidence are required by role and state?
- Who reviews exceptions and disputes?
- How often does verification expire?

### Discovery and contact

- Which five filters matter most?
- Should unverified profiles appear in results?
- Can visitors browse without an account?
- Which contact information is visible before acceptance?
- What must a contact request contain?

### Brand

- What three feelings must Bridge create in ten seconds?
- Should Bridge relate visually to The Trap?
- Which category clichés should be avoided?
- Does the name need a descriptor for clarity/trademark/search?
- Which direction feels closest, and what is specifically wrong with the others?

## Meeting discipline

- Record each decision as `approved`, `preferred pending revision`, `rejected`, or `open`.
- Separate usability findings from visual preferences.
- Classify new functionality as existing scope, clarification, or change request.
- Do not promise automated legal/license verification without authoritative sources and review ownership.
- End with a verbal read-back of decisions, owners, and dates.
