# Brand Guidelines — Provisional

> [!warning] Not client-approved
> This kit exists to make discovery concrete. It is not an official Bridge identity and must be reconciled with Tori's original prototype and any privately held assets.

## Recommended direction: Trusted Current

Bridge should feel like trusted business infrastructure with a human connection layer. Avoid generic corporate-software blue, overused cannabis-leaf imagery, and consumer-dispensary aesthetics.

### Attributes

- Credible, not clinical
- Modern, not trendy
- Connected, not noisy
- Industry-aware, not stereotypical
- Warm, not casual

### Color tokens

| Token | Hex | Use |
|---|---:|---|
| Bridge Navy | `#12324A` | Logo field, primary actions, large brand surfaces |
| Current Teal | `#0A766E` | Links, selection, connection moments |
| Signal Amber | `#D9820F` | Introductions, pending states, small attention details |
| Canvas | `#F5F8F7` | Page background |
| Surface | `#FFFFFF` | Cards, fields, tables |
| Primary Text | `#14232E` | Headlines/body |
| Muted Text | `#5D6D76` | Supporting metadata |
| Border | `#CEDBD7` | Dividers/controls |
| Success | `#177245` | Verified/completed |
| Error | `#B42318` | Blocking/failed |

Do not use amber for small body text on white. Never rely on color alone for states; pair color with a label/icon.

### Typography

Current portable stack: Aptos Display, Segoe UI, and system sans. Before production, select and license one of:

- Neutral grotesk for software-forward direction
- Humanist sans for warmer professional network
- Restrained display face for marketing headings paired with legible UI sans

Maintain 16px minimum body text, short lines, clear hierarchy, and accessible contrast.

### Mark

The provisional mark is a simplified bridge in a rounded square. It communicates connection and scales to an app icon. It is not trademark-ready.

Avoid:

- Cannabis leaf as primary mark
- Thin bridge lines below 24px
- Low-contrast gradients
- “The Ecosystem” lockups because that is the retired name

### Botanical Ledger

- Forest `#173C2C`
- Sage `#4F6F57`
- Copper `#B45F2A`
- Canvas `#F6F5EF`

Best if Tori wants premium, grounded, category-aware branding without consumer-cannabis clichés.

## UI principles

- Foreground role, location, verification, and partnership intent.
- Keep private contact details permissioned.
- Describe verification without implying legal endorsement.
- Administrative screens prioritize evidence, status, and auditability.
- Marketing pages may be expressive; operational screens remain calm and efficient.

## Voice

- **Direct:** state the action and next state.
- **Credible:** avoid hype and unsupported claims.
- **Human:** professional and warm.
- **Industry-aware:** use accurate cannabis-business terminology without stereotypes.

## Approval questions

1. Which three attributes must a member feel immediately?
2. Should Bridge stand apart from The Trap or share family resemblance?
3. Are there existing logos, colors, fonts, domains, or handles outside the audited history?
4. Is Bridge the final master brand, and does it need a descriptor?
5. Which direction is closest, and what is specifically wrong with the others?
