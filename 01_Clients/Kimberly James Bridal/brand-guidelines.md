# Brand Guidelines

## Palette
- Navy #2B3A52
- Gold #C9A96E / #C9AB5D
- Cream

## Typography
- **Body:** Cormorant Garamond.
- **Cursive accents:** Great Vibes.
- **Caps headings:** Montserrat.
- **Logo cursive "James":** Mrs Saint Delafield.

## Logo
Custom SVG: "KIMBERLY" in Montserrat caps, "James" in Mrs Saint Delafield gold cursive, "BRIDAL" in Montserrat caps.

## Tone
Elegant, warm, personal, boutique luxury. Never pushy, never discount-driven. Lean into concierge language, personal styling, and timeless craftsmanship.
