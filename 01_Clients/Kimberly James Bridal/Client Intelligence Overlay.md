# Kimberly James Bridal Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

KJB should optimize for qualified bridal appointments, not clicks or raw form
fills. Search, Meta, landing pages, FAQs, size-inclusive content, boutique entity
signals, scheduling, stylist follow-up, and appointment disposition need one
privacy-safe chain.

## Current evidence snapshot

- A higher-intent Meta lead campaign was published July 27 with four required
  qualification questions, a $10 daily budget, and the older traffic campaign
  turned off.
- The latest campaign readback was `Processing`; this is not proof of delivery,
  lead volume, quality, or appointments.
- Earlier evidence recorded Google paused and Meta active, but live channel state
  must be refreshed before present-tense reporting.
- Existing plans cover appointment measurement, same-window reconciliation,
  Search controls, Meta blueprints, FAQ/SEO work, and lead-quality review.

## Demand and answer map

Prioritize verified boutique location, appointment process, gown styles,
investment fit, timing, sizing, designer/collection facts, shopping preparation,
stylist experience, accessibility, and real reviews. Do not invent inventory,
pricing, guarantees, appointment availability, designer relationships, or
customer outcomes.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Read back current Google and Meta campaign state and the new form's delivery.
2. Verify lead delivery, stylist owner, response time, and disposition fields.
3. Reconcile the first forms through booked and attended appointments.
4. Export Search Console, paid search terms, GBP, and scheduling-source evidence.
5. Use qualified appointment language to prioritize FAQs, local pages, creative,
   exclusions, and the next channel decision.
