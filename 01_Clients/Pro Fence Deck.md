## Voice & Proof
Ten Google reviews are hard-coded on every page. The repeating themes are the real brand story:
1. **Sergei answers.** Fast texts/calls back, before *and after* the job. ("After companies finish their jobs you never hear from them. Not this company.")
2. **Honest pricing.** Best price out of several quotes, right price/value, no games.
3. **Obsessive detail.** Sections aligned and perfectly pitched, bends fabricated to match stonework, gates modified for slope, tiles leveled to match a ramp.
4. **Speed.** Weeks when competitors quoted months; worked into the late evening to hold a timeline.
5. **Repeat customers & referrals.** One customer used them 3 times; several handed the number to neighbors.

## Flags / Things to Verify Before Publishing
- **Years in business is inconsistent across the site.** Homepage says "Since 1998," About says "the past ten years," the Warrington gate page says "over 15 years," and a badge says "20+ Years of Combined Experience." Pick one and confirm with Sergei before it goes in a video.
- **Wood decks: yes or no?** The Decks page body copy offers "a traditional wood deck," but the FAQ on the same page says "We do not offer wood decks." Confirm.
- **Two phone numbers** in the header. Confirm which one should appear on screen.
- The "20+ years / 100,000 pool fences installed" stat is the *pool fence manufacturer's* claim, not Pro Fence Deck's. Don't attribute it to them on camera.
