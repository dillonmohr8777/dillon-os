# Florecita — Brand Research

## Business Snapshot

**CONFIRMED:** Florecita is a **wedding/event florist** (NOT a restaurant) based in
**Phoenix, Arizona**, operating as **Florecita Flower Co.** — website
[florecitaphx.com](https://florecitaphx.com/), Instagram
[@florecita.phx](https://www.instagram.com/florecita.phx/) (bio: "florecita · phoenix
floral design co." / "florecita ✿ arizona florist").

- **Founder:** Maddy — a Latina creative; started Florecita in **2022**. Described as a
  certified Master Florist via the Arizona State Floral Association and recipient of the
  Sharon Luoma Scholarship. (per web search summaries of florecitaphx.com)
- **Positioning:** Luxury, modern/romantic, **culturally inspired** floral design.
- **Service area:** Phoenix + greater Arizona.

## Target Audience / ICP
- **Primary:** engaged couples in metro Phoenix/Scottsdale planning weddings, 6–14 months
  out. Skews design-conscious, Pinterest/Instagram-driven.
- **Cultural niche:** the founder's Latina identity + "culturally inspired" branding is a
  real differentiator — strong fit for couples wanting bilingual/Latino-rooted aesthetics
  (a meaningful segment in Phoenix). High-intent, brand-loyal.
- **Secondary:** wedding planners & venues (referral/B2B), corporate event buyers.
- **Budget band (floral):** likely **$2,500–$8,000** range for full weddings *(inferred
  from positioning; confirm)*.

## SEO / Paid Media Notes
- **Instagram-led** is correct for this category — visual proof + reels drive discovery and
  the founder's personality is a selling point. Treat IG as top-of-funnel + brand.
- **Meta Ads (lead gen):** wedding florals are a considered, seasonal, geo-tight buy.
  Run lead-gen / Instant Forms or click-to-message campaigns to engaged-couple audiences in
  metro Phoenix; lean on carousel/reel creative of real installs. Layer interest/behavior
  signals (engaged, wedding planning) + radius targeting + age band.
- **Google Search:** capture high-intent demand on "wedding florist Phoenix," "Scottsdale
  wedding flowers," "Phoenix wedding florist [neighborhood/venue]," Spanish-language variants,
  and "Latina/Mexican wedding florist Phoenix" (low-competition, on-brand). Pair with a
  conversion-focused landing page + lead form.
- **Seasonality:** AZ wedding peak is roughly Oct–Apr (cooler months); concentrate budget
  into the booking-decision window ahead of peak.
