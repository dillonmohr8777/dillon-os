# Nexla Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Nexla has enough search evidence to protect brand demand and test tightly
controlled enterprise-data intent, but not enough downstream truth to optimize
or launch by assumption. The conversion definition, landing-page route,
tracking owner, and exact approved budget must be resolved before the prepared
nonbrand work can become a live campaign.

## Current evidence snapshot

- The Google Ads audit was delivered on August 20, 2026.
- Brand demand is the clearest observed account signal; nonbrand efficiency is
  uneven and requires query, creative, and landing-page control.
- The current operating context records approval to consider a $2,000 monthly
  budget, while the vault overview still states approximately $1,000. Treat the
  budget as unresolved until the current thread is revalidated.
- The preferred Enterprise Data Layer and MCP landing pages, primary conversion
  action, and Google Ads versus GA4 tracking authority remain unconfirmed.
- The proposed build remains unpublished. No dedicated client Slack channel is
  verified, so Gmail is the client communication route.

## Demand and answer map

Treat brand, enterprise data layer, MCP integration, AI-agent data access, data
pipelines, and analytics infrastructure as separate hypotheses until current
queries, landing pages, and approved product language support them. Do not infer
feature claims, integrations, pricing, or conversion quality from category
language alone.

3. Name the primary conversion action and whether Google Ads, GA4, or both own
   its tracking truth.
