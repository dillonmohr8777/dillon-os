## Content and legal guardrails

Do not invent guests, episodes, dates, listening platforms, audience size,
outcomes, awards, testimonials, legal advice, or sponsor benefits. Preserve the
firm disclaimer and avoid implying an attorney-client relationship. Use the
official firm site and supplied sponsor identity as factual sources.
