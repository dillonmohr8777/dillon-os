## SEO / Market Notes
- **Seasonality:** Two demand peaks drive HVAC local search — **heating/furnace** (Oct-Feb, sharp spikes on first cold snaps and during deep-freeze "no heat" events) and **cooling/AC** (May-Aug, spikes during heat waves). Shoulder seasons (Mar-Apr, Sep-Oct) = best window to push **tune-up / maintenance-plan** content and pre-book demand. Right now (late May) = AC season ramp; content should be AC repair / AC prep / "beat the heat" focused.
- **Emergency-service intent:** "no heat," "furnace not working," "AC not cooling," "emergency HVAC near me" are the highest-converting queries. These should be priority on both **Google Ads Search** and **LSA**, with 24/7 messaging front and center.
- **LSA factors that move the needle:** Google Guaranteed badge (background check + license/insurance verification — note the Feb/Mar 2026 Evident reset history), responsiveness/answer rate to LSA leads, review count + recency (Shadow's high review volume is a strong LSA asset — keep it fed), accurate service-area + job-type settings, and dispute/credit hygiene on bad leads. LSA ranking is heavily review- and responsiveness-weighted, so a fast lead-answer SOP matters as much as bid.
- **GBP:** 4 posts/week is aggressive (good) — geo-tag and reference real neighborhoods/towns; freshness + review responses + Q&A all support local pack ranking.
