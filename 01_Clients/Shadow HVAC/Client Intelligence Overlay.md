# Shadow Heating and Cooling Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Shadow's website, local discovery, service-area content, paid lead generation,
forms, calls, follow-up, and completed HVAC work should operate as one closed
loop. The immediate strategy is not to restart ads; it is to reconcile the
historical lead stream and verify current delivery, local entity truth, and
business outcomes.

## Current evidence snapshot

- Historical evidence described a live website, Google submission, Meta lead
  generation, improving lead quality, and local-search support.
- A live Meta Ads Manager readback captured July 26/27 found both observed
  Shadow campaigns off, zero active/delivering campaigns, and zero active ads;
  no mutation was needed.
- Earlier weekly reports contained lead and CPL figures, but those periods do
  not prove current delivery or completed jobs.
- Website QA, Lighthouse, analytics/form reporting, launch authority, Meta
  blueprint, and pause receipts exist.

## Demand and answer map

Prioritize verified HVAC services, service areas, urgent versus planned needs,
diagnostic questions, maintenance, replacement/repair decisions, seasonality,
financing only if approved, trust proof, availability, and contact routes. Never
invent licenses, pricing, warranties, response times, service radius, rebates,
equipment relationships, or outcomes.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Re-verify Meta, Google, website, GBP, and form/call state read-only.
2. Reconcile historical leads to contacted, qualified, booked, completed, lost,
   duplicate, or invalid outcomes.
3. Capture current query/search-term and service-area demand.
4. Audit entity/NAP, schema, local proof, reviews, and priority page ownership.
5. Decide whether organic repairs, follow-up, or a bounded paid restart has the
   strongest evidence and capacity fit.
