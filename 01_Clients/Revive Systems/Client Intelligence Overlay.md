# Revive Systems Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Revive needs one coherent organic path from an approved entry offer into the
higher-touch VIP coaching journey. Search, AEO/GEO, proof, application copy,
internal links, mobile UX, forms, nurture, and outcome measurement should support
that progression without increasing paid exposure or mutating HighLevel before
the exact authorized route is mapped.

## Current evidence snapshot

- A five-article AEO/GEO/SEO cluster and organic acquisition/sitemap package
  exist around health coaching, strength after 40, routines, program choice, and
  coach/trainer/dietitian comparison.
- Public-site verification found multiple competing entry actions and technical
  issues on application and legacy pages, including weak titles and excessive
  H1 counts.
- The agreed direction centers the entry offer and VIP progression, improves the
  application page, repositions the 7-Day asset as nurture, adds proof at
  objections, and repairs metadata, headings, links, and CTAs.
- Live HighLevel access, forms, notifications, analytics, pipeline, indexing,
  and conversions remain authority or evidence gaps.

## Demand and answer map

Use verified program, audience, geography, coaching, training, nutrition,
application, routine, and progression facts. Do not invent medical claims,
guarantees, outcomes, pricing, credentials, availability, or customer proof.
Clarify who each path serves, what happens next, and what evidence supports it.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Map exact authorized HighLevel, analytics, Search Console, and form routes.
2. Capture live page, query, event, and pipeline baseline.
3. Approve the canonical entry/application/VIP architecture and CTA language.
4. Stage technical, content, internal-link, and mobile repairs with QA receipts.
5. Verify application delivery and disposition before indexing claims or calling
   the funnel complete.
