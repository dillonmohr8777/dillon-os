# Revive Systems — 48-Hour Lead-Recovery Brief (Internal Draft)

## Purpose
Restore a measurable lead path before recommending additional spend. This draft uses only the verified local brief: the funnel revision is incomplete, and the required scope is GBP, AEO/GEO, conversion paths, a three-in-30-days plan, and approval-gated budget options. No live website, GBP, ad-account, CRM, call-tracking, or analytics findings are claimed yet.

## 48-hour operating plan

### Hours 0–6 — establish source of truth
- Confirm canonical website/domain, GBP profile, primary service areas, primary offers, and current contact owner.
- Inventory live conversion destinations: phone, form, booking, chat, email, and directions.
- Pull 30-day evidence from GA4, Search Console, GBP, call tracking, CRM/form inbox, and paid-media accounts where access exists.
- Create one source-to-lead reconciliation table; mark unknowns explicitly.

### Hours 6–18 — repair the conversion path
- Test every primary CTA on desktop and mobile.
- Verify form delivery, confirmation state, phone tap behavior, booking completion, UTM persistence, and lead ownership.
- Identify dead ends, duplicate events, false conversions, slow pages, and unclear offer/message transitions.
- Draft fixes for internal review. Publishing, account edits, and tracking changes remain approval-gated.

### Hours 18–30 — GBP + AEO/GEO recovery
- Audit GBP category, services, description, products, photos, reviews, Q&A, NAP, hours, appointment URL, and UTM tagging.
- Build a question map from real sales objections and high-intent local queries.
- Draft concise answer blocks, service-area pages, FAQ/schema recommendations, proof points, and entity references for search and answer engines.
- Separate verified facts from copy hypotheses requiring client confirmation.

### Hours 30–42 — build the three-in-30 plan
1. **Conversion integrity:** one verified primary lead event and a weekly source-to-lead reconciliation.
2. **Local demand capture:** one GBP improvement cycle plus one high-intent service/location page or offer page.
3. **Authority and follow-up:** one proof asset (case study, testimonial, process page, or comparison guide) plus a defined lead-response SLA.

### Hours 42–48 — decision pack
- Summarize baseline, verified breakpoints, proposed repairs, owners, due dates, and measurement plan.
- Rank actions by unblock value: tracking/delivery first, conversion friction second, traffic acquisition third.
- Present paid options only after the lead path is verified.

## Approval-gated budget options
- **Option A — No added spend:** repair measurement, conversion paths, GBP, and organic answer assets first.
- **Option B — Controlled validation:** a small, time-boxed campaign only after the primary conversion and lead-delivery path pass QA.
- **Option C — Scale:** only after source, lead quality, response time, and economics are reconciled.

No budget, targeting, campaign, tracking, GBP, site, or production-account change is authorized by this draft.
