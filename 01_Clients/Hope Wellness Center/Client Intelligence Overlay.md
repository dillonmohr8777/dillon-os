# Hope Wellness Center Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Hope can grow by making the path from state and service intent to an appropriate
telehealth appointment unmistakable: verified eligibility, provider fit,
clinical trust, booking clarity, and measured downstream outcomes. Marketing
cannot decide clinical, licensure, prescribing, insurance, age, or crisis facts.

## Current evidence snapshot

- The audited site describes telehealth-only delivery across Rhode Island,
  Massachusetts, New York, Colorado, and Arizona.
- Observable content covers psychiatric evaluation, medication management,
  therapy, testing, substance-use-related care, and condition topics, but
  availability is not proven uniformly across states or providers.
- The site audit found 50 page URLs, 52 post URLs, and eight potential base/`-2`
  duplicate topic pairs.
- Leadership approval, live analytics, booking outcomes, Search Console, CRM,
  provider eligibility, and GBP operating eligibility remain unresolved.

## Measurement contract

Track state and service page engagement, provider views, appointment clicks,
booking starts/completions, form starts/submits, and phone intent as separate
events. Report whole integer counts and preserve the distinction between an
event, inquiry, clinically appropriate appointment, attended appointment, and
business outcome. Never place sensitive patient details in the vault.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Resolve leadership decisions and approve the clinical/service/state truth
   matrix.
2. Repair the visible contact issue and temporary utility page in staging.
3. Verify analytics, booking, and form events with privacy-safe receipts.
4. Capture Search Console and technical baselines and audit duplicates.
5. Build the first state/service hubs only from approved eligibility and expert
   evidence, then test the complete booking path.
