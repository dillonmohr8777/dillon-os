# Onsite Concrete & Landscape Client Intelligence Overlay

## Executive operating thesis

Onsite can compound local authority by joining Vacaville and Solano County
service demand, useful answer-first content, verified project proof, entity/NAP
consistency, paid search, calls/forms, qualified estimates, and booked work. The
current bottleneck is contact reconciliation, not raw click generation.

## Current evidence snapshot

- Google Ads evidence through July 26 recorded $60.36 spend, 11,008 impressions,
  344 clicks, and six tracked events; Performance Max contained all six visible
  events on $23.13 campaign spend.
- Those events were pending named-contact and qualified-estimate validation.
- The local content system includes Vacaville concrete/landscape pages and
  approved blogs on retaining walls, drainage, repair/replacement, pool decks,
  and full-service landscape work.
- Measurement design and deduplication correction records exist, but downstream
  estimates and booked jobs are not established by the platform count.

## Demand and answer map

Prioritize verified service and location combinations, repair versus replace,
drainage, slopes, retaining walls, hardscape, pool decks, full-service landscape
scope, project process, estimates, and proof. Do not invent pricing, permits,
timing, warranties, financing, service radius, availability, or results.

## Next evidence sprint

1. Match the six events to calls, forms, inbox, and CRM records.
2. Record service, location, qualification, response, estimate, and outcome.
3. Export search terms and connect accepted/excluded language to service pages.
4. Audit entity/NAP, GBP, schema, project proof, internal links, and indexation.
5. Choose the next budget, page, and content tests from qualified-estimate mix.
