## Target Audience / ICP
- **Primary:** Suburban homeowners in Solano County (Vacaville, Fairfield, Dixon) wanting outdoor upgrades — new/replacement driveways, backyard patios, stamped-concrete curb-appeal, full backyard/landscape redesigns.
- **Secondary:** Light commercial / business property owners needing flatwork and foundations.

## SEO / Market Notes
- **Intent:** High-value local-service intent — "concrete contractor Vacaville," "stamped concrete patio [city]," "driveway replacement near me," "landscaping Fairfield." Strong commercial intent, geo-bound.
- **Existing footprint:** Site already has per-city service landing pages = good local-SEO skeleton; ensure each has unique content, NAP consistency, schema, and is crawlable (see 403 concern).
- **Seasonality:** Concrete/landscape demand peaks **spring–summer–early fall** in NorCal (dry season, outdoor-living spend). Winter (rainy season) is slower — pace ad budget up Mar–Sep.
- **Competitive SEO:** Several competitors run dedicated stamped-concrete and city pages; market is contested. Reviews volume (Onsite ~15 on Yelp) is modest vs. some rivals — review generation is a lever.
