# AI SEO Playbook — Ranking in Google and AI Search (AEO/GEO)

## The core shift

Buyers now ask ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity, and Google's AI Overviews who to hire. Traditional SEO alone leaves you invisible in those answers. The playbook optimizes for both: classic organic rankings AND AI-engine citations (AEO — AI Engine Optimization, also called GEO — Generative Engine Optimization).

## Local SEO foundation (do first)

1. **One page per service per city** you actually serve. "Garage door repair Wichita" is its own page with unique content, not a paragraph on a services page.
2. **On-page basics**: service + city in title tag, H1, first 100 words, and URL. One clear CTA above the fold with phone number.
3. **Internal linking**: every service page links to related services and the city hub; the homepage links to the money pages.
4. **Site speed**: under 3s mobile load. Compress images, kill unused scripts. Speed is a ranking and conversion factor.
5. **Content cadence**: 2–4 posts/month answering real customer questions ("How much does X cost in [city]", "X vs Y", "How long does X take"). Question-formatted H2s.

## AEO: getting cited by AI engines

AI engines cite sources that are structured, factual, and consistent:
1. **Answer-first writing**: open every page/post with a 2–3 sentence direct answer (TL;DR), then expand. AI engines lift these.
2. **FAQ schema + Q&A blocks** on every service page — the exact questions buyers ask, answered in 40–80 words each.
3. **Entity consistency**: identical business name, address, phone, and service descriptions across site, GBP, directories, and social. AI engines cross-check.
4. **Structured data**: LocalBusiness/ProfessionalService JSON-LD with services, areas served, reviews.
5. **Third-party corroboration**: reviews, local press, directory listings, and industry mentions — AI answers weight sources that other sources confirm.
6. **Track AI visibility**: monitor 15–25 buying-intent prompts monthly ("best [service] in [city]", "who should I hire for X") across ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity. Log mentions, position, and sentiment.

In client campaigns this combination produced 801 AI citations and average AI ranking position #2 (Vista River Hospice) and first-position AI answers for local service queries (Samuels Garage Doors).

## Keyword strategy

- Split money keywords (buy intent: "emergency", "near me", "cost", "install", "repair") from research keywords (learn intent). Money keywords get service pages; research keywords get posts that funnel to them.
- Expect movement in 60–90 days, meaningful lead flow in 4–6 months. SEO builds an asset; ads buy time while it compounds.

## What to report

Rankings are means, not ends. Report: qualified calls/forms from organic, keyword movement (Top 3 / Top 10 counts), AI mentions and positions, and GBP actions. Tie every number to leads.
