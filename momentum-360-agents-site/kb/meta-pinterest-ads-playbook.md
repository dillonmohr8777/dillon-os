# Paid Social Playbook — Meta Ads & Pinterest Ads

## Meta Ads (Facebook & Instagram) launch

1. **Offer before ads**: ads amplify an offer; they can't fix a weak one. Lead with a concrete, time-bound, risk-reversed offer ("$89 tune-up this month", "free design consult").
2. **Account structure (keep it simple)**: 1 campaign per objective. Start with 1 prospecting campaign (Advantage+ or 2–3 ad sets: broad, interest stack, lookalike) + 1 retargeting campaign (site visitors + engagers, 30–90 days).
3. **Pixel + CAPI**: install the pixel AND Conversions API before spending. Track the real conversion (lead form, call, booking) — not page views.
4. **Creative volume**: launch with 3–5 distinct concepts (not 5 crops of one image). Formats that work for local service: before/after, owner-to-camera 30s, customer testimonial clip, job-site photo carousel, "mistakes to avoid" hook.
5. **Budget**: minimum viable test is ~$30–50/day for 2 weeks per market. Judge nothing before 50 leads or 2 weeks, whichever first.

## Weekly optimization loop

- Check frequency (>3 on prospecting = fatigue), CPM trend, CTR (link CTR <0.8% = creative problem), cost per lead vs target, and lead quality feedback from the owner.
- Kill: ads below account-average CTR after 3+ days at meaningful spend. Scale: raise budget 20% at a time on winners (bigger jumps reset learning).
- Refresh creative every 3–4 weeks; fatigue is the #1 silent killer in local accounts.

## Creative testing SOP

One variable at a time: hook (first 2s / first line) → format (video vs carousel vs static) → offer framing. Name ads by variable (HOOK-A_VID_OFFER1) so results read at a glance. Log every test: hypothesis, result, decision. The library of "what worked" is the compounding asset.

## Pinterest Ads

Pinterest is a planning engine — users search months before buying. Best for visual, consideration-heavy purchases: remodels, landscaping, custom furniture, weddings, home design.
- **Targeting**: keyword-target like search ads ("kitchen remodel ideas", "backyard patio"), layer interests. Seasonal intent starts 2–3 months early — run Christmas campaigns in October.
- **Creative**: vertical 2:3 pins, text overlay stating the idea, brand mark on-pin. Idea pins/video outperform static for reach; static wins for direct response.
- **Funnel**: pin → idea-rich landing page (gallery/guide) → email capture → nurture. Pinterest converts on the second touch, not the click.
- Measure: saves and outbound clicks early; leads and cost per lead after the nurture kicks in.
