# Google Business, Local Service Ads & Listings Playbook — Owning the Map Pack

## Google Business Profile (GBP) optimization

The map pack (top 3 local results) drives more local service calls than anything else on Google. Checklist:
1. **Category precision**: primary category = your highest-value service (e.g., "Garage door supplier" vs generic "Contractor"). Add every legitimate secondary category.
2. **Complete every field**: services with descriptions and prices where possible, service areas, hours (including holiday hours), attributes, opening date.
3. **Photos weekly**: real job photos, team, trucks, before/after. Profiles with fresh photos get materially more calls. Add the 360 tour.
4. **Google Posts** 1–2x/week: offers, recent jobs, seasonal reminders. Treat like a mini social channel.
5. **Q&A**: seed it yourself with the 10 questions customers actually ask, answer them, upvote.
6. **Reviews**: see the reviews playbook — velocity, recency, and keyword-rich review text all move map rankings.
7. **NAP consistency**: name, address, phone identical everywhere (see Listings below).

Ranking factors in practice: proximity (can't control), relevance (categories + services + on-site signals), prominence (reviews + citations + links). Attack relevance and prominence hard.

## Local Service Ads (LSA / Google Guaranteed)

LSAs sit above regular ads with a "Google Guaranteed" badge and charge per lead, not per click.
- **Setup**: pass background/license/insurance verification early — approval takes weeks in some trades.
- **Dispute bad leads**: wrong service, out of area, spam — dispute within the window; recovered budget is free money.
- **Ranking inside LSA**: responsiveness matters most. Answer calls; missed calls tank your ad rank. Review count and rating are the other lever.
- **Budget**: start with a weekly lead target, not a dollar cap. Measure cost per booked job, not cost per lead.
- LSAs complement — never replace — SEO and Google Ads: they cap out on volume and you don't own the asset.

## Local listings & citations (NAP)

- **Audit first**: search your business name + phone; log every directory with wrong name, old address, or dead phone. Inconsistent NAP quietly kills map rankings and confuses AI engines.
- **Core set**: Google, Bing Places, Apple Maps, Yelp, Facebook, BBB, industry directories (Angi, Houzz, Healthgrades, etc. by vertical), and local chamber listings.
- **One source of truth**: exact legal name, one phone number (tracking numbers only where the platform supports it properly), one address format. Fix everything to match.
- Recheck quarterly — directories drift.
