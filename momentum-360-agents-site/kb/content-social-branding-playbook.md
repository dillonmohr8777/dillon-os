# Content Engine, Social Studio & Branding Playbook

## Content Engine — one idea becomes everything

The multiplication system: one strong idea, captured once, published everywhere.
1. **Source the idea**: the questions customers ask on sales calls are the content calendar. One question = one content unit.
2. **Capture the core**: a 3–5 minute owner video answering the question (phone camera is fine), or a 500-word dictated answer.
3. **Multiply into**:
   - 1 blog post (answer-first TL;DR, question H2s, FAQ schema) → SEO + AEO
   - 3–5 vertical clips (each one sub-point, hooked in 2s) → Reels/Shorts/TikTok
   - 1 carousel (the answer in 6–8 slides) → Instagram/LinkedIn/Facebook
   - 1 email to the list (the answer + soft CTA)
   - 3 Google Business Posts (tip framing, offer framing, proof framing)
4. **Cadence**: one idea per week sustained beats five ideas one week and silence after. A month of content = 4 ideas, fully multiplied.

## Social Studio — a month of posts in one session

The monthly mix for a local business (12–20 posts):
- 40% proof: job photos, before/after, reviews-as-graphics, team at work
- 25% education: tips, mistakes-to-avoid, cost explainers, FAQs
- 20% human: team intros, behind the scenes, local community
- 15% offer: seasonal promos, openings in the schedule, referral asks
Rules: captions front-load the point in the first line; every post has one job; local tags and city references on everything; video where possible. Batch-create in one sitting, schedule the month, spend daily time only on replies.

## Branding — identity & voice

A local service brand needs five decided things (write them down, apply everywhere):
1. **Positioning line**: who you serve + what outcome + what's different. Formula: "We help [who] get [outcome] without [pain]."
2. **Voice**: 3 adjectives + 3 "we never sound like" words. (e.g., straight-talking, warm, expert; never corporate, hypey, vague.)
3. **Visual kit**: one logo (+ clear-space rules), 2 brand colors + 1 accent, 2 fonts, photo style (real jobs > stock, always).
4. **Message hierarchy**: the one thing every touchpoint says first (usually the guarantee or the speed promise), then supporting proof.
5. **Consistency system**: templates for posts, proposals, review responses, and email signatures so every team member ships on-brand without asking.

Brand isn't the logo — it's the promise kept repeatedly. The visual kit just makes the repetition recognizable.
