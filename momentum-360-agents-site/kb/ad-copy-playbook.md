# Ad Copywriting Playbook — Copy That Converts

## The structure that works

Every winning direct-response ad has four beats:
1. **Hook** — stop the scroll with the reader's problem or a pattern interrupt. Never the company name.
2. **Agitate/empathize** — one or two lines proving you understand the situation.
3. **Offer + proof** — the concrete offer, plus one trust element (review count, guarantee, badge, years).
4. **CTA** — one action, stated plainly, with urgency that's real ("this month", "first 10 callers"), never fake.

## Hooks that work for local service

- Question hooks: "Garage door won't close all the way?"
- Cost hooks: "What a water heater replacement actually costs in [city] (2026)"
- Mistake hooks: "3 things roofers hope you don't check before signing"
- Speed/pain hooks: "AC died on the hottest week of the year?"
- Social proof hooks: "Why 400+ [city] homeowners called us last year"

## Rules of the Momentum 360 ad-writing style

- Write at a 6th–8th grade reading level. Short sentences. No jargon.
- One idea per ad. One offer per ad. One CTA per ad.
- Specifics beat superlatives: "answered in 90 seconds" beats "amazing service".
- Localize: city name in the first two lines lifts response for local service.
- Price anchoring: if you can't state price, state the range or the starting point — "from $X" outperforms silence.
- Compliance: no unverifiable claims ("#1", "best" need substantiation), no before/after health claims for medical, respect Meta's personal-attribute rules (never "Are you struggling with debt?" phrasing that names the reader's condition).

## Formats

- **Search ads (Google)**: put the keyword in headline 1, the differentiator in headline 2, the offer in headline 3. Description = proof + CTA. Pin only when compliance requires.
- **Meta primary text**: hook line, line break, 2–3 short lines, CTA. First 125 characters carry the ad.
- **Landing page headline**: mirror the ad's promise exactly (message match) — mismatch is the most common conversion leak.

## Testing copy

Test hooks before anything else — the hook is 70% of performance. Keep a swipe file of every hook that beat control, tagged by vertical.
