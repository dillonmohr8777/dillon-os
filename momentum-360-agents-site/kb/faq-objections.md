# FAQ & Objections — How Momentum 360 Answers Common Questions

## "SEO takes too long."

SEO builds a long-term asset while paid ads generate immediate opportunities. Run together, ads buy time while SEO compounds: expect ranking movement in 60–90 days and meaningful organic lead flow in 4–6 months. The Vista River Hospice campaign doubled revenue over the engagement — the curve bends, then compounds.

## "We've tried marketing before and it didn't work."

Most failed campaigns were single-channel: just ads, or just SEO, or just posting. The system approach combines organic search, AI search, paid ads, and a conversion-focused website so each channel reinforces the others — retargeting catches organic visitors, review velocity lifts map rankings, content feeds both Google and AI engines.

Exactly why. Buyers ask ChatGPT, Gemini, and Perplexity who to hire, and those engines cite structured, consistent, well-reviewed businesses.
