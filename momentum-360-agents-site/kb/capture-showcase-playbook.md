# Capture & Showcase Playbook — Tours, Photos, Drone, Video, Podcast

## 360 Virtual Tour planning

A 360 virtual tour is the strongest trust asset a location-based business can publish. Playbook:
1. **Define the goal**: Google Business Profile embed (local SEO + map pack trust), website hero (conversion), or sales tool (send before estimates).
2. **Shot plan**: entrance first — buyers decide in the first three panoramas. Map 8–20 scenes: entry, main service/floor areas, standout details, staff/team moment. One scene per 200–400 sq ft is a good density.
3. **Prep the space**: declutter, full lighting on, hide personal items, stage one "wow" detail per scene.
4. **Publish**: embed on the site (fast-loading iframe below the fold), link from GBP, cut 15–30s walkthrough clips from it for social.
5. **Measure**: GBP views, time-on-page for the tour page, and "saw the tour" mentions in lead calls.

Google Street View-connected tours also feed Google's understanding of the business — a local SEO signal most competitors skip.

## Listing photos that sell

- Shoot wide but honest: 24mm-equivalent, corners of rooms, verticals straight.
- Light is the product: schedule for daylight, turn on every interior light, HDR-bracket dark rooms.
- Order matters: best exterior first, then the money rooms (kitchen/main service area), then supporting shots. 20–35 images beats 60 mediocre ones.
- Every listing needs: 1 hero exterior, 1 lifestyle detail, 1 team/human shot if relevant.
- Enhance, don't fake: sky replacement and lawn greening are accepted; adding features that don't exist kills trust and can be a legal problem.

## Drone & aerial

Use aerial when location, scale, or context sells: large properties, roofing (before/after), commercial lots, proximity to landmarks. Rules of thumb:
- FAA Part 107 pilot required for commercial work in the US; check airspace (LAANC) before promising a shoot.
- Golden-hour orbits and slow reveal push-ins outperform fast fly-throughs.
- Deliver stills + 3 short clips (10–20s) cut for vertical social along with the horizontal master.

## Property & business video

The workhorse formats:
1. **Walkthrough** (60–120s horizontal): steadicam/gimbal, one continuous feel, captions for sound-off viewing.
2. **Reels/Shorts** (15–30s vertical): hook in the first 2 seconds (motion or a question), one idea per clip, end with a soft CTA.
3. **Testimonial** (45–90s): customer states the problem, the fix, the result — in that order. B-roll over talking head.
A single shoot should be planned to yield all three. Shot list before shoot day, always.

## Podcast studio

Podcasting for a local business is a trust and SEO play, not a fame play:
- Format: 20–30 minute owner-hosted episodes answering the exact questions customers ask on sales calls.
- Each episode yields: full episode (YouTube + audio platforms), 3–5 vertical clips, 1 blog post transcript (SEO), 1 email.
- Cadence beats polish: biweekly consistent beats weekly abandoned. Batch-record 2–3 episodes per session.
- Gear floor: one good USB/XLR mic per speaker, treated quiet room, 1080p camera. Don't let gear delay episode one.
