Constraints for any generation:

- No green anywhere in palette, gradients, iconography, or imagery
- Confident, direct, operator voice. Never salesy, never "guru"

## 3. Voice and Tone

Confident not arrogant. Direct not aggressive. Smart not academic. Conversational not casual. Authoritative but approachable. Opinionated without preaching.

**Use these words:** systems, infrastructure, compound, leverage, engine, machine, build, implement, optimize, diagnostic, performance, growth, operator, systematic, measurable, scalable, AI-powered.

**Never use:** guru, hack, secret, crush it, game-changer, disrupt, synergy, revolutionize, unlock, skyrocket, ninja, rockstar, hustle, grind.

1. **Systems thinking.** Marketing should be infrastructure, not random acts.
2. **AI leverage.** Modern tools used by operators, not just talked about by consultants.
3. **Operator credibility.** Built by someone who runs real campaigns every day.
4. **Clear ROI.** Every dollar spent should be traceable to a result.
5. **Speed and efficiency.** Deliver in weeks what agencies take months to produce.

**Differentiation lines (ok to quote in copy):**
- "We build systems, not slide decks."
- "Managed by operators, not account managers who have never run a campaign."
- "AI-powered delivery means agency-quality work at a fraction of the cost and timeline."
- "Fixed scope. Clear deliverables. Measurable results."
- "We do not sell hours. We sell outcomes."

---

## 5. The Mohr Method (framework)

Four stages. Always ordered. Always named this way.

1. **Map.** Audit the full funnel. Find the leak before anything moves.
2. **Optimize.** Tighten what works. Pages, copy, offers, targeting, tracking.
3. **Harness.** Layer AI into the system. Content, scoring, reporting, workflows.
4. **Run.** Launch, measure, compound. Insights feed the next campaign.

## 7. Design System

**Palette (exact hex, do not substitute):**
- `#0B1F3A` Navy (primary)
- `#2F6BFF` Electric (CTA, accent)
- `#F5F7FA` Fog (background)
- `#1E293B` Ink (text)
- `#E6EEFF` Mist (light accent)

**Typography:** Inter (sans). Display sizes 48 to 72px, body 16 to 18px, eyebrows 11 to 12px uppercase with 0.18 to 0.25em tracking.

**Feel:** Premium SaaS. Funded startup. Not a templated agency site. Strong whitespace. Rounded 2xl cards. Subtle grid and radial-glow backgrounds. Motion is tasteful: fade and slide on scroll, hover-lift on cards, pulse-glow on primary CTA, floating gradient blobs.

**Stack in use:** Next.js 15 App Router, React 19, TypeScript, Tailwind 3, Framer Motion 11, Lucide icons.

## 11. Codex Build Instructions (copy-paste-ready)

When generating pages, components, or copy for Mohr Media:

1. Use the exact palette in Section 7. Never substitute hues. Never introduce green.
2. Use Inter from `next/font/google` as `--font-inter`.
3. Use the Section 6 homepage order. Do not reorder.
4. Quote copy from Sections 1, 4, 5 verbatim when it already exists. Do not invent new taglines.
5. Cite only the clients listed in Section 10. Do not invent logos or metrics.
6. Avoid every word in Section 3 "Never use" list.
7. Never use em dashes. Use periods, commas, or colons.
8. Every section needs a Framer Motion fade-up on scroll and at least one hover-lift or gradient motion for premium feel.
9. Primary CTA is always "Book a Strategy Call". Secondary is "See Results".
10. The Mohr Method is always "Map, Optimize, Harness, Run" in that order.
