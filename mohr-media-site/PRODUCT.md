## Users

The primary user is a prospective high-value client evaluating whether Dillon Mohr can design and operate a complete growth system across brand, web, motion, content, reporting, CRM, automation, and performance marketing. Secondary users are hiring managers, creative collaborators, and partners evaluating Dillon's range, judgment, and ability to finish ambitious work.

## Product Purpose

This site is Dillon Mohr's definitive interactive professional portfolio. It must make the work itself impossible to overlook, establish Dillon as the operator behind the systems, and give visitors direct paths to inspect live work, download selected PDFs, watch edited videos, and contact Dillon.

Success means a visitor quickly understands the breadth and depth of Dillon's work, can verify meaningful examples without hunting, remembers the IMMOHRTAL mark, and has a clear next action.

## Positioning

Dillon is not presenting a gallery of disconnected mockups. He is showing a working body of full-stack marketing systems: strategy, websites, motion, client creative, reporting, research, CRM, automation, and measurable operating infrastructure built by one accountable operator.

Align HCM is the flagship case study because it carries the broadest verified body of industry-solution work, including web experiences, animated visuals, content systems, reports, PDFs, event material, dashboards, and lead intelligence.

## Operating Context

The portfolio is evaluated on phones and desktops, often by a time-constrained decision maker arriving from a direct link, résumé, repository, social profile, or referral. Visitors need a fast overview and optional depth rather than a forced linear presentation.

The source inventory spans local project files, authenticated GitHub repositories, live public websites, client logos, screenshots, downloadable documents, and edited video. Repository access is an evidence source, not permission to publish confidential client material.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Preserve functional navigation, contact links, canonical URLs, public project hyperlinks, accessibility semantics, analytics or tracking, and any legal or privacy material carried forward from the live Mohr Media site.
- Support real downloadable PDFs and embedded edited video with poster frames, captions where available, playback controls, and non-autoplay fallbacks.
- Use exact public project URLs and verified facts. Do not invent metrics, testimonials, client approval, publication status, or outcomes.
- Use only public-safe client evidence. Private material may inform selection but must not be exposed without a later explicit approval or a verified redacted artifact.
- The experience must remain complete with JavaScript disabled where practical and with reduced motion enabled. Keyboard access, visible focus, contrast, mobile overflow, loading, media failure, and performance are required completion checks.
- Resolve the exact repository, branch, existing Netlify site, and production destination before publication. A local build is not evidence of a live launch.
- Do not use emojis in visible interface copy.

## Product Principles

1. Let finished work lead; explanation supports the artifact instead of replacing it.
2. Make breadth legible through one flagship story and a deliberately edited supporting collection.
3. Every spectacular interaction must preserve comprehension, access, and performance.
4. Use public, verifiable evidence and label limitations rather than manufacturing proof.
5. Make Dillon's authorship unmistakable from the first viewport through the final contact action.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for the public web experience. Motion has a complete reduced-motion equivalent. Every pointer interaction has a keyboard and touch equivalent. Video remains controllable, text remains readable over visual effects, and no essential information is encoded only by color, hover, sound, or animation.
