How is this different from hiring an agency?
The work is scoped around infrastructure, not vague activity. You're paying for a system that compounds, not retainer hours. Everything gets documented and installed in your accounts, so you're never hostage to your marketing vendor.
Does this work for small businesses?
Yes. The strongest fit is a business that knows marketing matters but hasn't yet built the operational layer. The audit tells you exactly where you stand before you spend anything on a build.
What does "AI-powered" actually mean here?
It means AI is used where it creates leverage: content workflows, reporting, enrichment, analysis, and follow-up speed. It's implementation, not buzzwords, and an operator runs every agent.
What if I only need one thing fixed?
That's why the audit and strategy call exist. Start with a defined diagnostic, get clarity fast, and decide what to build next. Plenty of engagements stop at one system.
How long does an engagement last?
The diagnostic is one 90-minute session plus a written plan. A build runs 4 to 12 weeks. The retainer is monthly, and it earns its renewal with reported numbers.
