**Date:** 2026-07-17 · **Window:** Jan 26 2026 (HubSpot site launch) → today · **Sources:** live crawl of all 118 sitemap URLs, HubSpot content analytics + CRM + revenue attribution, live SERP probes (WebSearch backend; no Google Analytics / Search Console involved), domain records.

## 1. Executive summary

- **Infrastructure score: 49/100.** The site is *fast* (avg TTFB 89ms — HubSpot CDN is doing its job) and has zero availability problems, but it is carrying heavy **migration debt**: duplicate case-study clusters, redirecting sitemap entries, a UUID clone of the homepage, and 14 broken internal links (including your case-study CTAs — a direct conversion leak).
- **The site converts, the blog doesn't (yet).** 127 web-sourced contacts since launch → 7 opportunities → 3 customers. Contact form + Maher's meeting link do the heavy lifting. The 40+ blog posts drove ~1/3 of all views but **~1 direct lead** (blogs bounce at 93–100%, several with dead CTAs).
- **You're invisible on money keywords.** #1 for your brand and #1 for "strategic buyer's guide to Workday" (a phrase you coined), but **absent from top-10** for: hcm implementation services, workday implementation partner, ukg implementation consultant, dayforce implementation consultant, hcm post go-live support, workday post go-live optimization. HRchitect appears in 4 of those 6 SERPs.
- **Authority is the bottleneck, and it's buildable.** Domain is 6.7 years old with a strong entity footprint (UKG Marketplace, Dayforce Exchange, Raven Intel, Great Place to Work, LinkedIn) but competitors are stacked in aggregator/listicle results (Clutch, "Top 11 UKG consulting firms") where Align doesn't appear at all.
- **Organic search has influenced just $19.6K of closed-won revenue vs $225K from direct traffic** — the upside of fixing this is quantified below.

- `/site-watchdog` skill — one command: crawl + HubSpot analytics + lead deltas → brief in `Daily-Briefs/`.
- Daily routine — runs the skill every morning; only new/changed findings surface, so it stays quiet when the site is healthy.
