# AI search retainer pricing and Philadelphia position

**Summary:** Four figures anchor the AI division's pricing decision. All four come
from a scan, not from primary sources. They are good enough to price against and
**not** good enough to quote to a client.

## Verification status — read this before using a number

`verification_status: unverified`. These figures were produced by a scan in the AI
division session. In this pass no vendor pricing page was fetched, no primary
source was read, and no figure was independently confirmed. Treat every line as a
**planning anchor with a verification gate**, per
[[12_Brain/09_Ops/AGENT_PROTOCOL|Agent Protocol]]: missing evidence stays missing.

Before any of this appears in a deck, a proposal, or a sentence said to a buyer,
fetch the primary source and re-record it here with the URL and fetch date.

## The four findings

| # | Finding | Use |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | AEO retainers in the SMB tier cluster at **$1,500–3,000/month** entry, **$3,000–8,000/month** for active work | Price band |
| 2 | **WebFX (Harrisburg)** is the only Pennsylvania agency found publishing an AI-search price: **$3,000/month** | The only in-state anchor |
| 3 | **ChatGPT Ads went self-serve** for all US businesses on **2026-05-05** — cost-per-click, no minimum | Barrier-to-entry change |
| 4 | **No Philadelphia SMB agency has launched a named "AI division"** | The positioning |

## What each one means for the plan

**(1) and (2) together say the AI Visibility Program is priced at the floor.**
The plan's $1,500 setup / $1,500 monthly sits at the bottom of the scanned entry
band and 50% under the only published Pennsylvania number. That is defensible as a
founding-cohort rate and indefensible as a standing rate. The decision — which one
it is — belongs to Mac, and it should be made deliberately rather than by drift.
Drafted resolution in the plan extension: $1,500/month for the first three
clients, $2,500/month after three references exist.

**(3) removes a moat Momentum did not own anyway.** ChatGPT Ads with no minimum
means anyone can run them. The existing signed $500/month management lane is a
real price anchor; the specialist positioning around it is gone.

**(4) is the positioning line, not a footnote.** "Philadelphia's first named AI
division" is a claim with a shelf life. It is worth something only while it is
true, and its truth is exactly what item 4 asserts on unverified evidence — which
makes it the **highest-priority verification** of the four. A first-mover claim
that turns out to be false is worse than no claim.
