# Google Ads Search CTR benchmarks (WordStream 2026)

WordStream / LocaliQ 2026 Search averages, from 13,474 US campaigns running April 2025 through March 2026. Medians, not means.

| Slice | CTR | CPC |
|---|---:|---:|
| All industries | 6.64% | $5.42 |
| Automotive Repair, Service & Parts | 5.56% | $4.35 |
| Automotive For Sale | 8.28% | $2.27 |
| Personal Services | 7.16% | $7.17 |

All-industry conversion rate is 8.18%. Cost per lead is $66.69. Auto repair/service conversion rate is 15.51% and cost per lead is $29.96.

Tags 2 Go is not a named WordStream category. Auto tag / title / registration shops map closest to Automotive Repair, Service & Parts, not Automotive For Sale.

Used in the 2026-08-19 Silver draft (and the earlier plain-signature send): Tags Search-1 10.15% CTR and about $0.68 CPC versus 6.64% / $5.42 overall and 5.56% / $4.35 auto service.
