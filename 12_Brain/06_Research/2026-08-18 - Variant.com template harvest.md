# Variant.com template harvest

## Conclusion

Variant stays **outside the factory**. Dillon confirmed the six factory-native
briefs are the main templates we use. They now live in
`_templates/site-factory/starters/`. Variant remains an optional ideation feed
with account-gated prompts. Public cards are app and media widgets, not local
service landing pages.

## What was verified

| Check | Result |
|---|---|
| TikTok target | `variant.com`, video `7674018659425176853` |
| Public homepage | Live. Tagline: endless designs, just scroll |
| Public prompt box | Present. Submit opens sign-in |
| Surprise me | Sign-in wall |
| `/community` `/explore` `/gallery` `/pricing` | Sign-in wall |
| HTML/React export without account | Not visible |
| Public card types | Weather, EV charge, gauge, assistant, recorder, editor, music, video |

Public sample cards skew product-UI, not HVAC, bridal, landscape, or other
local-service landing pages. Fast Company notes the eyedropper and also that
outputs can look flat and similar. Treat that as a third-party review, not a
live export test.

## Factory fit

The factory already has the production contract: one `index.html`, tokens,
attitude skins (`glass`, `editorial`, `brutal`, `warm`, `industrial`, `neon`),
JSON-LD, noindex demos, and QA. Variant does not replace that.

Safe later use, if a free account is approved:

1. Prompt a vertical (`HVAC emergency landing page`, `bridal fitting page`).
2. Screenshot 8 to 12 cards. Keep them as untrusted layout reference, same rule
   as LandingFolio: composition only, never brand truth.
3. If HTML export exists, diff it against `philly-sites/DESIGN-SYSTEM.md`. Keep
   only rhythm and density. Rebuild in the factory. Do not paste Variant markup
   into a client site.

Do not adopt now:

- Variant as the 25-site generator
- Shared Variant visual DNA across a batch
- Account, spend, or MCP wiring

These are now the starter library. They are not client sites and must not be
deployed.
