# Dillon operating goals and revenue-first delegation

## Why this matters

Dillon wants one user-facing operator that delegates work to agents without
requiring him to manage or converse with the specialist agents. The operating
system must prioritize measurable business outcomes over activity.

## Source material

Dillon described five connected outcomes:

1. A deeper agentic work system in which Marketing Chief acts as Dillon's
   interface, delegates to Buzz and specialist agents, verifies their work, and
   reports back in one place.
2. Revenue-first client growth, beginning with qualified landscaping,
   concrete, and bridal opportunities instead of platform activity or
   unqualified calls.
3. A complete growth system for *The Ironic Ineptocracy*: positioning,
   newsletter, video-game packaging, AEO/GEO/SEO, organic discovery,
   analytics, and download conversion.
4. A high-craft automated website factory: unique visual identity per site,
   uncropped imagery, polished motion and scroll behavior, excellent mobile
   behavior, very low defect rates, and the complete outreach and mailing
   pipeline requested by Mac.
5. An owned-audience and measurement system for the Ironic and IMMOHRTAL
   ventures, using current source-linked research and day-to-day analytics to
   grow qualified visits, retention, and downloads.
