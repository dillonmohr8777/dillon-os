# Erie and Pittsburgh outreach campaign result

**Summary:** 241 cold emails were sent to Erie and Pittsburgh prospects across
two waves on 2026-09-02, reconciled against live Gmail SENT on 2026-09-03. The
campaign produced **zero verified human replies.** It is complete and closed;
nothing further is authorized.

Read directly from `status.json` on 2026-09-09, not from a summary.

Source of truth is recorded as "live Gmail SENT reconciliation." The record
carries its own honest disclaimer: "SENT and the absence of an explicit bounce
do not prove inbox delivery."

## The list behind it

2,066 unique official sites harvested, 870 priority owner-operated, 32 in the
first review batch. Markets Erie and Pittsburgh; `erieHometown: true`. Brand
pages offered were `needmomentum.com` and `momentumvirtualtours.com`.

## Data-integrity gap, recorded not hidden

Wave 1 is only partially evidenced locally: 41 planned messages, 7 local receipt
entries, **34 missing**, and 21 subjects that differ from the local plan. Wave 2
reconciles exactly — 200 planned, 200 receipts, 200 exact recipient/subject
matches. The record explicitly asserts `missingMessageIdsFabricated: false`.

So wave 2 is trustworthy evidence; wave 1 is not fully reconstructable from
local files.

## What this closes

`status.json` states the stop condition directly: "No additional send or
follow-up is authorized by this record. Before any future action, review current
replies and suppression state and obtain approval for the exact preview."

The alignment document notes the sequencing problem plainly — the market check
happened *after* the campaign was designed around Erie.

**The finding worth keeping: 241 sends into a self-built 2,066-site list
returned nothing. Any future cold-outreach plan should be argued against this
number, not against the size of the list.**
