# 2. High-signal findings
**Empirical / platform / datasets**
- **Zero-click & click composition (iPullRank/Datos, published ~30 Jul 2026)**: 13.1B events / 10.2B sessions / 9.1M users (Oct 2024–Dec 2025). Session zero-click rate 46.96%. Rising slowly (~+2.6 pts over period), mostly at expense of organic. ~86% of clicks are discovery (searcher did not name the destination); only ~14% navigational/branded. Top destinations include YouTube, Reddit, Wikipedia, ChatGPT (#6 overall); Google intercepts higher share of would-be Wikipedia/IMDB traffic than Reddit/Amazon/GitHub. ChatGPT shows unusually high paid-click share among top destinations. Single-event shallow sessions dominate (~76%).[1](https://ipullrank.com/datos-clickstream-data)[3](https://x.com/iPullRank/status/2082817737991090243)
- **GSC platform properties (Google, initial 7 Jul; Aleyda notes global 29 Jul)**: New property type to verify and track Instagram, TikTok, X, YouTube posts/performance (clicks, impressions, queries, insights, achievements) in Search, Discover, News. Explicit recognition that brand value extends beyond owned site traffic amid AIOs/features/ads.[4](https://developers.google.com/search/blog/2026/07/search-console-social-video-platforms)[2](https://x.com/aleyda/status/2082548747213426842)[2](https://x.com/aleyda/status/2082548747213426842)
- **AI Mode UX (iPullRank + Farrah Bostic)**: Users treat AI Mode as research companion; many still default to classic Search, Reddit, or ChatGPT. Frustrations include missing maps/carousels/trusted publishers; generic lists feel redundant with AI Overviews. Early referral data (prior but referenced): external clicks low (~<3%).[5](https://ipullrank.com/resources/webinars/ai-mode-ux-study-replay)[6](https://x.com/iPullRank/status/2082498095691235661)
- **LLM citation patterns (Aleyda citing Semrush Enterprise AI Visibility Index)**: Category-dependent—some verticals cite brand-owned sources heavily (e.g., consumer electronics/Apple ~60% on ChatGPT); others pure “referees” (finance: NerdWallet/Investopedia, zero top brand sites); news leans heavily Reddit. Optimize distribution accordingly.[7](https://x.com/aleyda/status/2082418441508520417)
- **Lead quality (Ross Hudgens / Siege + cited client data)**: ChatGPT leads landing on product pages show worst close rates among sources >$1M pipeline; one cited analysis showed LLM average deal value 37x lower than organic. Mixed bag overall—volume high, conversion/quality often lower; treat as top-of-funnel.[8](https://x.com/RossHudgens/status/2082126181063917716)
- **LLM traffic trends (Lily Ray + Similarweb desktop)**: Visualized growth; Claude/Gemini strong early 2026 gains, ChatGPT slight decline Mar–Jun. AI Overviews prevalence layered in. Warning: ChatGPT increasingly cites spammy pages via web retrieval; strategies must not harm Google AIO/organic.[9](https://x.com/lilyraynyc/status/2081799123116740842)

# 3. Consumer taste and demographic signals
- Shallow, single-query sessions dominate; users often satisfied by SERP features/AI summaries.
- Preference for “real people” signals (Reddit threads hard for Google/AI to fully replace) and trusted publishers/maps over generic AI lists.
- Multi-tool usage: AI Mode not yet “ride or die”; classic Google + Reddit + ChatGPT remain defaults.
- Younger/ heavier AI users and certain geos/devices show higher zero-click variance (panel segmented but not fully detailed publicly here).
- Social/video content increasingly discovered via Google Search/Discover—creators without sites now measurable.
- Local/service implications: Maps, reviews, real images, community (Reddit/local forums) retain click and citation power; pure info queries most zero-clicked.

# 5. Website factory, design, AEO, and retention implications
For Dillon OS / 25-site factory / local-service SMB:
- **Maps-to-site parity + real-image trust**: Critical. AI Mode users want maps/carousels; Google intercepts less for hard-to-summarize local/experience content. Use authentic photos, consistent NAP/entities across GBP + site + social.
- **Source-ready FAQs**: Direct answer in first 1–2 sentences + facts/numbers/sources co-located + clean schema. Supports both classic featured snippets/AIOs and LLM extraction. Category-aware (own brand vs referee strategy).
- **Multi-channel entity**: Verify social/YouTube in new GSC properties. Optimize posts for Search/Discover queries. Brand mentions on Reddit/Wikipedia/reviews compound LLM citations.
- **Zero-click / third-visit retention**: Traffic scarcer; design for brand demand, community visibility, email/SMS, and on-site conversion UX that captures value even from AI-referred short sessions. Prospect discovery via SparkToro-style affinity + local reviews.
- **AEO/GEO ↔ SEO convergence**: Same foundations (technical health, E-E-A-T proxies, structured data, internal links) plus distribution (PR, Reddit, social, YouTube) and measurement of citations/mentions/business impact. Distinct: pure LLM prompt optimization or spammy retrieval hacks risk Google demotion.
- **Design**: Trust signals (real images, reviews, clear service areas) over stock; fast, mobile, accessible for any residual clicks or AI-cited pages.
