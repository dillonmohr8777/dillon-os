# CMO board should match Momentum agents look

## Why this matters

The operator asked how the CMO front end looks and asked for a gorgeous app:
platform icons, cute agentic icons, and the look and feel of the Momentum 360
19-agents board.

## Source material

Operator request during the CMO lane goal thread. Visual reference is the
Momentum 360 agents site (navy `#14274E` / gold `#E1A93B`, Montserrat + Inter,
glass panels, constellation of 19 nodes). That site uses squad pills, not
per-agent character art. Cute mascots were generated for this operator board
as original navy/gold robots, not as copies of a Momentum character set that
does not exist.

or official platform logos. Marks on the board are stylized originals.
