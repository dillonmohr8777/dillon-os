## Making a 93,000-unread inbox workable

Do not read it. Do not archive it. Declare 2026-09-04 as bankruptcy day and build forward from here.

**Step 1 — One rule that does most of the work.** Everything from a client domain skips the noise and lands in one place:

```
from:(nexla.com OR driveshack.com OR highlinecomedy.com OR vaclaimsedge.com
  OR kimberlyjamesbridal.com OR getreplenish.com OR freshblends.com
  OR barcrawlusa.com OR nkcdc.org OR boklawfirm.com OR resy.com
  OR omegalandscapingandconcrete.com OR thehopewellnesscenter.com
  OR getonthebridge0@gmail.com OR jkauffman@kw.com OR mjover09@gmail.com
  OR onsiteclp@gmail.com OR faganpainting@gmail.com
  OR greenslatemasonry@gmail.com OR info@tags2go.pro)
→ Apply "Clients", Never send to Spam, Always mark important
```

That single filter would have surfaced every one of the twelve waiting clients above. Build it before anything else.

**Step 2 — Rebuild the Security labels around message type, not sender.** The current rules are the wrong shape.

```
subject:(receipt OR declined OR disabled OR suspended OR "past due"
  OR "payment failed" OR "action required" OR "minimum payment" OR invoice)
  -from:notes@e.pnc.com -from:team@info.digitalocean.com
→ Apply "Money", Always mark important
```

Add Discover, LinkedIn, Cloudflare, Netlify, Zoho, and Apple to the watched set. Excluding those two marketing streams alone removes ~30 messages of noise and pulls in the Discover and LinkedIn exposure the current rules cannot see.

**Step 3 — Kill the four dead labels or wire them.** Empty labels are worse than no labels; they read as coverage that does not exist. Either delete Client Triage, Client Triage/Drafted, and Conversation History, or build the filters. For KJB Daily Leads the fix is upstream: **reconfigure the Zap to put lead name, email, phone, and campaign into the email body** instead of a "open this in Zapier" stub. That one change turns the match-back from an archaeology project into a forward, and it is the same change that makes the Omega promise deliverable.

**Step 4 — The drafts folder needs a rule you enforce, not a filter.** No mechanism in Gmail will fix 86 unsent client deliverables. The generation step works; the send step is where it dies. Either send at generation time or stop generating. A weekly report that is drafted and not sent is worse than no report — it produces the internal feeling of having reported.

**Step 5 — Sweep, don't read.** Select everything older than 2026-06-01 and mark as read in one action. It is 90,000 newsletters. The signal is in the twelve rows of the waiting table above, and you now have all of it.
