# 2026-08-14 research — PennDOT agent directories do not publish websites

Receipt for a written PennDOT answer that public agent listings include name, address, and phone only. Compiled into [[12_Brain/concepts/Google Ads Government Documents Certification]].

## Question

Can PennDOT add an official website to a public authorized-agent listing, or publish another official page that links an agent’s domain?

## Receipts

1. **Claim:** Dealer Agent Services has no business-URL field. Contract Administration does not collect that information and does not advertise websites on the public agent list. The published list is address and phone only.
   - Source: PennDOT Bureau of Support Services, Budget Administration and Contracts Division, written reply 2026-08-14 on the Contract Administration / DAS thread about agent 18005397 (Gmail thread `1a001d851cf3c721`).
   - Quote (redacted of staff contact details): “As advised by the DAS helpdesk there is no field for your business URL. We do not need that information for your application so, we do not have a field for that information. We do not advertise that information on your list of agents. We only list the address and phone number.”
   - Matches the live PDFs checked the same day: [[12_Brain/raw/research/2026-08-14 - research - google-ads-govdocs-penndot-agents]].

## Killed claims

- PennDOT Contract Administration or DAS can add `tags2go.pro` (or any agent website) to the public directory. Contradicted by the 2026-08-14 written reply.
- A further PennDOT inbox will create the Google-required government hyperlink. Agent Support already declined; Contract Administration / DAS now declined. Do not keep asking PennDOT units for a URL field.
