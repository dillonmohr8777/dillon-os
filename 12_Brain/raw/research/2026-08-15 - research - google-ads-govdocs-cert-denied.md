# 2026-08-15 research — Google gov-docs cert denied; directory still lists the agent

Receipts for the 2026-08-15 denial and a same-day recheck of the live PennDOT directories. Compiled into [[12_Brain/concepts/Google Ads Government Documents Certification]].

## Question

After Google denied the authorized-provider filing, is there a government page that now links the advertiser domain, and what did Google actually ask for next?

## Receipts

1. **Claim:** Google denied the authorized, non-government provider request for the Tags 2 Go Ads account and the submitted domain. The reason is the standard eligibility template. Google asked the advertiser to resubmit the form and include the link directly to the page that shows eligibility.
   - Source: Gmail thread `1a003de0cabae88e` from ads-support, 2026-08-15 05:21 UTC, case `1-8585000041572`.
   - Policy cited in that email: [Government documents and services](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/13156083) and the apply step at the same page.

2. **Claim:** The live PennDOT Agent Services directory still lists `TAGS 2 GO LLC` at `6001 TORRESDALE AVE`, Philadelphia. The directory has no website column.
   - Source: [Agent Services for Web](https://docs.penndot.pa.gov/Public/DVSPubsForms/Agent%20Services%20for%20Web.pdf) fetched 2026-08-15. PDF run date printed as 2026-08-07.

3. **Claim:** The live PennDOT online-agent directory also lists `TAGS 2 GO LLC` at the same street, city, and ZIP, and includes a phone number. Still no website column.
   - Source: [online_stations.pdf](https://docs.penndot.pa.gov/Public/DVSPubsForms/online_stations.pdf) fetched 2026-08-15.

4. **Claim:** The official government landing page for those PDFs is still [Authorized Agents and Messengers](https://www.pa.gov/agencies/dmv/resources/business-partners/authorized-agents-messengers). It describes authorized agents and links the PDFs. It does not hyperlink individual agent domains.
   - Source: page fetched 2026-08-15.

5. **Claim:** Starting 2026-10-05, Google will treat government-managed directories of accredited commercial partners as a valid authorization example, and will reject business-registry entries, trade licenses, commercial contracts, and government-hosted articles as substitutes.
   - Source: [October 2026 policy update](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/17260489) (posted 2026-08-04; enforcement 2026-10-05). Rechecked 2026-08-15.

6. **Claim:** Vehicle owner registration and license-plate ads targeting the United States are not on the regional-exclusion lists. The certification form’s exclusion picker does not offer a US tag-agency option.
   - Source: [policy list](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/answer/13156083) and [godos_certification](https://support.google.com/adspolicy/contact/godos_certification) fetched 2026-08-15.

## Killed claims

- A new government page now hyperlinks `tags2go.pro`. Contradicted by the 2026-08-15 pa.gov page and both PDFs.
- Filing as a regional or business exclusion will unlock Pennsylvania registration or plate ads. Contradicted by the live exclusion lists on the policy page and the form.
- Filing “out of scope” will unlock ads while the live homepage still promotes registration, titles, plates, and driver-license products. Contradicted by the policy’s destination review and by the earlier disapproval on this account.
- Vehicle titles are named as their own restricted category on the comprehensive list. The 2026-08-15 policy page lists vehicle owner registration and license plate number; it does not name vehicle titles as a separate row. That does not make title ads safe on a domain that also sells registration and plates. Do not use that gap to file out of scope.

## Open

- Whether a human reviewer will accept the government-managed partner PDFs (legal name + address, no domain hyperlink) plus PennDOT’s written “address and phone only” confirmation as authorized-provider proof after the template denial.
