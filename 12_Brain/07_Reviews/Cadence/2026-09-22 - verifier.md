## The one defect

**`production-briefs` has two ledger lines for one run.**

The first, `08:18:56`, records `"commit": "PENDING"` — a literal placeholder
written before the commit sha was known. The second, `08:19:08`, records the real
sha `83b9966a` and carries a note saying it corrects the line above it. Both
point at the same artifact with status `ok`.

This is a **field error, not a false success**: the artifact exists, is
non-empty, is fresh, and meets its gate, and the commit `83b9966a` is real and
contains that file. Nothing was claimed that is not true. But two rows for one
run will make any naive per-job count read 8 daily runs where there were 7, and
the first row's commit field is unusable.

It was left in place rather than edited, per the driver's rule that ledger lines
are never rewritten or pruned. Anything reading `run-ledger.jsonl` for today
should take the **last** entry per `(date, job)` pair.
