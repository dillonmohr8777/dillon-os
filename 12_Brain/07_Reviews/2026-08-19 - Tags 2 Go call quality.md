# Tags 2 Go | two counted calls were real conversations; 10% CTR is real and not scalable yet

The two Google Ads conversions are the only in-window calls that cleared the **60-second** `Calls from ads` threshold: **3 minutes 7 seconds** and **4 minutes 13 seconds**, both mobile click-to-call from the ad, 21 minutes apart on Aug 18 around 11am Eastern. Six other ad-calls happened and did **not** count (2s to 49s). That is a real conversation filter, not a 1-second pixel.

They are not yet proven jobs. Counting is `MANY_PER_CLICK`, so two long calls can be two customers or one person calling back. Ask the client about two ~11am Aug 18 calls. Do not invent close rates.

The ~10% CTR is not invalid-click theater (2 invalid clicks, 3.7%) and not insurance leakage (0 insurance clicks). Desktop and mobile CTR both sit near 10%. It is a tiny 5-mile exact/"near me" slice: the campaign only captures **17%** of eligible IS and loses **83%** to rank. When it shows, the query is already "auto tags near me" / "tag agency near me". That CTR will not survive a naive budget hike.

Quality score on the winning keyword `auto tags near me` is **1** (creative, landing page, and predicted CTR all below average) even with an 11% actual CTR. RSA is `APPROVED_LIMITED` for government documents. Fix ad/LP quality before raising the $15 cap.
