# Pritzker Law Group podcast sponsor landing page SEO-AEO-GEO plan

Fact-safe, attorney-approved, human-voiced sponsor landing page for Pritzker Law
Group — with keyword research, search-intent architecture, internal linking, and
AEO/GEO — all gated behind six open confirmations.

## 1. Summary

Plan the search, answer-engine, and conversion layer for Pritzker Law Group's
Love, Philadelphia — The Podcast sponsor landing page so it ships in the firm's
authentic voice, survives attorney review, and can be measured — with **no
execution until the open confirmations land**.

## 2. Context & constraints

- Momentum 360 client; Dillon's locked lane is a reviewable **sponsor landing
  page** for the firm's premium sponsorship of the named podcast. See
  [[01_Clients/Pritzker Law Group/overview]] and the
  [[01_Clients/Pritzker Law Group/Client Intelligence Overlay]] — client truth
  stays there; this note links to it and does not duplicate it.
- **Fact-safety guardrail (from the overlay):** do not invent guests, episodes,
  dates, listening platforms, audience size, outcomes, awards, testimonials,
  legal advice, or sponsor benefits. Preserve the firm disclaimer; avoid
  implying an attorney-client relationship. The official firm site and the
  supplied sponsor identity are the only factual sources.
- **Anti-AI directive (honest framing):** per
  [[12_Brain/01_Captures/Communications/2026-09-03 - Pritzker anti-AI stance and SEO directive]],
  the client-side decision-maker is reported as very anti-AI. The response is
  human-voiced drafting + attorney sign-off + high craft — genuine human-owned
  quality, not concealment. This attribute is **Dillon's reported directive, not
  independently verified against the client's own words.**
- **Gating:** execution (search work, publishing, instrumentation) begins only
  after the open confirmations in §10 are answered and attorney sign-off is in
  hand.

## 3. Operating model for an anti-AI client + a law firm

The anti-AI client and the law firm's fact-safety needs point at the same
practice:

- **Voice-capture profile.** Build the page copy from a real capture of the
  firm's authentic voice (intake call, prior firm writing, founder phrasing) so
  the draft reads as the firm, not as a generic template.
- **Attorney sign-off before publish.** Every claim, disclaimer, and CTA passes
  named attorney review before anything goes live. This is an approval gate, not
  a formality.
- **Fact-safe.** Only claims verifiable from the firm site or supplied sponsor
  identity ship. Everything else stays visibly draft-level.
- **Disclaimer preserved.** The firm disclaimer stays intact; nothing implies an
  attorney-client relationship.
- **High craft.** Held to a quality bar that makes it genuinely human-owned
  work.

Stated plainly: this is about producing authentic, high-quality, human-owned
work — **not** about deceiving the client or hiding tooling.

## 4. Keyword research plan

Method (per [[12_Brain/03_Concepts/Keyword Research and Search Demand]]): cluster
candidate queries by intent → validate volume/difficulty in a keyword tool → map
each survivor to a page or section. **All keywords below are candidates pending
tool validation. No search volumes are asserted here.**

Seed clusters:

- **(a) Branded / podcast intent** — e.g. "Love Philadelphia podcast", the
  podcast title + "sponsor", firm name + "podcast", firm name + partner name.
  These anchor the page to the sponsorship and the firm brand.
- **(b) Local legal intent** — e.g. Philadelphia + real estate attorney /
  zoning attorney / land-use attorney / development attorney /
  government-relations counsel. Tie only to the firm's **evidence-backed**
  practice areas; **mark every practice area as needs-confirmation against the
  official firm site** before targeting.
- **(c) Informational / answer intent** — question-form queries a prospect might
  ask (e.g. how zoning variances work in Philadelphia, what a land-use attorney
  does, how development approvals work locally). These feed the AEO/GEO Q&A
  blocks in §6.

Practice-area candidates trace to the public evidence summarized in the overlay
(Philadelphia-area real estate, development, government-relations, zoning,
land-use, public-property acquisition) and must be re-verified against the firm
site before use. A `/research-sweep` or WebSearch pass may surface real query
patterns, but everything remains a **candidate** until validated in a tool.

## 5. Search intent → page architecture + internal-linking map

Per [[12_Brain/03_Concepts/Search Intent and Topic Architecture]] (primary home
of internal-linking guidance):

Landing-page sections mapped to intent:

- **Hero / sponsor statement** → branded/podcast intent (cluster a). States the
  sponsorship in the firm's voice; disclaimer preserved.
- **Who Pritzker Law Group is** → local legal intent (cluster b), constrained to
  confirmed practice areas.
- **Answer blocks / FAQ** → informational intent (cluster c). Concise Q&A.
- **One CTA** → the single confirmed conversion goal (§9, §10).

Hub-and-spoke internal linking:

- The landing page is a **spoke** off the firm site hub. It links **out** to the
  firm's relevant practice pages using descriptive, non-manipulative anchor text
  (e.g. "Philadelphia zoning and land-use practice"), and the firm site should
  link **in** to the sponsor page where appropriate.
- Anchor text describes the destination topic, not keyword-stuffed. Reciprocal
  links only where they genuinely help the reader.
- Exact link targets depend on the firm site's live URL structure — **confirm
  before building the map.**

## 6. AEO/GEO best practices

Per [[12_Brain/03_Concepts/AEO GEO and AI Discovery]],
[[12_Brain/03_Concepts/Entity Authority and Citation Readiness]], and
[[12_Brain/03_Concepts/AI Visibility Measurement]]:

- **Answer extractability** — concise Q&A and definition blocks a model can lift
  cleanly; direct answer first, detail second.
- **Entity consistency** — identical firm name, address, and phone (NAP) across
  the page, firm site, and any profiles; `sameAs` pointing at the firm's
  canonical profiles only.
- **Corroborating sources** — align facts with the firm site and other
  authoritative first-party references so claims are cross-checkable.
- **Multi-platform distribution** — surface the sponsorship where it can be
  cited (firm site, podcast surfaces the firm controls), within the fact-safe
  boundary.
- **Citation-readiness** — every durable claim is verifiable and sourced, so
  answer engines can cite it without risk to the firm.

## 7. Schema markup spec

JSON-LD types to consider, **fact-safe — only fields verifiable from the firm
site or supplied identity ship**:

- **Organization / LegalService / Attorney** — firm name, URL, confirmed
  location and contact, confirmed practice areas, `sameAs` to canonical
  profiles. Omit any field that cannot be verified.
- **FAQPage** — only for Q&A actually published on the page, with fact-safe
  answers.
- **Podcast / sponsorship markup** — only where factually supported by confirmed
  show facts. Because episode slate, hosts, dates, and platforms are unconfirmed
  (overlay guardrail), **defer podcast/episode schema until those facts are
  confirmed.**
- Do not emit review, rating, award, or audience markup — none is established.
