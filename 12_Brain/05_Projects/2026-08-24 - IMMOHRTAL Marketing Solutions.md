# IMMOHRTAL Marketing Solutions

## Durable direction

IMMOHRTAL Marketing Solutions replaces the broad, ad-led MOHR MEDIA offer with
a specialized lane for expressive web design, website optimization, AEO and
GEO visibility, CRM integration, and bounded business agents.

The homepage uses the primary portfolio's visual authority, the verified old
MOHR MEDIA shared-canvas robot renderer, and a rapid particle sequence for the
21 currently verified public-safe client and partner marks. The sequence ends
with the same particle treatment for `YOU`. Do not claim 22 clients unless a
22nd relationship is verified in the canonical source.

## Operating boundary

The agency runtime prepares research, opportunity briefs, queue records, and
outreach drafts. Suppression, deduplication, approval fingerprints, maker and
checker separation, and execution receipts are required. External sending,
publishing to a new or ambiguous site, ad spend, credentials, and CRM mutation
remain gated by exact human approval and verified routing.

The personal 2023 B2B Google Sheet is a discovery source only. Its rows must be
requalified against current public evidence before outreach. Momentum 360
prospect sheets and automations are client assets and are excluded from this
business.

## Internal company workforce

Codex Marketing Chief is the sole user-facing orchestrator and final verifier.
The internal subordinate workforce has exactly five seats:

1. Operations and Finance Controller
2. Demand Intelligence Lead
3. Revenue Pipeline Manager
4. Delivery and Client Success Lead
5. Independent Quality and Risk Auditor

Scout, Atlas, Forge, Relay, and Proof are client-facing service characters on
the website. They are not internal employees and do not own the company board.
The crew's canonical definition, authority matrix, evidence contract, and daily
cadence live under `11_Agents/IMMOHRTAL Business Crew/`.

The operating day begins with an active Codex heartbeat at 08:30 ET and runs
through demand, revenue, delivery, independent audit, chief review, and a dated
closeout. The separate office schedule manifest remains uninstalled so there is
only one scheduler. The heartbeat may research, reconcile, and prepare drafts,
but it may never send outreach, create a calendar invitation, or claim a booking
without the required evidence and Dillon's exact approval.

## Live responsive baseline

`www.immohrtalmarketing.com` is the verified canonical public IMMOHRTAL destination. The apex
redirects to `www` in one permanent hop. Both former MOHR MEDIA hostnames preserve paths and
redirect permanently to the matching IMMOHRTAL route. The mobile
baseline supports 320, 390, 430, and 768 pixel viewports without accidental
