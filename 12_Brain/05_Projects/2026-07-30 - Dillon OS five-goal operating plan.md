# Dillon OS five-goal operating plan

## Goal 1: One-interface agentic work system

**Outcome:** Dillon talks to Marketing Chief; Marketing Chief routes, delegates,
checks, learns, and reports.

**Scoreboard**

- One canonical queue and one user-facing command center.
- Every material delegation has an owner, evidence, checker, and result.
- Routine work does not create follow-up coordination for Dillon.

## Goal 2: Qualified client revenue

**Outcome:** paid media and landing pages generate service-fit opportunities
that can become contracts or appointments.

**Current order**

1. Recover Omega, Onsite, and KJB lead quality and measurement.
2. Keep Bridge Software and VA Claims as the first two portfolio priorities.
3. Elevate Pritzker and Align as named strategic priorities without mixing
   Align's full-time lane into client revenue. Pritzker deep integration:
   [[12_Brain/05_Projects/2026-09-03 - Pritzker Law Group deep-integration goal]].

**Scoreboard**

- Qualified opportunity, estimate, appointment, and booked-work counts.
- Cost per qualified opportunity, not cost per click or raw platform event.
- Weekly reconciliation from ad interaction to named CRM outcome.

## Goal 3: The Ironic Ineptocracy growth system

**Outcome:** a complete, measured marketing system for the book and its related
newsletter and game experience.

**Finish line**

- Positioning, audiences, launch calendar, newsletter loop, game packaging,
  press and creator outreach, and organic distribution plan are complete.
- AEO/GEO/SEO content clusters and structured trust signals are implemented.
- GA4, Search Console, conversion events, and download/lead delivery are
  verified.
- Research produces experiments, not automatic strategy changes.

## Goal 4: High-craft website factory

**Outcome:** fast production without template sameness or predictable defects.

**Craft floor**

- Unique art direction and brand point of view per site.
- No accidental image crop, overflow, broken layout, or unreadable overlay.
- Mobile-first QA across key breakpoints and real devices.
- Purposeful motion and scroll behavior with reduced-motion support.
- Accessibility, performance, form, analytics, AEO/trust, and independent
  design-review gates before deployment.
- Mac's prospect-to-mailing pipeline is documented as a complete system with
  human approval at external delivery.

## Goal 5: Owned audience and measurable venture growth

**Outcome:** Ironic and IMMOHRTAL attract and retain the right visitors instead
of relying on launch spikes.

**Scoreboard**

- Qualified organic sessions, returning visitors, newsletter subscribers,
  downloads, and meaningful engagement.
- Weekly search and discovery deltas.
- Third-visit retention and re-engagement experiments.
- Separate analytics and assets for Ironic and IMMOHRTAL.

## Operating cadence

- Daily: ingest material client signals, rank by revenue and urgency, execute
  safe work, and update outcome state.
- Twice weekly: reconcile ad events to actual opportunities and inspect search
  terms, landing pages, calls, forms, and CRM dispositions.
- Weekly: review the five scoreboards, retire weak experiments, promote
  verified learning, and select the next highest-leverage build.
