# Philadelphia 25-site factory batch 4

## State

This is a separate 25-business cohort, explicitly excluded from the earlier
session's Batch 3 list. All 25 previews were generated locally as private,
noindex evidence artifacts. The bundle is now published to the newly created
Netlify site `https://philly-25-redesigns-batch-4.netlify.app`. The hub and an
A.M. Electric page returned HTTP 200 with noindex, and a real-photo JPEG
returned HTTP 200 as `image/jpeg`. No business was contacted and outreach
remains on hold.

The real-logo gate passed for the full roster: 25 of 25 businesses have a
first-party logo asset captured from the business's official site and recorded
in `official-logo-cache-b4/manifest.json`. No fallback concept wordmarks were
used in the final sites.

## Reference language absorbed

The build mirrors the prior reference grammar: asymmetric cinematic hero,
editorial service/work story, verified-facts ribbon, moving signal marquee,
brand/process bridge, live-pulse service diagnosis, interactive dashboard
language, impact band, source-to-output rail, and roadmap/FAQ close. Each site
uses a distinct trade-specific skeleton, scroll reveals, responsive mobile
transitions, working-motion control, and a reduced-motion path. The real-photo
pass replaced the abstract media on 24 sites with visually inspected
photographs captured from official business pages or their page-served CDNs.
APT Heating and Cooling is explicitly held because no usable official photo was
verified; its abstract media is not approved as real business photography.
