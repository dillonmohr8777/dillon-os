# Compiler Protocol

**Summary:** agents compile; they do not rewrite history.

1. Capture into `12_Brain/raw/` (or let `/session-mine` / `/research-sweep` do it).
2. Compile into `12_Brain/entities/` or `12_Brain/concepts/` with `source:` links.
3. Update `12_Brain/INDEX.md` in the same change.
4. Never edit `12_Brain/raw/` after capture.
5. Decisions also land in `12_Brain/decisions/` with bi-temporal frontmatter.
6. Ship diffs, not claims.
