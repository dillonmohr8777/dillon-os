# Adopt the gated intelligence stack

## Decision

Dillon OS will use one connected operating path:

1. completed Grok research runs become immutable, source-linked vault captures;
2. promising findings enter an experiment queue instead of being installed directly;
3. material builds require different maker and checker identities;
4. MCPs require source, Inspector, permission, injection, and overlap review;
5. Context7 is limited to version-sensitive third-party documentation; and
6. website previews must pass the AEO/trust gate before mapped deployment.

## Why

The stack converts high-volume research into bounded, testable operating changes
without letting social posts, documentation, or automation output authorize account
changes, software adoption, sends, or publication.

## Reversal

Each layer is independently removable: disable the scheduled collector, remove the
Context7 MCP registration, or stop calling an individual gate. Immutable source
captures and prior review evidence remain for audit.
