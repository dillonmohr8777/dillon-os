# Book characters stay out of Agency Wars

**Decided by Dillon, 2026-09-09: no.**

The four Grand Theft Bureaucracy / Ironic Ineptocracy characters — Alec,
Brittany, Darnell, Javon — do not go into Agency Wars.

## What was being asked

The four characters are web-ready: 14 MB of `.glb` total, rigs and clips
intact, tracked in `Codex/philly-game/assets/characters/`, already proven to
walk around an OSM bake of Center City.

The tempting move was to reuse them in the browser Philadelphia world. The
answer is no.

## Why it is the right call

Agency Wars is Momentum's sales floor. It is the thing clients and prospects
are shown, with Mac Frederick and Sean Boyle as the founders in it. The book
is a separate property with a political-thriller cast. Free assets are not a
reason to mix the two — the cost is not the 14 MB, it is what the agency's
demo says about the agency.

## What this settles

- The founder pipeline (Higgsfield generation, Blender fitting, the
  `Idle`/`Walk`/`Greet`/`Present` clips) is the character pipeline for Agency
  Wars. There is no shortcut through the book assets.
- `Codex/philly-game` is a technical spike, not a product line. It proved a
  rigged character can be walked around an OSM bake in a browser. That finding
  transfers; the characters do not.
- The book characters keep their home in
  `2026-07-27/.../grand-theft-bureaucracy`, which is the only game tree with an
  off-device copy.
