# Fable 5.1 routing and effort defaults

**Summary:** the launch changes cache price, not the routing logic. On a Max plan
Fable 5.1 at medium effort is the cheapest Heavy seat; the savings come from effort,
short sessions, and cheap subagents, not from the model picker.

## Decision (revised 2026-09-01 after Dillon chose Fable)

1. Default model is `claude-fable-5-1` at **medium** effort. On a Max subscription the
   per-token price is irrelevant; quota is what burns, and cache reads dominate quota.
   Fable 5.1 cache reads cost a quarter of Fable 5's, so at medium effort it is the
   cheapest Heavy-tier seat available. High is the model default and roughly the
   Fable 5 spend profile; xhigh is for nothing in this vault.
2. Subagents default to Sonnet through `CLAUDE_CODE_SUBAGENT_MODEL`; the scout agent
   is pinned to Haiku; only marketing-chief, web-product-builder, and qa-critic keep
   opus-class pins. Prose rules in CLAUDE.md are not enforcement; settings are.
3. Codex/Sol takes mechanical build passes (scaffolding, batch site generation,
   lint fixes). Fable holds design authority and synthesis only.
4. Two lines went into CLAUDE.md: keep changes to what the task asks; edit
   surgically instead of rewriting files.

## Cost playbook for the next 44% of quota

- **One job per session, then quit.** Cache reads compound with conversation length;
  a 3.5 hour session is the failure mode. Start fresh per task.
- **Skills over chat.** `/site-factory`, `/mirror-and-improve`, `/vault-compile`,
  `/am-report` carry their own instructions; invoking them is cheaper than explaining.
- **Read by link, never by folder.** `INDEX.md` then wikilinks. Grep before open.
- **Delegate fetches and sweeps** to Sonnet or Haiku subagents; the lead only reads
  their compact report.
- **Never edit an earlier turn** and never switch models mid-session; both restart
  the cache.
- **Effort down before model up.** Try low on formatting and data pulls before
  reaching for high on anything.
- **Batch tool calls** in one turn instead of one at a time.
- **Nightly and weekly loops stay on cheap models** as already configured.

## Supersedes

Nothing. Extends the tiering in `claude-skills-repo/skills/model-routing`, which
already puts Fable in the Heavy tier and makes promotion, not demotion, the default.
