# Fable 5.1 routing and effort defaults (proposed)

**Summary:** the launch changes cache price, not the routing logic. The laziest
correct move is to leave Fable 5.1 off the default slot and spend it only where
design authority or synthesis earns it.

## Decision

1. Worker default stays Opus 5 or Codex/Sol. Anthropic's own doc says start there.
2. Fable 5.1 runs at **medium** effort. High is the model default and roughly the
   Fable 5 spend profile; xhigh is for nothing in this vault.
3. Fable 5.1 is allowed for: `/synthesize`, the design-authority pass in
   `/site-factory` and `/ui-design`, and final QA verdicts from `qa-critic`.
4. Subagents (`Explore`, scouts, radar audits, `/vault-compile`) are pinned to
   Haiku or Sonnet in settings. Prose rules in CLAUDE.md are not enforcement.
5. Two lines go into CLAUDE.md: keep changes to what the task asks; edit
   surgically instead of rewriting files.

## Supersedes

Nothing. Extends the tiering in `claude-skills-repo/skills/model-routing`, which
already puts Fable in the Heavy tier and makes promotion, not demotion, the default.
