# Need Momentum AI Division

**Summary:** do not launch an AI startup — package the factory, the radar, and Kind Insurance into four named offers, sell proof not acronyms, and scale through existing clients then QR outreach.

Need Momentum is a Philadelphia SMB shop (ads, SEO, web, GBP). Mac already asked for an AI nav with four sub-pages: AEO/GEO, AI Design, AI Marketing, AI Automation. One of those is live. The other three are still blogs or Google Docs. The offer index was empty. That is the launch blocker, not missing models.

## What is already true

- **AI Automation is a live page** with packages listed $1,500–$3,000 plus setup. Growth is priced *below* Starter on ecommerce — treat as brochure until Mac confirms sold prices. [AI Powered Marketing Automation](https://www.needmomentum.com/ai-powered-marketing-automation/)
- **Proof object already shipped:** The Kind Insurance PA — chatbot + CRM, 5 qualified conversations and 1 closed policy in 30 days, 3–6 week impl. [Kind Insurance case](https://www.needmomentum.com/marketing-case-studies/lead-automation/)
- **AEO is a blog, not a SKU** (2026-02-08). [Answer Engine Optimization](https://www.needmomentum.com/answer-engine-optimization/)
- Internally: 25-site/week [[12_Brain/entities/Website Factory|Website Factory]], [[12_Brain/projects/Prospect Radar V2|Prospect Radar V2]], human-gated QR mail engine, HubSpot agent prototype. Mac’s three outbound buckets (2026-07-09): site + QR, audit outreach, video “we made you this.”
- Homepage still sells Google, not AI. That is correct. 60% of US consumers in an April 2026 WordPress VIP survey called “AI” in brand messaging a turnoff ([TechCrunch 2026-06-16](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/sixty-percent-of-u-s-consumers-say-ai-in-brand-messaging-is-a-turnoff-survey-finds/)).

## What to offer (four SKUs, one system)

Match Mac’s nav. Do **not** invoice four acronyms. One engagement can pull two scoreboards (Google + citations) plus a site or a workflow. Internal cards: [[05_Offers/AEO GEO Visibility|AEO/GEO]], [[05_Offers/AI Design Site Factory|AI Design]], [[05_Offers/AI Marketing Creative|AI Marketing]], [[05_Offers/AI Automation Ops|AI Automation]].

1. **Visibility (AEO/GEO).** Paid Radar diagnostic, then a 90-day program of people-first pages, GBP, schema-for-rich-results, and citation measurement. Google’s 2026-05-15 guide: generative features are RAG over the Search index; ignore llms.txt, chunking, fake mentions, and “write for the model.” Measure Search Console generative AI + GA AI referrals + a named prompt list — not a vanity visibility score. [Google AI optimization guide](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/fundamentals/ai-optimization-guide)
2. **Design.** The factory. Cold: a noindex demo of *their* business. Warm: Tier A template rebuild or Tier B bespoke after they engage. Do not put “AI-designed” on the client homepage.
3. **Marketing.** Ads and creative with human taste. AI drafts, humans ship. Do not sell Meta’s default-on AI creative as the product. Do not lead client ads with the word AI.
4. **Automation.** Already live. Tighten to the Kind Insurance pattern: intake bot → client-owned CRM → producer routing → UTMs. Disclose that a chatbot is AI. Client keeps the accounts.

Entry motion for every SKU: **paid diagnostic → 90-day pilot → retainer**. Seer (Philly) starts many GEO clients on a diagnostic, not a 12-month lock. [Seer GEO, Apr 2026](https://www.seerinteractive.com/generative-engine-optimization)

## How to differentiate at launch

Philly already has a GEO leader (Seer) and a $3k/mo software+service GEO card (WebFX Bronze, 6-month, OmniSEO bundled — [WebFX AI SEO pricing](https://www.webfx.com/seo/pricing/ai-seo/)). Do not out-Seer Seer or out-OmniSEO WebFX.

The wedge only Need Momentum has:

- **We already built the artifact.** Demo site in the mail, Radar PDF in the inbox, Kind Insurance as the ops proof. Competitors pitch. Momentum shows the work.
- **Local SMB, not funded SaaS.** LoudFace Autopilot (~$5k, Dual = site + AEO) is the packaging analog and the wrong ICP. Home services, medical, legal, franchise — the industry pages already on the site.
- **QR + human gate, not “I built you a site” email.** That email motion is a 2026 spam product. Mac’s StackAdapt/PostGrid path stays a physical piece with approval. That is the opposite of Klaudius-style spray.
- **Two scoreboards, one invoice.** Google (SEO/GBP/ads) plus citations. Seer says treating SEO and GEO as the same *or* as unrelated silver bullets are both mistakes. Google says the work is still SEO. Sell the work, not the acronym.
- **Client owns the stack.** CRM, ad accounts, GSC, GA4 stay on their logins. Vendor-owned n8n with no handover is the landlord model buyers walk from.
- **Taste at volume.** Tier A factory for outreach; Tier B only after a bite. Hallmark-class anti-slop on the design lane. Speed without looking like one template.

Do not copy: First Page Sage’s published GEO tiers are paid listicles ($2–3k / $4–7k / $8–12k, 2025-11-14, seller survey). Seer warns scaled listicle “AI visibility” can reverse. [FPS GEO cost](https://firstpagesage.com/seo-blog/generative-engine-optimization-geo-cost-breakdown/)

Do not copy: enterprise AI studios, 105-item menus, citation guarantees, “AI-powered results,” or a public exact rate card for unproven SKUs.

## What we are going to do

Order is the strategy. Detail and owners: [[12_Brain/projects/Need Momentum AI Division]].

1. Fill the offer index (this change). Confirm prices with Mac before any public number except the live automation page.
2. Ship the three missing **service** pages. Keep the homepage human. Nav can say AI; body copy says the outcome (cited, rebuilt, followed-up).
3. One proof link + 5-min Loom: Radar walkthrough + Kind Insurance + one factory demo with human redlines. That *is* the deck.
4. AI addendum + chatbot disclosure to counsel. FTC substantiation on claims ([2023-02-27](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check)). NY synthetic-performer rule if generated people appear in ads (in force 2026-06-09).
5. 90-day pilots on two or three **current** clients (home services + one Kind-like ops). Learn before outbound spend.
6. Then scale Mac’s three buckets: weekly factory + Radar audits + video, human-approved, Philly verticals inverted toward home services / medical / legal.
7. Productize skills **inside** the factory (Hallmark, Vercel web-design-guidelines, upstream claude-ads, one delivery method). Do not sell a 300-skill dump or Dillon’s camofox / semrush-proxy / unfiltered-gen forks.
