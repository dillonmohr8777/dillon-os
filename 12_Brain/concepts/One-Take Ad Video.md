# One-Take Ad Video

**Summary:** write Seedance 2.5 ads as a 30s shot list with timestamps —
generate and post only after spend and publish approval.

Mac's 2026-07-09 line still stands: AI Video Outreach is "hey we made
you this" on IG/LI via Higgsfield
([[02_Campaigns/AI Site Builder Outreach Engine/Slack Evidence Log|Slack Evidence Log]]).
[[12_Brain/entities/Seedance 2.5|Seedance 2.5]] is the model that
makes a 15–30s one-take plausible.

Pattern:

1. Pick aspect first (`9:16` for Reels/Shorts/GBP, `16:9` for YouTube
   pre-roll). Extensions inherit that aspect.
2. Prompt as a shot list, not a vibe paragraph. Timestamp the beats.
3. Attach harvest stills as omni refs when identity matters. Do not
   put client names or private facts in the prompt.
4. Default `generate_audio` off when a platform soundtrack will
   replace it.
5. Tell the truth about resolution: live Higgsfield path is 720p.
6. Draft in `/seedance`. Do not post.

`/motion-design` is page motion. This concept is generative video.
