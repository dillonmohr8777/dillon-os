# Research Disclaimers Are Not Copy

**Summary:** prospect pages name the food, the doctor, and the service. They never narrate what the writer refused to invent.

Harvest gaps are a research problem. They are not a voice. Lines like "no invented house specials," "the claim stays at that scale," or "as their locations page states" read like a compliance memo, not a restaurant.

Do the research (menu, reviews, staff names, neighborhood nouns). Then write like you already work there. Keep addresses, phones, hours, and prices sourced. Put the sourced nouns in the hero and the catalog: General Tso's, kalbi, Dr. Nguyen, 119-point inspection.

`_write_briefs.js` now fails the batch if hedge phrases or banned sentence openers land in customer copy.
