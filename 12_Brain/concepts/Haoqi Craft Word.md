# Haoqi Craft Word

**Summary:** one or two lowercase Pacifico glass words sit in the back; skip the trade if you cannot name the feeling after the visit.

## The test

Say the line out loud. If it is the outcome of the visit, keep it. If it is a service list, kill it.

`smile more` passes for a dentist. `stay cool` passes for HVAC. `window & wall units` still fails. The first one-word pass skipped HVAC. Two words unlock the trade.

## How it sits

1. Same font as `hello`. Pacifico, lowercase, tubular glass. `SMILE` in caps is a different face. The runtime lowercases whatever you pass.
2. One reserved slot. On desktop the slot is the right column and the line fills that width. Type stays in the left column. Stickers stay in the slot and keep off the letters.
3. Photos and headlines never share pixels with the line.
4. The line fades on scroll. It is atmosphere, not the H1.

## Lines that earned a row

`smile more` `stay cool` `stay warm` `breathe easy` `come home` `see more` `come hungry` `glow up` `fresh cut` `go hard` `go up` `sink in` `lights on` `grow wild` `your side` `stay dry` `one scoop` `sip more`

Skip: funeral, campus research, tax, IT, inventory retail, catalog SKUs.
