# Haoqi Craft Word

**Summary:** one lowercase Pacifico glass word sits in the back; skip the trade if you cannot name what they do in a single `hello`-shaped word.

## The test

Say the word out loud. If it is the outcome of the visit, keep it. If it is a service list, kill it.

`smile` passes for a dentist. `window & wall units` fails for HVAC. Jarman has no craft word.

## How it sits

1. Same font as `hello`. Pacifico, lowercase, tubular glass. `SMILE` in caps is a different face. The runtime lowercases whatever you pass.
2. One reserved slot. On desktop the slot is the right column and the word gets bigger. Type stays in the left column. Stickers stay in the slot and keep off the letters.
3. Photos and headlines never share pixels with the word.
4. The word fades on scroll. It is atmosphere, not the H1.

## Words that earned a row

`smile` `sight` `paws` `glow` `bloom` `shine` `ink` `brew` `pour` `sweet` `sip` `taste` `style` `gloss` `ease` `strong` `climb` `float` `move` `align` `heal` `flow` `spark` `grow` `ride` `step` `soak` `read`

Skip: HVAC, insurance, lawyers, roofing, flooring, inventory retail, campus research, anything that needs a phrase.
