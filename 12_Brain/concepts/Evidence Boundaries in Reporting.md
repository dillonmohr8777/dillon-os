# Evidence Boundaries in Reporting

**Summary:** a client report claims only what a source explicitly proves — everything else ships labeled as a caveat, and lead PII never appears in public artifacts.

- Don't claim GSC metrics, image compression, alt-text, or internal-link edits without explicit proof (Bar Crawl lesson).
- A polished report can ship **"Ready with caveat"** when date windows, conversion counts, or policy framing are unverified — the caveat is stated, not hidden.
- Modeled numbers stay separated from measured numbers (7-Eleven ROAS model: directions clicks → assumed visit rate → assumed purchase rate → AOV — clearly framed as a model).
- **PII discipline:** public reports and Slack/Gmail summaries stay aggregate-only; lead names/emails only in private channels on explicit request (KJB precedent).
- Never imply a person wasn't contacted without checking emails/calls/meetings first (Ben Harrison BDR rule).
