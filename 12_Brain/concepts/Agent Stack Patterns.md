# Governed Agent Stack Patterns

**Summary:** the stack compounds when authority, evidence, proposals, execution,
and verification remain separate and reviewable.

1. **Authority is explicit.** Dillon decides; Codex/Marketing Chief orchestrates
   and verifies; workers return bounded evidence and artifacts.
2. **The vault remembers but does not authorize.** `12_Brain/` can preserve a
   proposal or decision receipt without becoming a queue or config controller.
3. **Harness and model are measured together.** Agentic benchmark scores depend
   on the harness, effort, environment, date, and cost.
4. **Research and application are different transactions.** A scout never
   silently turns its own recommendation into canonical state.
5. **Handoffs are bounded.** Every worker receives source limits,
   `budget_tokens`, `timeout_seconds`, acceptance checks, and an approval state.
6. **Independent verification is the bottleneck.** A fresh verifier rejects
   missing locators, stale prices, provider-only claims, and untested routing.
7. **External and durable changes stay gated.** Installs, config changes,
   schedules, commits, pushes, publishes, spend, and client-state mutations need
   the authority that actually covers them.
8. **Use representative tasks.** Public leaderboards identify candidates;
   Dillon-specific evals decide whether a route improves real work.
