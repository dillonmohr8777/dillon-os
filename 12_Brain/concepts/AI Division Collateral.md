# AI Division Collateral

**Summary:** launch as four service pages plus one Loom and an offer index — not a SaaS kit, not a 40-slide deck, not a public rate card.

Mac already prefers one link and a five-minute Loom. Buyer checklists in 2026 ask for the same thing: named stack, human edits on last week’s work, client-owned accounts, itemized fees, a short paid pilot. Seller-authored lists (Soku 2026-07-20, DigiMark 2026-05-04, Bliss Drive 2026-06-30) still converge. Strategy: [[12_Brain/concepts/Need Momentum AI Division]].

## Shipped in vault (2026-08-14)

Pack lives at `02_Campaigns/Need Momentum AI Division/`. Operating plan (`plan.md`), WordPress pages, legal drafts, hub, Loom script, proposed pilots, branded HTML leave-behind (`pack/Need-Momentum-AI-Division.html`). Live site, Loom recording, counsel markup, and client pilots still wait on Mac.

## Must-have before launch

1. **Internal offer index** — four cards, not public. Deliverables, in/out, stack, human checkpoints, price *band*, sample SOW. Without this the nav is vapor. Cards live in `05_Offers/`.
2. **Four service pages matching the nav.** AEO/GEO, AI Design, AI Marketing, AI Automation. Each: who it’s for, 5–6 steps, what’s in/out, engagement shape (project / 90-day / retainer), CTA to a **paid** diagnostic. Pattern: [Liquid LQDAI](https://www.liquidagency.com/lqdai/), [First Page Sage AEO](https://firstpagesage.com/aeo-services/), [Metajive AI](https://www.metajive.com/ai). Automation is already live — rewrite it; don’t add a fifth page.
3. **One proof URL + 5-min Loom.** Redacted [[12_Brain/projects/Prospect Radar V2|Prospect Radar]] audit + Kind Insurance walkthrough + one factory before/after with the human redlines visible. This replaces the sales deck. Do not build a public AEO-checker SaaS to compete with SRP; wrap Radar.
4. **Named stack + human-in-the-loop on each page.** Buyers disqualify “leading AI tools.” Name Claude / the actual CRM / the actual ad platforms. Name who approves when it breaks.
5. **90-day pilot one-pager.** Week-by-week deliverables, exit, what “good” looks like (calls, citations, time-to-follow-up — pick one). No 12-month lock before proof.
6. **Claims sheet.** Allowed: “AI-assisted production with human review.” Forbidden: citation guarantees, “AI-powered results,” “better than a non-AI agency” without proof. [FTC 2023-02-27](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check).
7. **AI addendum + per-SOW tool list** to counsel. Standing: IP carve-out for generated portions, no training on client data, liability when output is wrong. Per engagement: which tools, which vendor tier. Chatbot: disclose AI at first interaction (EU Art. 50 in force 2026-08-02 for in-scope work; still good practice in Philly).
8. **Creative disclosure checklist** (ops, not footer). NY synthetic-performer rule in force 2026-06-09 for ads with a non-identifiable digital human. Platform labels (Meta / TikTok / YouTube) for photoreal generated media. Not a duty to stamp “AI” on every blog.
9. **Data/ownership FAQ.** Client owns ads, GSC, GA4, CRM. Where prompts go. No consumer ChatGPT for confidentials.

## Nice after three paying AI clients

- Problem / Solution / Outcome case with **one operational number** (Kind Insurance is the template: 5 conversations, 1 policy, 30 days).
- Public price *bands* as education, quotes still private. First Page Sage publishes bands on a blog, not a downloadable SKU list.
- Live Looker/GA4 access as a contractual default.
- Named internal agent only after Slack intake / HubSpot prototype run on real accounts (Mod Op launched Brand Agent internally first, 2025-04-15).
- Raw-vs-edited sample pack.
- Written internal GenAI policy (4As sells a template; don’t block launch on membership).

## Do not waste time on

- Academies and long trainings. Mac vetoed this.
- Product Hunt / waitlists / “AI Pods.”
- Public exact rate card for unproven SKUs.
- Footer-only AI policy as the disclosure.
- 40-slide decks. Recycled case-study decks are a buyer red flag.
- C2PA / industry watermarking program as launch work.
- Hold-co press theater.
- Guaranteed ChatGPT citations.

## Copy rule for the site

Homepage stays human (“more customers from Google”). AI lives in the nav and the four pages. Body copy leads with the outcome: cited in answers, a site that doesn’t look dated, follow-up that happens, ads a human would run. 60% of US consumers in the April 2026 WordPress VIP survey called “AI” in brand messaging a turnoff ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2026/06/16/sixty-percent-of-u-s-consumers-say-ai-in-brand-messaging-is-a-turnoff-survey-finds/)).
