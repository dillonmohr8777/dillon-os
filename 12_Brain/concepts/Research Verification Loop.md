# Research Verification Loop

**Summary:** research isn't a prompt, it's a pipeline — fan out, collect
receipts, let a skeptic attack every claim, and only survivors land in the
vault as dated pages with expiry dates.

The pipeline:

1. One question splits into 3–5 sub-questions.
2. Parallel agents fan out across different surfaces: socials for the
   practitioner layer (what people run right now, what breaks), the web for
   docs and pricing, scrapers for full text.
3. Every finding becomes a receipt: claim + source link + date.
4. A **skeptic agent that did not do the research** attacks every claim —
   single-source hype gets labeled, contradictions get surfaced, only
   survivors pass. Fresh-context checkers outperform a model reviewing its
   own work.
5. Verified findings land in `concepts/` (or `entities/`) as dated, linked
   pages carrying an `expires:` date so stale knowledge announces itself.

Why: in AI/marketing, advice from six months ago is often actively wrong, and
the real practitioner layer lives on socials, not official docs. This is the
difference between research and rumor collection.

Run it: `/research-sweep <question>` (weekly, on the niche).
