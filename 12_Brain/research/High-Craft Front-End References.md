**Summary:** dated, source-linked catalog of the operator's Awwwards / CSSDA / Codrops / WebGL list, plus the three live portfolios. Award tags are submitter tags unless a second source agrees.

Use this page as a reference shelf for craft demos. The reusable visual system lives on [[12_Brain/concepts/Haoqi Design Language|Haoqi Design Language]]. Built this pass: `haoqi-radar-sites/` for Jarman Sales and Andorra Family Dentistry from [[Daily-Briefs/radar-2026-08-15|radar-2026-08-15]].

## How to read the table

- **Survivor** means the award date, live host, and named studio checked out on 2026-08-15.
- **Single-source** means one article or one gallery. Fine to study, not fine to treat as audited engineering.
- Award "Technologies & Tools" chips are not a second implementation audit.
