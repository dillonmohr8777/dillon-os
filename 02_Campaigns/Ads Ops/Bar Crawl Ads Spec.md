# Bar Crawl USA — Ads Spec

Biggest account: $950/mo. Google Ads **435-710-2897**. Events/entertainment,
20-city landing pages on barcrawlusa.com.

## Standing landmines (check every cycle)
- Recurring policy disapprovals: **"Alcohol information"** and **"Destination
  not working"** — audit disapproved ads/assets first, fix destinations, keep
  alcohol-compliant copy patterns on file once found.
- 20-city LP family: destination URLs must match live pages (the tracker sheet
  is the source of truth; Crawl Maps is its own URL).

## Optimization lanes
- Per-city campaign performance: kill/scale by city + seasonality (events are
  date-driven — pause past-event ads, scale upcoming cities).
- Search terms hygiene; branded vs non-branded split.
- Conversion tracking: verify what conversion action exists and what it counts
  (ticket click-outs? form fills?) — undocumented in vault; record it.
