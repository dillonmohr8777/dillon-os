Real, on-brand images of Dillon Mohr (IMMOHRTAL). These are the source
of truth for photo-based content and for swapping into the site's
placeholder image slots. More to come.

| `portrait-bw.png` | Clean B&W studio portrait, editorial quality | Press / EPK hero, about-page portrait, quote cards, IG profile |

## Notes
- The three newsprint collages share the video/site aesthetic (Erie
  lighthouse, 814, Pittsburgh skyline) so they cut together as a set.
- `portrait-bw.png` is the clean counterweight: use it where the collages
  would be too busy (profile pic, press, single-subject quote cards).
- Site placeholder slots these can replace live in
  `immohrtal-site/public/`: `artist.jpg` (hero), `about-portrait.jpg`,
  `daughter.jpg` (story image), and the OG/press images.
