## Personalization contract

Every outbound record must contain:

1. One current first-party URL.
2. One observation visible on that URL.
3. One buyer action affected by the observation.
4. One smallest sufficient IMMOHRTAL offer.
5. One reason not to send, including identity ambiguity, prior relationship, suppression, category risk, weak evidence, or missing postal footer.
6. A fresh owner and QA readback before release.

## Fifty-per-day operating model

Fifty is the daily preparation ceiling immediately, not an instruction to blast a new domain.

| Phase | Research records | New first touches | Follow-ups | Release rule |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| Days 1 to 3 | 50 daily | 5 to 10 daily | 0 | Exact postal footer, SPF, DKIM, DMARC, verified address, manual QA |
| Days 4 to 7 | 50 daily | 10 to 20 daily | As due | Bounce below 2%, no complaint signal, positive inbox placement |
| Week 2 | 50 daily | 20 to 30 daily | Included in daily cap | Healthy reputation and verified list quality |
| Week 3 onward | 50 daily | Up to 25 new | Up to 25 follow-ups | Fifty total messages per day, not fifty new plus follow-ups |

Stop conditions: hard bounce above 2%, complaint signal, authentication failure, missing unsubscribe handling, duplicate recipient, unresolved client or employer conflict, or any sender mismatch.
