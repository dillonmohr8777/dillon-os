# IMMOHRTAL Founder Pitch

Owner: Dillon Mohr  
Status: Approved internal positioning language. External use still requires exact context and factual review.

## The one-line position

IMMOHRTAL helps service businesses turn their website, search visibility, paid media, and CRM into one clear path from attention to a real sales conversation.

## The 15-second pitch

I run IMMOHRTAL. I find where a service business is losing clarity or momentum between its website, search presence, ads, and follow-up, then I fix the smallest part that will make the customer path work better.

## The 30-second pitch

I run IMMOHRTAL Marketing Solutions. I work with service businesses whose marketing looks active but does not operate as one system. The website says one thing, search shows another, ads send people into a weak page, or the CRM loses the context. I diagnose the break, show the work, and build the clearest next move, whether that is a stronger website, technical SEO, AEO and GEO, paid media management, or a cleaner HubSpot handoff.

## The 60-second pitch

Most service businesses do not need another disconnected tactic. They need the website, search presence, paid media, content, and follow-up process to tell the same story.

That is what I build at IMMOHRTAL. I start by inspecting the current customer path and finding the first point where trust, clarity, or action breaks down. Then I create something the owner can inspect before the scope gets bigger. It might be a page direction, a technical repair plan, an answer-surface outline, an ad-to-page correction, or a website-to-HubSpot map.

The goal is simple: make it easier for the right customer to understand the offer, trust the business, and start a real conversation.

## The cold-email credibility sentence

I run IMMOHRTAL and help service businesses make the website, search presence, paid media, and follow-up path work as one customer system.

Use this only after a verified observation about the recipient. Never open a cold email with this sentence.

## The discovery-call opener

Before I recommend anything, I want to understand where the customer path is breaking now. Is the bigger issue getting found, explaining the offer, turning visits into inquiries, or following up once someone raises their hand?

## The owner-to-owner version

I help service businesses fix the part of marketing that is making the next sale harder than it needs to be. I look across the website, search, ads, content, and CRM, then show the first move I would make and why.

## The CMO version

IMMOHRTAL connects acquisition, website experience, answer visibility, and CRM handoff around one measurable customer path. I diagnose the highest-friction break, define the proof required, and build the smallest sufficient intervention before expanding scope.

## Offer bridge

Use the problem to choose the offer. Do not recite the menu.

| Verified problem | Best first offer |
|---|---|
| Important pages are hard to crawl, interpret, or index consistently | Technical SEO at $700 per month |
| Customer questions are not answered clearly for search and AI answer tools | AEO at $700 per month |
| Business expertise and entity facts are poorly connected across public sources | GEO at $700 per month |
| Search, answer visibility, and entity structure all need to work together | Technical SEO, AEO, and GEO bundle at $1,500 per month |
| Google Ads needs focused management | Google Ads management at $400 per month |
| Meta Ads needs focused management | Meta Ads management at $400 per month |
| Google and Meta need coordinated management | Google and Meta bundle at $650 per month |
| The site undersells the business or creates conversion friction | Scoped website design or optimization engagement |
| The public form and CRM handoff lose context or ownership | Scoped HubSpot and CRM implementation |

## Proof language

Use:

- I inspected the current page.
- Here is the exact point I would fix first.
- I built a direction you can inspect.
- This is a hypothesis until we review the business context together.
- The work should be visible before the pitch gets bigger.

Do not use:

- Guaranteed rankings
- Guaranteed leads
- Guaranteed revenue
- Transform your business
- Unlock your potential
- Revolutionary marketing
- AI-powered growth engine
- Any result, client story, or metric that has not been verified for public use

## Closing questions

Choose one.

- Worth sending over the three changes I would prioritize first?
- Want the exact evidence and the smallest repair path?
- Would a one-question answer and source outline be useful?
- Should I map the public handoff and the questions to verify inside HubSpot?
- Is this already handled, or would the short version be useful?

## Final voice test

Before using the pitch, confirm:

- It starts in the buyer's world.
- It names one real problem.
- It connects the problem to a commercial action.
- It avoids unsupported outcomes.
- It gives one next step.
- It contains no em dash or en dash.
- It sounds like Dillon talking to another capable owner.
