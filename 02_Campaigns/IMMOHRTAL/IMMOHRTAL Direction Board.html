The Angle

IMMOHRTAL is what happens when the guy who markets everyone else has to sell the one thing he can't be objective about. The audience isn't asked to believe he'll blow up — they're asked to recognize the feeling of a dream you were supposed to have outgrown. "Delusional" is not an insult here. It's the ticket in.
