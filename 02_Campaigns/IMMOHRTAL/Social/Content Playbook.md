# IMMOHRTAL Content Playbook

The hard truth first. Waveforms and glowing lines are packaging, not
content. The content is you. Your face, your voice, the story, the room
you make this in. This doc is the part that actually grows an artist,
and almost all of it you can shoot on a phone with no gear.

The beat clips we made still have a job. They are backdrops to talk
over, loops for a story, or the last few seconds of a video. They are
not the post by themselves.

---

## Your unfair advantage

You have the best hook in music marketing already: a Chief Marketing
Officer who quietly made a rap album. Most artists have to invent a
reason for people to care. You have a real one. Lead with it.

The whole account can run on one promise: watch a marketing exec try to
become a rapper for real, and be honest about every part of it.

---

## The hooks (first line is everything)

First 2 seconds decide if anyone watches. Say the hook flat and real,
no hype voice. Shoot these talking straight to the camera, phone
propped up, decent light from a window.

1. "I am a chief marketing officer. I market other people's stuff all
   day. Nobody knows I made a whole rap album."
2. "I am almost 29 and I decided to start rapping. I know how that
   sounds. Let me explain."
3. "My whole job is getting people to pay attention to things. So watch
   me try to do it for the one thing I actually care about."
4. "Mac Miller is the reason I rap. I am gonna tell you exactly what his
   album did to me at 18."
5. "I recorded this album in 2023 and sat on it for two years because I
   was scared. Here is the first one I am putting out."
6. "People at my job have no idea this account exists. If you are one of
   my coworkers, hey. Guess I am a rapper now."
7. "I am from Erie, PA. Nobody makes rap here. That is exactly why I am
   doing it."
8. "Everybody said do the safe thing. I did the safe thing for ten
   years. Now I am doing this. If not now, when."
9. "Let me play you thirty seconds of something I made and you tell me
   if I am delusional."
10. "This is what happens when the marketing guy stops marketing for
    everybody else."

Each of these is one video. Say the hook, then talk for 20 to 40
seconds. Real, not scripted word for word. End on a track playing.

---

## Repeatable series (so you never wonder what to post)

- **The Delusion Diary.** You, to camera, one honest thought per post.
  The doubt, the excitement, the coworker fear, the why. This is the
  spine of the account.
- **First Listen.** Film someone hearing a track for the first time.
  Their face is the content. Family, friends, Keev.
- **The Split.** Same day, two clips. Morning you in work mode. Night
  you in the booth. Cut them together.
- **Making it.** Phone clips of writing, recording, the notebook, the
  screen, the levels. Rough is good. Rough reads as real.
- **The 814 / Pittsburgh.** You in the actual places. Lake, city,
  driving, the spots that are in the songs.
- **Play it loud.** You in the car playing your own track, just vibing,
  windows down. Low effort, high relatability.

## Phone shot list (no gear, no crew)

Capture a pile of these whenever. I turn them into finished pieces.

- 5 takes of you saying different hooks straight to camera
- Hands writing in a notebook, close up
- The studio or wherever you record, wide and close
- You with headphones on, eyes closed, nodding to a track
- Driving, track playing, you mouthing nothing, just feeling it
- Walking through Erie or Pittsburgh, phone in hand
- The work version of you: laptop, coffee, meeting energy, tie if you
  wear one
- The night version: dark room, one light, booth
- Anything with Keev, you two just being friends

Vertical, hold each shot 5 seconds longer than feels natural. Do not
narrate while filming b roll, I add the audio.

## What I do with it

You film rough. You drop the clips in a folder or send them. I cut them
into finished vertical videos with the brand look, captions in your
voice, the beat under it, and the end card. I also write the caption
and hashtags for each and slot it into the posting calendar.

That loop is the actual content engine. The graphics were me working
with what I had. You on camera plus me editing is the real thing.

---

## Start tiny

Do not plan a shoot. Right now, today, prop the phone against something,
say hook number 1 or number 2 one time, and send it. One clip. We build
from there. If not now, when.
