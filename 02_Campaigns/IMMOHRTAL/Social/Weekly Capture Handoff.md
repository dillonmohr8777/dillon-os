# IMMOHRTAL Weekly Capture Handoff

Record one 45 to 60 minute vertical phone batch each week. Upload the original files without platform watermarks.

## Dillon captures

- Five direct-to-camera hooks, each delivered twice.
- One honest Delusion Diary answer lasting 60 to 120 seconds.
- One audience question answered three different ways.
- One first-listen reaction with clear permission from everyone shown.
- Five workday shots and five after-hours studio shots.
- Notebook, microphone, screen, headphones, driving, Erie, or Pittsburgh B roll.
- One relationship story involving Keev, Mac's influence, the 814, fatherhood, or starting late.
- Five seconds of clean room tone.

## Recording rules

- Shoot vertically at 1080p or higher.
- Use a window or soft front light.
- Keep the phone at eye level for direct-to-camera clips.
- Leave one second before speaking and two seconds after finishing.
- Hold B roll for at least eight seconds.
- Do not use unapproved lyrics or expose unreleased full masters.
- Mark family footage `APPROVED` or `PRIVATE` before upload.

## Codex returns

- Four vertical discovery edit briefs.
- Two supporting feed or carousel posts.
- Platform-specific captions, titles, CTAs, and UTMs.
- One live-session outline.
- A scheduler-ready calendar update.
- Three controlled variants of the prior week's winning post.
- The updated performance scorecard and next-week recommendation.

Nothing is published, emailed, advertised, or pitched until Dillon approves the final queue.
