# IMMOHRTAL Content Upgrade Plan v2

**Prepared:** July 15, 2026  
**Decision:** Keep the visual identity. Rebuild the distribution mix around Dillon's face, voice, music, and real listener proof.  
**Publishing status:** Planning and production only. Nothing has been posted or scheduled externally.

## Executive verdict

The social package looks good enough to launch, but it is not yet good enough to scale by itself.

The design system is coherent, premium, and recognizable. The typography, dark editorial palette, waveform language, Split concept, and campaign lines create a real world around the album. The weakness is that too many assets look like campaign packaging rather than native social content. A stranger can admire the design without caring about the person or playing the song.

The upgrade is not a redesign. It is a change in emphasis from roughly 70 percent designed packaging and 30 percent human material to 70 percent human material and 30 percent designed support.

## What changes now

### 1. Put a human opening on discovery posts

Every discovery video should begin with one of four things: Dillon's face, a visible reaction, a specific conflict, or the strongest approved musical moment. Branded motion can enter after the viewer understands why they should care.

Good opening patterns:

- "I spent years marketing everybody else because promoting myself felt embarrassing."
- "I am a CMO, almost 29, and I just made the rap album I kept talking myself out of."
- "This is the exact moment my best friend realized I was serious."
- "Nobody hears Erie when they picture rap. I wanted this record to change that."
- "I need an honest answer: is this brave or completely delusional?"

### 2. Stop making every post perform the same job

Use one CTA based on the viewer's stage. Do not append the campaign closer to every sentence if it weakens the natural voice.

| Funnel stage | Post job | CTA examples |
|---|---|---|
| Acquisition | Earn the next second | "Would you keep listening?" or no CTA |
| Activation | Create participation | "Pick the next track" or "Brave or delusional?" |
| Retention | Build a returning series | "Part two tomorrow" or "Follow the experiment" |
| Referral | Give people a person to send it to | "Send this to the friend who keeps waiting" |
| Revenue/fan action | Move proven interest | "Hear the private preview" or the verified smart link |

### 3. Build proof around the music

The audience needs evidence that the record affects people. Capture Keev, friends, local listeners, musicians, marketers, fathers, and strangers hearing a controlled section for the first time. Keep the camera on the listener longer than the playback edit. Never script the reaction.

### 4. Make the art native to each platform

- TikTok: blunt hooks, reply videos, rough edges, comments as prompts.
- Instagram Reels: clean captions and covers, emotional proof, collaborative posts, Story follow-through.
- YouTube Shorts: searchable titles, a complete mini-story, and a clear next video.
- LinkedIn: the honest CMO/artist conflict, transparent lessons, and results without turning the album into corporate content.
- Facebook: personal history, Erie and Pittsburgh identity, relationships, and local sharing.

### 5. Create a real weekly capture constraint

One 60-minute capture session must deliver:

- 10 opening hooks, two takes each.
- 4 complete direct-to-camera stories.
- 2 listener reactions.
- 1 live or stripped performance moment.
- 10 office/studio/notebook/location B-roll clips.
- 1 longer answer for LinkedIn, YouTube, blog, and email repurposing.

## Upgraded weekly content mix

- 40 percent direct-to-camera confession or story.
- 20 percent music performance or first-listen reaction.
- 15 percent process, notebook, studio, and location footage.
- 15 percent carousel, photo, quote, or designed asset.
- 10 percent live, poll, comment reply, or community decision.

Publish four strong vertical videos, two feed support posts, daily Story frames, one live touchpoint, and one deeper text post per week. Quality and learning matter more than filling every slot.

## Creative testing system

Each core story gets three controlled variants:

1. Conflict first: the uncomfortable truth in the first sentence.
2. Proof first: reaction, lyric, location, or visual evidence before explanation.
3. Question first: a polarizing but honest audience decision.

Change only one major variable per variant. Log the hook, length, music section, CTA, and distribution platform. Evaluate after comparable reach rather than declaring a winner from one early spike.

Promote only posts that combine strong retention with a meaningful downstream action. Views alone are not a scaling signal.

## Free publishing stack decision

### Recommended now: Buffer Free

Connect Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts. Buffer Free currently allows three channels and ten queued posts per channel, with slots refilling as posts publish. That is enough to keep a rolling seven-to-ten-day launch queue without paying for a tool.

Use Buffer for:

- Approved evergreen Reels, TikToks, and Shorts.
- Carousels and static support posts when the format is supported.
- Drafting and queue visibility.
- Basic analytics and comment triage.
- Consistent UTM-tagged links.

Post natively for:

- Trending or licensed audio selection.
- Instagram Story polls, question stickers, links, and Lives.
- Instagram Collab invitations and sensitive music tagging.
- TikTok reply videos, stitches, duets, and trend-dependent posts.
- Posts whose final cover, caption placement, or sound must be inspected in the app.

### Why not the other free plans as the primary tool

| Tool | Free-plan fit | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Buffer | 3 channels; 10 queued per channel; supports Instagram, TikTok, and YouTube Shorts | Best single free publisher for this launch. |
| Metricool | 1 brand; 20 posts per month; LinkedIn and X excluded; stronger free competitor and analytics view | Useful as a secondary research dashboard, but the monthly publishing cap is below the campaign cadence. |
| Publer | 3 accounts; 10 queued per account; X excluded; only 24 hours of published-post history | Capable alternative, but weaker historical visibility for a measurement-led launch. |
| Meta Business Suite | Native Facebook Page scheduling and Meta account management | Best free native companion for Facebook and Instagram-specific features, not the cross-platform system of record. |

## SEO and AI-discovery upgrade

The site already has a useful technical base. The next gain comes from building the query and authority layer around real stories rather than publishing generic music blogs.

### Priority query clusters

1. IMMOHRTAL, Dillon Mohr, Dance With The Delusional, and individual track names.
2. Erie rapper, 814 hip-hop, Pittsburgh independent rapper, and Pennsylvania hip-hop.
3. CMO rapper, marketing executive makes rap album, and starting music at 29.
4. Independent album making, first release process, and balancing creative work with a career and fatherhood.
5. King Keev collaborations and approved Mac Miller influence context without implying affiliation.

### Pages and articles to create

- A definitive artist/about page with a concise fact block and named author.
- One page per released track with story, credits, approved lyrics excerpt, audio, and FAQ.
- "Why a CMO made a rap album at 29."
- "From Erie to Pittsburgh: the 814 story behind the record."
- "How Dance With The Delusional was made."
- A transparent release experiment page updated with real results.
- Press mentions and interviews consolidated into an authority page.

Add relevant MusicAlbum, MusicRecording, Person, Article, FAQPage, and Breadcrumb structured data only when the visible page supports it. Keep artist naming, bios, links, locations, collaborators, and release facts consistent everywhere. Do not generate fake reviews, invented quotes, or unapproved lyrics.

## Measurement and decisions

Track by platform and content ID:

- Three-second hold or chose-to-view rate.
- Average watch time and completion rate.
- Saves and shares per reached viewer.
- Profile visits and follows.
- Site clicks, preview plays, gate opens, and confirmed signups.
- Verified pre-save or DSP clicks once those links exist.
- Comment quality and recurring audience names.
- Production time and any paid cost, including editing and creator fees.

Use the account's own four-week history as the first benchmark. Use multiple attribution views once paid activity exists: platform-reported, last-click web analytics, and blended spend-to-fan-action. Never mix incompatible windows into one claimed truth.
