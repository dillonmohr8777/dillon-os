# Homepage Surface

## Mode

Persuade

## Job

Convince a practical business leader that With Not For turns AI access into real adoption by building systems around the team's actual workflows and teaching people to use them.

## Primary action

Work With Us → `/contact`

## Secondary action

See What We Do → `/services`

## Proof sequence

1. The `WNF.AI` shorthand becomes a tangible robot collaborator in a human hand.
2. The exact positioning line establishes the human-with-AI premise.
3. The adoption-problem argument explains why tools alone fail.
4. The current service pillars show how With Not For closes the gap.
5. A practical briefing-agent example makes implementation concrete without claiming a client result.
6. The closing conversation CTA lowers the commitment threshold.

## Chosen direction

Use the current Apple Mac mini page as the approved motion and layout reference at roughly three quarters of its total narrative length. Match its opening whitespace, wordmark scale, sticky scroll pacing, compression into a central product, hand-supported reveal, bold single-idea folds, horizontal highlight rhythm, alternating light and dark fields, and decisive closing CTA. Replace every Apple-specific brand, asset, product, price, and claim with With Not For source material.

## Hero motion contract

1. `WNF.AI` is centered alone on a light field.
2. Letterforms briefly acquire brand-colored material accents without gradient text.
3. The wordmark scales forward, then compresses and drops toward the robot's center.
4. The exact Grok robot resolves at product scale above the existing transparent hand asset.
5. The robot blinks and follows pointer or touch position within a restrained range.
6. The hero hands off to “AI that works with your team, not for them.” and the source CTAs.
7. With reduced motion, the page loads directly into steps 4–6 with no scroll-linked transforms.
