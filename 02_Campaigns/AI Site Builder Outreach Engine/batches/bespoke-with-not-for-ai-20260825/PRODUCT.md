# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

The primary audience is a business owner, operations leader, or team lead who knows AI matters but has not turned scattered tool access into dependable adoption. They are evaluating whether With Not For can understand the way their team actually works, build the right systems, and help people use them. A secondary audience is a practical internal AI champion who needs a credible implementation partner and a clear way to explain the work to leadership.

## Product Purpose

This private homepage concept demonstrates how With Not For can present its existing offer with a sharper, product-grade experience before a prospect call. Success means a visitor quickly understands that the company builds AI systems businesses actually use, sees how strategy, systems, training, and policy connect, and chooses to start a conversation.

## Positioning

AI that works with your team, not for them.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: **Work With Us**, linking to the existing contact route.
- Secondary CTA: **See What We Do**, linking to the existing services route.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: **AI that works with your team, not for them.**
- Belief ladder: most businesses have an adoption problem rather than an AI-access problem; signing up for tools is not a strategy; useful AI must be built around real workflows and paired with training and policy; With Not For provides that end-to-end implementation without agency layers; the first conversation is practical and low commitment.
- Proof on hand: the current With Not For homepage copy and official logo, plus the existing Grok-produced Claude robot asset supplied by Dillon. The current source does not present quantified case-study proof or testimonials, so this concept must not invent them.

## Brand Personality

Direct, pragmatic, and human-controlled. The experience should feel confident enough to be memorable, disciplined enough for an executive audience, and playful only where the robot makes the human-with-AI premise tangible.

## Anti-references

Do not make this look like a generic AI SaaS template, a neon cyberpunk robot page, or a card-filled agency theme. Do not copy Apple trademarks, Apple product media, Apple copy, or Apple navigation branding. Do not let the opening choreography obscure the real With Not For message. Do not invent clients, results, metrics, testimonials, product capabilities, or guarantees.

## Design Principles

1. **Practice the positioning.** The human remains visibly in control; the robot is a collaborator, not an autonomous replacement.
2. **Make one opening unforgettable.** Concentrate motion into the WNF.AI-to-robot sequence and keep the rest of the page calm.
3. **Use proof before decoration.** Existing copy, services, and practical workflow examples carry the page; visuals clarify them.
4. **Translate, do not costume.** Borrow the Mac mini page's pacing, spatial logic, and product-reveal choreography while preserving With Not For's identity.
5. **No fictional confidence.** Every claim, CTA, and service description must be traceable to the current source.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Meet WCAG 2.2 AA contrast and interaction expectations. The full story must work with keyboard navigation, touch, browser zoom, and a 320px-wide viewport. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` by showing the complete hero end state immediately and removing scroll-linked choreography without hiding content.
