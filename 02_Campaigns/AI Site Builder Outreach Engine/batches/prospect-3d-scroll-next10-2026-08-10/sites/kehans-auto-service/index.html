The bay

It arrives as a symptom, not a diagnosis.

A piston rises out of the dark first. The noise, the light on the dash, the pull to one side — the bay's first job is translating symptoms into causes.

Opened

Honest repair starts with showing the part.

The belt and hardware swing into the light. The difference between a repair and an invoice is whether the customer gets to see what was actually worn.

Visual confidence should sharpen the next question — not disguise what still needs verification.
Torque

Tight is a number, not a feeling.

The wrench arrives last and set to spec. Fasteners at the right torque are the quiet difference between fixed and fixed-for-now.

  • Ask to see the worn part
  • Ask what fails next if this is ignored
  • Ask what the shop would do on their own car

The sequence

Six honest steps between a symptom and a running car.

Auto repair is diagnosis, parts, and torque — in that order, with the customer in the loop.

01

Interview

When it happens, what it sounds like, what changed. The owner knows more than they think.

02

Reproduce

The shop confirms the symptom before selling the cause.

03

Diagnose

Test, measure, isolate. Parts-cannon repair is just expensive guessing.

04

Estimate

The cause, the fix, the number — agreed before the wrench moves.

05

Repair

Right part, right torque, old part in the box for the customer.

06

Verify

Road-tested against the original complaint, not just started in the lot.
