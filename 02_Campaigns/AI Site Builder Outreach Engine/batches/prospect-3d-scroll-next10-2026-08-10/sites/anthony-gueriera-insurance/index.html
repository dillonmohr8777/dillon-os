Layers

Coverage arrives in overlapping sheets.

Paper planes settle around the key, one exposure at a time. Independent agents work like this: match the layer to the risk, not the risk to a single carrier's shelf.

Visual confidence should sharpen the next question — not disguise what still needs verification.
The gaps

What is not written is not covered.

The last sheet closes the set. The difference between agents is in the questions asked before binding — the exclusions found early, not discovered at claim time.

  • Ask what the policy excludes, in plain words
  • Ask what changed since last renewal
  • Ask which claims scenarios were priced in
