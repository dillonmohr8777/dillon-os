The sequence

Six lookups between a symptom and the right box.

A parts counter is a search engine with a person attached. The discipline is in the questions.

01

Identify

VIN first. Trim levels lie; the number does not.

02

Diagnose

The failed part is sometimes the victim. Ask what killed it.

03

Cross-reference

OEM number to aftermarket equivalents, grade by grade.

04

Grade

Economy, standard, premium — matched to the vehicle's remaining life.

05

Verify

Measure the old part against the new before anyone drives away.

06

Back it

A part is only as good as the counter standing behind it.
