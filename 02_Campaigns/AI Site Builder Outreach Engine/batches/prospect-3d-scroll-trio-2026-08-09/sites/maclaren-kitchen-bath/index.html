The sequence

Six stages between a drawing and a finished room.

Every stage below inherits the errors of the one before it. That is why the order matters more than any single material choice.

01

Survey

Measured openings, service runs, and which walls are actually square. Assumptions made here get paid for later.

02

Layout

Clearances, door swings and working distances resolved before a single box is ordered.

03

Carcass

Cabinet boxes set and shimmed dead level, because every surface above them inherits this line.

04

Stone

Templated after install, never before. Seam placement decided where it will be least seen.

05

Hardware

Pulls, hinges and reveals aligned to one common line across the whole run.

06

Light

Under-cabinet and pendant light aimed last, once there are real finishes to reflect it.
