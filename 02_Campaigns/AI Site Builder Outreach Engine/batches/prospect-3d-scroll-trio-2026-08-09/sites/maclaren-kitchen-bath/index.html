The sequence

Six decisions that turn separate materials into one room.

The work becomes more precise at every stage. A clear sequence protects the layout, the finish, and the details that make the room feel effortless.

01

Survey

Measure openings, service runs, and wall conditions before the design asks the room to do too much.

02

Layout

Resolve clearances, door swings, and working distances before a single cabinet is ordered.

03

Carcass

Set the cabinet structure level so every surface above it begins from a dependable line.

04

Stone

Template after installation and place seams where the finished room can absorb them quietly.

05

Hardware

Align pulls, hinges, and reveals to a shared line across the full run.

06

Light

Aim task and pendant light against the real finishes that will reflect it.
