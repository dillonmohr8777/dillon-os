The sequence

Six decisions that give raw material a lasting form.

The character of a finished piece is shaped long before the final polish. Each decision protects what the material can become.

01

Rough

What the material actually is, before anyone decides what it should become.

02

Orientation

Where the table sits relative to the inclusions you cannot cut out.

03

Cut

Angle, table, and girdle create the geometry that guides light through the stone.

04

Setting

Metal closed onto stone tightly enough to hold it and lightly enough to show it.

05

Finish

Polish, plating or none, and whether tool marks get removed or left as evidence.

06

Wear

How the piece behaves after a year of being worn, not after an hour of being photographed.
