Company line

Eighty years of temperature-control equipment, built to spec.

Union Chill Mat Company has built temperature-control equipment since 1946, starting with cooling mats for steel mills before expanding into the Red Rocket radiant heater, the Clayton Fan, and the Ultramatic indirect-fired heater for construction and aviation work. Today the line covers vented and ducted heaters, portable air conditioning from 1 to 50 tons, generators, dehumidifiers, and industrial fans, available to purchase, rent, lease, or refurbish out of the Zelienople Industrial Park.

Company history

From steel-mill cooling mats to a full equipment line.

Union Chill Mat Company started in 1946 producing cooling mats for steel mills. That work led to the Red Rocket radiant heater and the Clayton Fan for industrial use, then the Ultramatic indirect-fired heater built for construction and aviation sectors. In the 1980s the company added portable air conditioning systems to the line. The equipment ships from the Zelienople Industrial Park in Zelienople, Pennsylvania, and serves industrial and commercial customers including aircraft manufacturers, power plants, and steel mills. Every product line is available to purchase, rent, lease, or have refurbished rather than only sold new.

Questions

Straight answers on equipment and terms.

Can equipment be rented instead of purchased?

Yes. Union Chill Mat Company offers its heaters, air conditioners, generators, and dehumidifiers for purchase, rent, lease, or refurbishment.

What size air conditioning systems are available?

Portable A/C systems are available from 1 to 50 tons of capacity, sized to the job.

What kind of heat does the Ultramatic provide?

The Ultramatic is a vented, indirect-fired, ducted heater that provides safe, 100% uncontaminated heat.
