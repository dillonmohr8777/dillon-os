Who supplies who

Built To Stock The Dealer Trade, Not The Retail Counter

Nolt's Auto Parts #2 in Denver, PA is one of nine Nolt's Factory Warehouse locations that has been, in the company's own words, "steadily growing to serve the automotive dealer since 1959." The stores carry ignition parts, brake components, filters, batteries, cooling system parts, and chassis parts from a long list of manufacturer lines, plus a hard-to-find parts service for less common requests. The Denver store is part of a network stretching from Ephrata to Lancaster, Manheim, New Holland, Myerstown, Quarryville, Elizabethtown, and Harrisburg.

What we stock

Product Lines

Ignition & Electrical

Brake Parts

About Nolt's

Nine Stores, One Dealer-Trade Focus

Nolt's Auto Parts #2 sits at 1016 Dry Tavern Road in Denver, PA, one of nine stores in the Nolt's Factory Warehouse network that has been serving the automotive dealer trade since 1959. The flagship warehouse and call center is in Ephrata, with sister stores in Manheim, New Holland, Myerstown, Quarryville, Lancaster, Elizabethtown, and Harrisburg.

The company stocks a broad manufacturer line card covering ignition and electrical parts, brakes, filters, batteries, cooling and climate parts, and chassis and suspension components, plus a hard-to-find parts line for requests outside the standard catalog. The business is built around supplying shops and dealers rather than walk-in retail, with a parts catalog available on request.
