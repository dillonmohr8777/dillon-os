A century in Media

One dealership, every department a car needs.

Weathers Motors has operated as a family-owned dealership in Media, PA since 1922, over a century of buying, selling, and servicing cars, trucks, vans, and SUVs. The dealership runs as a full-service operation: quality pre-owned vehicle sales, a service and repair center, a parts center, tire sales, PA state inspection, a body shop, and in-house financing for shoppers across the credit spectrum. Remote options include video walkarounds, at-home test drives, home delivery, and maintenance pick-up within a 15-mile radius. The team serves Media and surrounding Delaware County, with customers coming from as far as West Chester to Allentown.

What the dealership runs

Six departments under one roof.

Pre-Owned Vehicle Sales

Quality pre-owned cars, trucks, vans, and SUVs across domestic and import brands. Every vehicle is inspected before it's offered for sale.

Service & Repair Center

Oil changes, brake work, tire rotations, alignments, and general auto and truck repair. The shop services all makes and models since the dealership isn't tied to one brand.

State inspection and tire sales and service done on-site, part of keeping vehicles road-ready year-round.

Body Shop

Financing

Who we are

A dealership that never changed hands outside the family.

Weathers Motors has been a family-owned and operated dealership in Media, PA since 1922, over a century of serving Delaware County. The dealership buys, sells, and services cars, trucks, vans, and SUVs across domestic brands like Ford, Chevy, and GMC alongside imports like Honda, Hyundai, and Kia. Because the shop doesn't sell one particular brand, its service experts work on all makes and models, backed by state-of-the-art equipment and ongoing technician training.

The service department earned the Federated Dealer of the Year Award for its work. Every pre-owned vehicle passes inspection before it reaches the lot, and Carfax One Owner vehicles are available for buyers who want that added history. The dealership's finance center works with shoppers across the credit spectrum, and remote services — video walkaround, at-home test drive, home delivery, and maintenance pick-up — are available within a 15-mile radius for customers who can't make it into Media.
