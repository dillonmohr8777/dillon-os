Every season, one crew

A furnace or AC failure never picks a convenient time.

Heating and cooling systems in Cumberland County work hard through hot summers and cold winters, and when a furnace or AC unit fails it rarely picks a convenient time. F M Berkheimer serves homes and businesses around Mechanicsburg with repair, maintenance, and installation for the systems that keep a house comfortable year-round. The approach is straightforward: diagnose the actual problem, explain the options, and do the work right the first time.

What we do

Repair, maintenance, and installation

Furnace Repair & Service

Diagnostic and repair work for furnaces that are short-cycling, blowing cold air, or failing to start, with straightforward explanations of what's wrong before any work begins.

AC Repair & Service

Air conditioning repair and tune-ups for homes and businesses, addressing weak airflow, refrigerant issues, and units that won't cool on the hottest days.

System Installation & Replacement

Installation of new furnaces, air conditioners, and full HVAC systems for homeowners upgrading from an aging or failing unit.

Preventive Maintenance

Seasonal tune-ups and inspections designed to catch small issues before they turn into a mid-season breakdown.

Indoor Air Quality

Filtration, humidity control, and ventilation options for households looking to improve the air moving through their HVAC system.
