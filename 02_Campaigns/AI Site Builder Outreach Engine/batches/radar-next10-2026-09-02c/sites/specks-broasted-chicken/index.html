Our Story

Family owned and operated since 1953.

In 1953, Stanley B. Landis, known as Speck, started the original Speck's Drive-In on Ridge Pike in Limerick on the idea of Pipin' Hot Sandwiches and good, affordable fast food, years before fast-food franchises defined the American landscape. After a fire at the Big Pixie location at Ridge and Germantown Pikes, Speck acquired the property and ran both locations.

The Limerick spot changed hands over the years, but the Collegeville location has stayed in the family, with Speck's children, their spouses, and his grandchildren holding roles over the decades. Today Speck's son Randy still owns the restaurant and is heavily involved in daily operations.

The restaurant remains known for its sandwiches, with the Broasted chicken and homemade coleslaw as the primary draw. Speck's is a Collegeville landmark and often the first stop for people coming back to the area.
