Our story, plainly told

Since 1992, the team at Golden Sea has strived to provide dear patrons with exceptional Chinese cuisine prepared with the freshest ingredients and delivered with smiling service. Family owned by Jack and Amy Chung, the restaurant's chef, Ka Yam Yip, an experienced chef from Hong Kong, spices up the regular menu with recurring specials. Golden Sea was voted Best Chinese Restaurant by The Reporter and The Times Herald.

From our menu

Five sections, straight off the printed menu

Special Appetizers

Starters straight off their own menu: egg rolls, shrimp toast, spring rolls, chicken lettuce wraps, and B.B.Q. spare ribs. A short list built for sharing before the main course.

Chow Mein

Served with dry noodles and rice in chicken, roast pork, shrimp, beef, and vegetable versions, plus the house Golden Sea Special Chow Mein. A menu staple since the restaurant opened.

Lo Mein

Soft noodles in vegetable, roast pork, chicken, beef, shrimp, and a House Special version. Ordered by the pint or quart, their own menu's terms.

Sweet & Sour

Sweet & Sour Pork, Chicken, or Shrimp, served with rice. A dine-in and take-out standard on their printed menu.

Chef's Suggestions

Chef Ka Yam Yip's own specials, including Golden Sea Special Chicken, Seafood Special, Four Seasons, and Szechuan Seafood Deluxe. Their menu calls this section out by name.

About Golden Sea

Serve good, fresh food and treat people well

Golden Sea opened in 1992, run by Jack and Amy Chung, who first opened a restaurant called Golden Light in Horsham before moving to Blue Bell. Jack came from mainland China by way of Hong Kong and Newark; Amy escaped from Canton by sea in 1974. Their son Andrew grew up in the business and often serves as the restaurant's spokesperson.

Chef Ka Yam Yip, trained in Hong Kong, spices up the regular menu with recurring specials like Soft-Shell Crabs and whole Chinese Style Sea Bass. Jack Chung sums up the philosophy plainly: serve good, fresh food and treat people well, and the rest follows. Golden Sea has been voted Best Chinese Restaurant by The Reporter and The Times Herald.
