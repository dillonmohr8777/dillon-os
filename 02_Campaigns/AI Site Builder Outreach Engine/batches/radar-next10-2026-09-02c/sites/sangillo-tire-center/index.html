What we do

Tire sales and service, plain and simple

Tire Sales

Sangillo's sells most major brands of tires for automobiles and light trucks. If a size isn't in stock, they can normally get it within 24 hours.

Tire Rotation

Rotating tires on a regular basis saves money through more mileage and fewer replacements. Staff check for damage and proper inflation at every rotation.

Tire Repair

In many cases Sangillo's is able to repair a tire instead of replacing it. The shop inspects for punctures, cracks, and uneven wear first.

Wheel Balancing

Every tire purchase includes balancing on state-of-the-art computer balancing machines. Improperly balanced tires cause vibration that increases with speed.

Free Tire Disposal

Every tire purchase comes with free disposal of the old tire. Sangillo's recycles its scrap tires rather than sending them to landfill.

About Sangillo Tire Center

Sangillo Tire Center is a family owned and operated tire shop on MacDade Blvd in Folsom, serving the Delaware Valley for more than 60 years. The shop sells most major tire brands for cars and light trucks and can usually source a size that isn't in stock within 24 hours. Every rotation includes a check for tire damage and proper inflation, and every purchase includes computer wheel balancing plus free disposal of the old tire. In many cases the team can repair a tire instead of selling a new one, and scrap tires are recycled rather than landfilled. The shop is open six days a week: Monday through Friday from 8:00 to 5:00 and Saturday mornings until noon, closed Sundays.
