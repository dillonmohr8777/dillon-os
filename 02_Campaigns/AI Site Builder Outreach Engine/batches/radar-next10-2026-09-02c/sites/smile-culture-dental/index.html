Who we are

Kindness, comfort, and transparency, in that order.

Smile Culture Dental in Huntingdon Valley is built around three words: kindness, comfort, and transparency. The team, led by Dr. Harshil Boghara and Dr. Kushal Parikh alongside general dentists, an orthodontist, and specialists, offers everything from routine exams and cleanings to implants, veneers, Invisalign, and emergency care. Every PPO insurance plan is accepted, and virtual consultations are available for patients who want to start the conversation before coming in. The practice was recognized among Top Dentists 2025.

Our team

Led by doctors who trained for this.

Smile Culture Dental’s Huntingdon Valley team is led by Dr. Harshil Boghara, CEO and Clinical Director, a Temple University Kornberg School of Dentistry graduate with advanced training in cosmetic dentistry and implantology, recognized as a Top Doctor in Philadelphia for four consecutive years. Co-Owner Dr. Kushal Parikh graduated Summa Cum Laude from Temple with the Dean’s Award for Clinical Excellence in Patient Care and holds advanced implantology and full-mouth rehabilitation certification. General dentists Dr. Tony Tharakan and Dr. Miraj Dudhat and orthodontist Dr. Adam Taee, a top Invisalign provider since 2017 with a research fellowship at Harvard School of Dental Medicine, round out the clinical team.

The office describes its approach as bringing transparency, technology, and trust together, with a philosophy that merging cutting-edge technology with a highly trained team is the key to delivering top-tier dentistry in a warm, welcoming environment. Located at 2150 E County Line Rd. alongside Smile Culture’s Trooper, Trevose, Glen Mills, and Fairless Hills offices, and a member of the Pennsylvania Dental Association, American Dental Association, and International Congress of Oral Implantologists.
