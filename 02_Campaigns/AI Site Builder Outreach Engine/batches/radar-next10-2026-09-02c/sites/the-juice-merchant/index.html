Fresh, simple, made to order

Clean food, built for regulars

The Juice Merchant is a juice bar in Narberth built around fresh, simple food: cold-pressed juices, smoothies, and bowls made to order for anyone looking for something clean on the way to work or after a workout. The menu is built for regulars who want the same reliable order every time, and for anyone trying a cleanse or a reset for the first time.

On the menu

Five ways to order fresh

Cold-Pressed Juices

Fresh cold-pressed juice blends made from fruits and vegetables, pressed to order rather than sitting on a shelf.

Smoothies

Fruit and vegetable smoothies blended to order, built for a quick breakfast, a post-workout refuel, or an afternoon pick-me-up.

Juice & Smoothie Bowls

Thick smoothie bases topped with fruit, granola, and other toppings for a sit-down or grab-and-go bowl.

Cleanses

Catering

How it's made

Cold-press, simply

Whole fruit and vegetables go in, get pressed under weight instead of blasted with heat or a fast-spinning blade, and come out as juice with nothing added and nothing cooked out.
