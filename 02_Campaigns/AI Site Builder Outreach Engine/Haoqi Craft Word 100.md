# Haoqi Craft Word 100

**Summary:** 100 radar businesses with a one- or two-word lowercase glass line, scored from all 1,042 rows on 2026-08-16.

Nothing here is outbound-ready. A human approves every send.

## The rule

One or two words. Lowercase Pacifico, same cut as `hello`. The line sits in a reserved slot in the back. Headlines, body, stickers, and photos do not share that slot.

The line is the feeling after the visit. `smile more` for a dentist. `stay cool` for HVAC. `come home` for a vet. A catalog phrase like window and wall units is still a skip.

## How the 100 was cut

Scanned 1042 registry rows. 780 had a line at quality 84+. 262 were skipped. The 100 is score-sorted (line quality + opportunity + rebuild bonus) with a cap so dentists and restaurants do not eat the whole list: 16 `smile more`, 8 `come hungry`, 15 of most others.

Regenerate: `node _os/automation/bin/haoqi-craft-words.js`
