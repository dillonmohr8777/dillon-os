# Haoqi Craft Word 100

**Summary:** 100 radar businesses with one honest lowercase glass word, scored from all 1,042 rows on 2026-08-16.

Nothing here is outbound-ready. A human approves every send.

## The rule

One word. Lowercase Pacifico, same cut as `hello`. It sits in a reserved slot in the back. Headlines, body, stickers, and photos do not share that slot.

The word is what they do, not a service phrase. `smile` for a dentist. `paws` for a vet. `sight` for an eye shop. HVAC, insurance, lawyers, and roofing are skips. Jarman has no craft word.

## How the 100 was cut

Scanned 1042 registry rows. 583 had a word at quality 84+. 459 were skipped. The 100 is score-sorted (word quality + opportunity + rebuild bonus) with a cap so dentists do not eat the whole list: 18 `smile`, 8 `taste`, 12 of most others.

Regenerate: `node _os/automation/bin/haoqi-craft-words.js`
