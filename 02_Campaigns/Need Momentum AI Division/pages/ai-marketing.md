# More tests. Same brand.

NeedMomentum already runs Google and Meta for local businesses. This offer is the production layer on top: more angles, more creative, same tracking, a human still hits publish.

The ad itself doesn't say "AI-powered." People came for the offer, not the toolchain.

[Talk through last week's tests](#contact)

## How the work runs

1. **Lock the offer.** What you sell, who it's for, what a lead is worth.
2. **Draft variants.** Angles, copy, and creative options from the brief. AI first pass.
3. **Human edit.** Brand, claims, banned terms, platform policy.
4. **Ship with tracking that fires.** Conversion events you can defend in a report.
5. **Log the tests.** What ran, what lost, what we do next week.
6. **Disclose when the law or the platform requires it.** Generated people in ads, photoreal media labels. A person still owns the checklist.

## Who this is for

Accounts already in Google or Meta with NeedMomentum, or a site rebuild that now needs traffic. Home services and medical first.

## What's in / out

**In:** offer matrix, edited variants, working conversion tracking, weekly test log, disclosure checklist, a named AM.

**Out:** turning on Meta's default generator and calling it strategy, "AI" in the customer-facing headline, replacing the AM, spend changes fired from a skill with no human.

## Pricing

Addon to the ads retainer. Mac sets the delta. We don't invent a new $5k SKU that is the same hours with a new label.
