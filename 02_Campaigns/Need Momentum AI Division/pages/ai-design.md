# A site that looks like your business, not like last year's template

If the website hasn't been rebuilt in years, Google and customers both treat it as stale. NeedMomentum harvests what you already have (photos, words, colors) and ships a page that could only belong to you.

[See how a rebuild is scoped](#contact)

## Two speeds

**Volume rebuild.** Shared architecture, your brand tokens, your photography. Fast enough for outreach and for trades that need a modern homepage now.

**Bespoke.** Own layout, type, and motion. Reserved for after you engage, or for franchise and multi-location work that can't share a skeleton.

AI is the production system. The live homepage never says "AI-designed." Customers didn't ask for that.

## How the work runs

1. **Grade the current site.** If it's already strong, we route you to ads and SEO instead of a wasted rebuild.
2. **Harvest.** Your site, your socials, your palette, your lingo. Facts we can't verify stay blank.
3. **Build.** Semantic page, mobile-first, real images, Maps and contact that are true.
4. **QA.** Contrast, overflow, alts, unique photos. Then a human looks at it.
5. **Handoff.** You own the repo and the CMS path. Demos stay private and `noindex` until you say go live.

## Who this is for

Operators with a dated site, real photography (or a shoot we can do in Philadelphia), and a vertical we already serve: home services, medical, legal, wellness, industrial.

## What's in / out

**In:** harvest, rebuild or demo, QA, sourced facts, `noindex` until you own it.

**Out:** indexing a demo of a business we don't work for, invented addresses, the same template dropped on 25 competitors with a hue shift, "generated in an afternoon" as the pitch.

## Named stack

NeedMomentum site factory: harvest from your live site, locked colors and type, human QA, handoff on your hosting or CMS. Claude drafts copy. A designer still looks at the page. The live homepage never says "AI-designed."

## FAQ

**Is this a template?** Volume work shares a skeleton so we can move. Tokens, photos, and copy are yours. Bespoke work doesn't share a skeleton.

**Will you put a demo of my business on Google?** No. Previews stay `noindex` until you hire us and we launch.

**Why not just generate it?** Speed is the factory. Taste is the gate. A page that could wear a competitor's logo isn't done.
