## SEO still sits underneath

Two scoreboards, one program:

- Google: rankings, GBP, Search Console
- Citations: whether named prompts still return you, plus AI referral traffic in analytics when it exists

## How the work runs

1. **Diagnostic.** Prospect Radar on the live site, GBP, and entity consistency. Named prompt list from questions customers actually ask.
2. **Fix the foundation.** Crawl, mobile, NAP, LocalBusiness schema for rich results, pages that answer like a person who did the job.
3. **Ship non-commodity pages.** First-hand process, local proof, clear authors. No one URL per keyword variation.
4. **Measure.** Search Console generative AI report, GA AI referrals, monthly re-test of the prompt list.
5. **Human QA.** Nothing publishes until an account manager reads it.
6. **90 days, then decide.** Keep, expand, or stop. No 12-month lock before proof.
