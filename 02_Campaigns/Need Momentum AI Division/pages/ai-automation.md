# Follow up while the lead is still on the site

People who request a quote and hear nothing go to the next tab. NeedMomentum builds the intake, the routing, and the first reply in the CRM you already pay for. Staff still set the rules and handle the edge cases.

[Schedule an automation walkthrough](#contact)

## How the work runs

1. **Map the funnel.** Goals, offers, who should get the lead, what "qualified" means.
2. **Audit the stack.** CRM, forms, ads, email, SMS, tracking. Gaps first.
3. **Build the paths.** Triggers, segments, messages, and routing for each stage.
4. **QA.** Every path, tagging, consent, and the report the owner will actually open.
5. **Launch.** Watch the first runs. Fix timing and routing in the first week.
6. **Iterate.** Copy, rules, and steps against the numbers, not against a vibe.
