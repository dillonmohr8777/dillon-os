# Claims sheet

What NeedMomentum may say about AI work. What it may not. FTC substantiation still applies ([Keep your AI claims in check](https://www.ftc.gov/business-guidance/blog/2023/02/keep-your-ai-claims-check), 2023-02-27). Using a model in production doesn't make the *service* an "AI product."

## Allowed (with human review in the sentence)

- "AI-assisted production with human review."
- "We draft variants, then an account manager edits and publishes."
- "The chatbot is software. A person handles exceptions."
- Kind Insurance numbers exactly as published: 5 qualified conversations, 1 policy, 30 days, agency-reported.
- "Packages from $1,500 to $3,000 plus setup" on Automation, matching the live FAQ, until Mac changes it.

## Forbidden

- Citation guarantees ("we'll get you into ChatGPT").
- "AI-powered results" with no metric.
- "Better than a non-AI agency" with no proof.
- Invented metrics, invented testimonials, invented client counts.
- Presenting the Cedar Ridge Radar fixture as a real client.
- "AI-designed" on a client's live homepage.
- 12-month lock as the first offer.

## If someone asks "do you use AI?"

Yes, in production, with a named person who can stop a publish. Name the stack for that engagement (Claude, HubSpot, Google Ads, Meta). Don't say "leading AI tools."
