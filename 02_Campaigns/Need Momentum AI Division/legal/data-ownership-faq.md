# Data and ownership FAQ

Copy onto each AI service page (short form) and into the SOW (long form).

## Who owns the accounts?

You do. Google Ads, Meta, Search Console, GA4, CRM, domain, and inboxes stay on your logins. NeedMomentum works as a user you can revoke.

## Where do prompts go?

Only to the vendors listed in the SOW, on the commercial tier named there. Client confidentials don't go into a personal ChatGPT account.

## Do you train models on our data?

No public-model training on your confidentials. If a vendor can't contract that, we don't use that vendor on your work.

## What happens if we leave?

You keep the accounts, the site files we handed over, and the CRM records. We don't hold the keys.

## Can we see the tools?

Yes. We'll name Claude, HubSpot, Google, Meta, or whatever that engagement actually uses. "Leading AI tools" isn't an answer.
