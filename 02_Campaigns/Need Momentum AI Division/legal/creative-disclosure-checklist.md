# Creative disclosure checklist

Run before any ad with generated or heavily altered media ships. Not a website footer. Not a duty to stamp "AI" on every blog post.

## Always

- [ ] Claims match the claims sheet.
- [ ] A named AM approved the final.
- [ ] No invented metrics in the creative.
- [ ] Brand and banned-term lists for that client were checked.

## Generated or photoreal people

- [ ] NY synthetic-performer rule: if the ad can run in New York and shows a digital human who isn't a recognizable real person, disclose conspicuously. In force 2026-06-09.
- [ ] Meta / TikTok / YouTube labels for photoreal generated video or audio, when those products require them.
- [ ] Don't imply a real person endorsed the business unless they did.

## Chat / SMS / site bot

- [ ] First message discloses the visitor is talking to software.
- [ ] Escalation to a person exists.
- [ ] Consent and CRM fields match the client's policy.

## Don't

- Label every AI-assisted blog as an "AI ad."
- Bury the only disclosure in a site footer.
- Turn on platform default generators without the AM's say-so.
