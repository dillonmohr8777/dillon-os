# AI addendum (draft for counsel)

Counsel rewrites this. Don't paste it into a live SOW.

Standing addendum for NeedMomentum / Momentum 360 AI-assisted work. Per-engagement tool list lives in the SOW, not here.

## 1. Tools used this engagement (SOW)

The statement of work lists each model, platform, and vendor tier (example: Claude via Anthropic commercial terms, HubSpot, Google Ads, Meta Ads). Consumer ChatGPT on a personal account is not an allowed path for client confidentials.

## 2. Human review

A named NeedMomentum person reviews and accepts client-facing output before it publishes or sends. Automated paths (chat, SMS, email) stay inside the workflows the client approved. Exceptions route to a person.

## 3. IP

Client owns deliverables specified in the SOW (copy, site files, ad accounts, CRM records) on payment, same as non-AI work. Portions generated by a model are assigned to the client to the extent the vendor terms allow. NeedMomentum doesn't claim model weights or third-party platform IP.

## 4. Training data

Client confidentials aren't used to train public models. NeedMomentum uses vendor tiers that contract against training on customer content where those tiers exist. If a tool can't make that promise, it stays off the SOW.

## 5. Accuracy and liability

Model output can be wrong. NeedMomentum is responsible for the human review it promised, not for a model's unreviewed hallucination. Client remains responsible for regulated claims in their industry (insurance, medical, legal, cannabis) after they approve copy.

## 6. Chat and agents

If a visitor or lead talks to software, the first message discloses that. EU AI Act Art. 50 (in force 2026-08-02) is the reference for in-scope deployers. Same disclosure is the default in Philadelphia work.

## 7. Ads with generated people

New York GBL §396-b (synthetic performer disclosure, in force 2026-06-09) applies when an ad uses a digital human who isn't a recognizable real person. Platform labels (Meta, TikTok, YouTube) apply when those products require them. The AM runs the checklist in `creative-disclosure-checklist.md`.

## 8. Accounts and data

Client owns ads, Search Console, GA4, CRM, and domain. NeedMomentum works inside those accounts. Offboarding returns access and doesn't hold data hostage.

## 9. No guarantees of model behavior

NeedMomentum doesn't warrant that any generative engine will cite, rank, or recommend the client.
