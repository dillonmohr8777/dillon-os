# Sample SOW attachment (internal)

Counsel rewrites this. Don't paste it into a live SOW until they do. Standing AI terms live in `legal/ai-addendum-draft.md`. Per-engagement tools go here.

## Header (every AI engagement)

• Client owns ads, Search Console, GA4, CRM, domain, and inboxes.
• Named NeedMomentum reviewer: ________________ (AM).
• Tools this engagement (commercial tiers only): ________________
• Consumer ChatGPT on a personal account is off-limits for confidentials.

## Pick one motion

**Visibility (AEO/GEO).** Paid Radar diagnostic. Named prompt list. 90 days of people-first pages, GBP, and citation measurement. Two scoreboards, one program. No citation guarantee. No `llms.txt` deliverable.

**Design.** Site grade, then harvest and rebuild or a private demo. `noindex` until launch. Homepage never says "AI-designed." Client owns the files on payment.

**Marketing.** Addon to the ads retainer. AI drafts, humans ship. Weekly test log. Disclosure checklist when generated people or photoreal media run. Spend stays in the ads UI.

**Automation.** Funnel map, stack audit, workflows in the client's CRM. Packages currently published $1,500–$3,000 plus setup pending Mac confirm. Chat discloses software at first message. Client keeps the leads.

## 90-day shape

Days 1–14 diagnostic and written scope. Days 15–45 build with human QA. Days 46–75 live and weekly log. Days 76–90 keep / expand / stop.

**Good** is one number written on day 1. Activity reports don't count.

**Exit:** client keeps accounts and files. NeedMomentum keeps the method.
