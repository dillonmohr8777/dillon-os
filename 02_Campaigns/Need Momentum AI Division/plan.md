## Price posture (internal)

Until Mac sets numbers, use only what is already live or clearly marked seller-card analog.

• Automation: live FAQ **$1,500–$3,000 + setup**. Keep the band. Fix the inversion after Mac.
• Visibility: paid Radar, not a free toy. WebFX Bronze $3,000/mo is a Philly software+service floor (seller card, OmniSEO bundled). FPS $2–12k GEO is listicle/PR. Don't copy either as the product.

• Marketing: addon to the ads retainer. Don't invent a $5k label on the same hours.

## What we will not do

• Launch an AI startup, waitlist, or Product Hunt page.
• Guarantee ChatGPT / Overview citations.
• Publish a public exact rate card for unproven SKUs.
• Index a demo of a business we don't work for.
• Auto-send mail, Slack, or CRM writes.
• Sell Dillon's HubSpot agent as a named product before it runs on client accounts.
• Merge a 300-skill dump, camofox, semrush-proxy, or unfiltered gen-media into an offer.
• Put "AI-designed" on a client's live homepage.

## Kill criteria

Stop a SKU if, after one 90-day pilot: no operational number moved, the client won't own the accounts, or counsel blocks the claim we wanted to make. Stop outbound if Mac's taste pass fails the hub or if harvest facts can't be verified.
