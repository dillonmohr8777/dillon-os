# 90-day pilot one-pager

Paid. No 12-month lock. One operational number per pilot. Mac confirms before any client conversation.

## Shape (every SKU)

| Window | What ships |
|---|---|
| Days 1–14 | Diagnostic (Radar, stack audit, or site grade). Scope in writing. |
| Days 15–45 | Build: pages, workflows, or creative tests. Human QA. |
| Days 46–75 | Live. Watch. Fix. Weekly log. |
| Days 76–90 | Review. Keep, expand, or stop. |

**Good** is one number agreed on day 1 (calls answered in 5 minutes, citations on a named prompt list, form-to-CRM with zero manual entry). Activity reports don't count.

**Exit:** client keeps accounts and files. NeedMomentum keeps the method.
