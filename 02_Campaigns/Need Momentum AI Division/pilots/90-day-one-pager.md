# 90-day pilot one-pager

Paid. No 12-month lock. One operational number per pilot. Mac confirms before any client conversation.

## Shape (every SKU)

| Window | What ships |
|---|---|
| Days 1–14 | Diagnostic (Radar, stack audit, or site grade). Scope in writing. Day-1 number locked. |
| Days 15–45 | Build: pages, workflows, or creative tests. Human QA. Client approves anything live. |
| Days 46–75 | Live. Watch. Fix. Weekly log in the client folder. |
| Days 76–90 | Review. Keep, expand, or stop. Client keeps accounts either way. |

**Good** is one number agreed on day 1 (calls answered in 5 minutes, citations on a named prompt list, form-to-CRM with zero manual entry). Activity reports don't count.

**Exit:** client keeps accounts and files. NeedMomentum keeps the method.

## Scoreboard (pick one per pilot)

| SKU | Number | How we know |
|---|---|---|
| Automation | Time-to-first-response | Form/call timestamp vs first human or bot reply in the CRM |
| Design | Site Quality Score + conversion path | Radar/grade before vs after; phone or form above the fold on mobile |
| Visibility | Named prompts + AI referrals | Monthly re-test of the list; Search Console generative AI / GA AI referrals |

Two scoreboards can live on one invoice (Google + citations). Don't add a third acronym fee.

## Kill / keep

**Keep** if the number moved and the client owns the stack.

**Stop** if the number didn't move, logins never arrived, or counsel blocks the claim we wanted to make.
