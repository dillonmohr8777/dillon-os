# Search Terms Review Queue

This queue converts real paid-search queries into explicit, reversible
decisions. Keep every account, client, brand, date range, and conversion
definition separate. This note is an index; detailed exports and decisions live
inside the correct client or campaign record.

## Classification

Use one primary class and record nuance in the reason:

- qualified service or product intent;
- problem, solution, comparison, proof, or local research;
- brand navigation;
- competitor intent;
- wrong company or identity confusion;
- employment, education, DIY, supplier, or vendor intent;
- wrong geography or unsupported service;
- ambiguous and needing more evidence; or
- privacy, policy, reputation, or compliance risk.

## Allowed decisions

- keep and monitor;
- isolate into a tighter ad group;
- add an exact or phrase target;
- test landing-page or ad-message alignment;
- add a campaign-level negative;
- add an account-level negative only when the exclusion is universally valid;
- promote language into [[03_Content/SEO Keyword Targets|organic research]];
- investigate tracking or CRM disposition; or
- defer until evidence is sufficient.

Negative keywords and targeting changes are consequential account writes. Draft
the exact scope and rationale here, verify the target account, and keep execution
behind the applicable approval and access boundary.

## Review protocol

1. Verify account, client, campaign, reporting dates, attribution window, and
   conversion definition.
2. Export actual search terms and preserve the dated source.
3. Join CRM or call disposition when authorized and reliable.
4. Classify query intent without using raw form fills as proof of quality.
5. Draft bounded targets, negatives, page changes, and tracking investigations.
6. Run an independent sanity check for destructive exclusions, brand mixing,
   geographic errors, and overbroad account-level negatives.
7. After approved execution, record the exact change and monitor its outcome.
8. Compile reusable lessons into [[12_Brain/03_Concepts/Keyword Research and Search Demand|Keyword Research and Search Demand]].

## Scorecard

- spend and queries reviewed;
- accepted, excluded, ambiguous, and unresolved query counts;
- negative decisions by scope;
- new high-intent clusters promoted;
- landing-page or ad-message mismatches found;
- tracking investigations opened and resolved;
- qualified opportunity rate by accepted cluster; and
- time from new query signal to verified decision.
