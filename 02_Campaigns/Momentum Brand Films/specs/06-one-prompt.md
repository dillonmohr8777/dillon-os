## Craft notes

- **The opening type run is the signature.** Get the crop and the speed right: letters must
  be too big to read comfortably, moving fast, then decelerate hard into legibility.
  Emphatic easing, one continuous move, no cut.
- Platform names (`GOOGLE`, `CHATGPT`, `MAPS`, `PERPLEXITY`) are **set as plain text pills
  in Momentum's own type** — no third-party logos, no borrowed brand colours, no imitation
  of their marks. This keeps the film clean of other people's trademarks.
- The pills' final resolution into a row under the lockup is the film's structural rhyme.
  Track their positions from beat 2 to the end so the settle reads as *the same pills*.
- No invented ranking claims, no "#1" badges, no fake review counts. The `Your business`
  chip highlighting is enough to make the point.
- Anti-slop watch: three cards fanning out is one careless step from the banned
  three-equal-card row. They must sit at **different depths, sizes and vertical offsets** —
  a lead and two followers, never a symmetric trio.
