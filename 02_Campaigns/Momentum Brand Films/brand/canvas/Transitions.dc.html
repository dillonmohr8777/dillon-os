The transition devices
HOW BEATS BREAK
Four devices, used across all six films so the slate reads as one system rather than six one-offs.
Device 01
TORN WIPE
next beat
A torn paper edge sweeps across and the next beat is already behind it. Replaces every cross-fade.
Device 02
TAPED CARD
11 calls. 9 were real people.
Text boxes arrive taped and slightly off-axis, never centred and square. The tilt is between 1.5 and 3 degrees, never more.
Device 03
BIRDS SCATTER
The engraved birds cut loose from their plate and scatter out of frame, taking the beat with them.
Device 04
PLATE UNDER LENS
A hard circular mask holds still while a strip of plates slides beneath it. The lens never cuts — it travels.
