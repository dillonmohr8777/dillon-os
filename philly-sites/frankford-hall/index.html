The story

Hearty biergarten fare meets laid-back courtyard energy.

Frankford Hall blends German tradition with contemporary Fishtown energy. Rotating sausages, burgers, warm Bavarian pretzels, and schnitzels meet a broad selection of German and American beer in a spacious industrial courtyard built for long afternoons and groups.

Group dining and private-event inquiries are welcome.
