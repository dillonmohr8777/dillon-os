ABOUT US

Named for Roland Kassis' grandmother, Suraya translates family recipes into a modern restaurant, market, and garden. Its hospitality is rooted in community, generosity, and the idea that everyone deserves a seat at the table.

Suraya is named for partner Roland Kassis' grandmother, whose wartime hospitality in Lebanon fed allies and enemies alike
