The story

Roasted with love in Philly

The Rittenhouse Square cafe connects La Colombe's Philadelphia roasting roots with a wide coffee lineup: signature blends, single origins, cold brew, Draft Latte, and seasonal releases, served daily in the heart of Center City.

The brand identifies itself as La Colombe Coffee Workshop and emphasizes that its coffee is roasted in Philadelphia
