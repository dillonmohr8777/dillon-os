The story

In the Italian Market since 1906

Fante's is a small family retailer devoted to specialty goods for the kitchen. For generations, home cooks, professional chefs, and cookbook writers have come here for useful tools, informed help, and discoveries they cannot find everywhere else.

Family owned and operated in Philadelphia's Italian Market since 1906.
