The story

A beloved Center City gathering place

Square 1682 brings modern American cooking, polished cocktails, and an easygoing spirit to the heart of Philadelphia. Meet for brunch, settle in for dinner, or stop by for happy hour before a night in Center City.

Square 1682 is a Center City gathering place at the Hotel Palomar, positioned where Rittenhouse Square meets downtown culture and nightlife.
