The story

Our Story

The original Good Dog Bar fills three stories of a century-old Center City building with food, drinks, dog-friendly outdoor dining, and a third-floor game room. It has been operating since 2003 in a space with watering-hole roots reaching back to Prohibition.

Well-behaved leashed dogs are welcome outdoors, and there is a weekday happy hour at the bar.
