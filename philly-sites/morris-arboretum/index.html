A garden for beauty, discovery, and connection

Morris Arboretum & Gardens brings spectacular trees into harmony with colorful gardens. Its living collections, research, education, and seasonal experiences invite visitors to slow down, look closely, and nurture a deeper connection with the natural world.

The former Compton estate of siblings John and Lydia Morris began in 1887 and opened to the public in 1933.
