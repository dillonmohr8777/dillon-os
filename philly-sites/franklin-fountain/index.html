The story

Our History

Our presentation is elevated by our craftsmanship. Our artistry interprets the forgotten past, while welcoming in the present.

Eric and Ryan Berley opened The Franklin Fountain at 116 Market Street in the last week of summer 2004.

01Eric and Ryan Berley opened The Franklin Fountain at 116 Market Street in the last week of summer 2004.
02The brothers built the concept around craftsmanship, social responsibility and experimentation, drawing inspiration from Benjamin Franklin and turn-of-the-century soda fountains.
03Ice creams, toppings and baked goods are made from scratch by confectioners using high-quality and local ingredients.
04The shop recreates an early American soda fountain with original tile, decorative tin and a bronze-and-onyx fountain from the early 1900s.
