The story

We are glad you are here.

Bartram's Garden is many things to many people: a home for horticulture, an ancient riverfront, an African Diaspora farm, and a place of untold histories. This free public park welcomes everyone to make the land and water their own.

Bartram's Garden stands in Lenapehoking and preserves North America's oldest surviving botanic garden alongside histories that precede, overlap, and follow the Bartram family.
