# Characters

## Darnell Covington
Math prodigy. East Atlanta upbringing, Harvard trajectory. File BI-01. Seventeen, disciplined, observant, private — and suddenly legible to people who confuse promise with property. His excellence does not protect him; it makes him easier to locate. Carries the moral voltage of the novel.

**Portrait:** `social/references/darnell.png`  
**Social set:** Harvard surveillance, inventory desk, campus dusk, cast triptych.

## Javon Whitfield
Harvard-adjacent quarterback gravity from Rosedale, MS. Six-five, 242, state title weight — and a mind nobody gets to reduce to muscle. Darnell's counterweight and witness. Humor as reconnaissance; when the joke dies, consequence has arrived.

**Portrait:** `social/references/javon.png`  
**Social set:** night field after rain, diner booth, lobby TV, campus dusk.

## Dijon Garnier
French billionaire antagonist. Private capital with a cigar, a mansion, a technological alibi, and a brutal appetite for controllable memory. Drives donor politics and the Garnier Dossier lead magnet.

**Portrait:** `social/references/garnier.png`

## Alec Daheim
CIA operative. File D-09, Langley route. Green eyes, empty column, conscience arriving later than it should. Begins as an instrument of surveillance and force; the book keeps him unstable because loyalty is expensive.

**Portrait:** `social/references/alec.png`  
**Social set:** Langley corridor, rain window remorse, control room, cast triptych.

## Ronald McNulty
POTUS · erratic. Reality television ego with executive power and a talent for making collapse sound procedural.

**Portrait:** `social/references/mcnulty.png`
