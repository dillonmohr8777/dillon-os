# Privacy Policy — Mohr Agents

_Last updated: 2026-07-02. Draft — have counsel review before publishing. Host
at a public URL and link it from the App Store listing and the paywall._

Mohr Media ("we") operates the Mohr Agents iOS app. This policy explains what
we collect, why, and your choices.

## What we collect

- **Account identifier.** When you sign in with Apple we receive a stable,
  Apple-provided user identifier (and your email if you choose to share it).
  We use it to associate your subscription and your saved conversations with
  your account. We do not receive your Apple password.
- **Your messages.** The questions you send to an agent, and the business
  details you include, are sent to our backend and to our AI provider
  (Anthropic) to generate a response. We use them only to answer you and to
  operate and improve the service.
- **Subscription status.** We receive purchase and renewal events from Apple
  (via App Store Server Notifications) to know whether your subscription is
  active. We never receive your payment card details — Apple handles billing.
- **Basic diagnostics.** Standard server logs (timestamps, error codes) used
  to keep the service running.

## What we do NOT do

- We do not sell your data.
- We do not use your data for advertising or cross-app tracking.
- We do not store your Apple payment information.

## AI processing

Your messages are processed by Anthropic's API to generate agent responses.
Anthropic processes this data as our subprocessor under their commercial terms
and does not train foundation models on it. See Anthropic's privacy commitments
at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

## Data retention

Conversations and account data are retained while your account is active. You
can request deletion of your account and associated data at any time (see
Contact). We delete or anonymize data when it is no longer needed to provide
the service or meet legal obligations.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live (e.g. GDPR/CCPA), you may have the right to access,
correct, export, or delete your personal data, and to withdraw consent. Contact
us to exercise these rights.

## Children

Mohr Agents is a business tool not directed to children under 13, and we do not
knowingly collect their data.

## Changes

We will update this policy as the service changes and revise the date above.
