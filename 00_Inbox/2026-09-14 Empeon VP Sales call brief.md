# Empeon VP of Sales call brief (14 September 2026)

One page. Read once before the call. The audit PDF is the evidence; this is the conversation.

## The one-line diagnosis of the PDF

It is written for a CFO. He is a VP of Sales. Every number below is the same evidence
re-told as pipeline, meetings, speed, and displacement. Lead with what it means for his reps.

## Open (60 seconds)

"I pulled the live Google result for *healthcare payroll software* this afternoon. Paylocity,
Netchex, ADP, then a Reddit thread where a stranger says 'look at Empeon, that's all they do.'
Your best-ranking asset for your own head term is someone else's comment. Netchex is cited
three times in the AI Overview; Empeon zero. I closed exactly this gap at my last stop, and
I can name the deals it produced."

## The definition I would ask him to sign in week one

| Term | Definition | HubSpot field | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|
| First touch | First recorded session source on the contact | Original source (drill-down 1 and 2) | Marketing |
| Lead creation source | Channel that created the contact record | Lead source (required on create) | Marketing |
| Assist | Named marketing touch inside 30 days before deal creation | Marketing assist (multi-select, dated) | Marketing, sales can add |
| Opportunity source | Who created the deal and from what | Deal source (required, no blank allowed) | Sales |
| Revenue source | Opportunity source of a closed-won deal, locked at close | Deal source at close (read-only) | Sales ops |

Rule: a deal is marketing-sourced only when lead creation source and opportunity source
agree. Everything else is assisted or partner-sourced. Nothing in this table needs spend.

## Speed-to-lead SLA proposal

- Qualified form (named buyer, in-scope industry) → owner assigned automatically, inside 5 minutes.
- First human touch inside 1 business hour; logged call, email, or meeting in HubSpot.
- Unworked after 4 business hours → escalates to the VP.
- Weekly number on his dashboard: hours to first touch, and qualified-form-to-meeting rate.
- Cost of not having it, on the record: 7 hot buyers with no follow-up at Align, plus one
  ChatGPT-referred Chief People Officer with an immediate need who never got a reply.

## Six questions to ask him

1. What share of new pipeline comes through partner reps versus direct, and does he want that mix to move?
2. Average deal size and sales cycle for a skilled nursing or home care operator?
3. From booked demo to close, where do deals stall most?
4. Who works an inbound demo request today, and how fast?
5. What does "marketing-sourced" mean in his forecast right now, and does anyone trust the number?
6. Which competitor does he lose to most often, and on what objection?

## Likely objections

- **"PBJ and HPPD are on our homepage."** True, and I checked today. The sentence that also
  says wage parity and "built for healthcare" in one breath is in the payroll FAQ. The point
  is not presence, it is position: the strongest commercial sentence on the domain is not the
  first thing a buyer reads.
- **"We're partner-led; inbound is small."** Agreed, and the Align data says the same: UKG and
  Dayforce reps sourced $14M of pipeline. The website column was $510K. But $1.91M sat in
  deals with no source at all. Instrumentation decides whether inbound looks small or is small.
- **"SEO takes too long."** The first 30 days are metadata, mobile speed, and CRM fields.
  Those show up in weeks. The four wave-one terms are low volume and near-zero competition;
  "skilled nursing payroll software" is winnable with one page.
- **"You did this at a services firm, not a SaaS vendor."** Same buyer (HR and finance
  leaders at mid-market operators), same CRM, same partner-heavy motion, same measurement
  problem. The difference is that at Empeon I would design the fields before the campaigns.

## The ask

"If I'm the person building demand here, I need two things from sales in the first 30 days:
a signed definition of marketing-sourced pipeline, and one rep who will work inbound on a
clock. Give me those and I'll hand you a named-deal answer to 'what did marketing produce'
inside six months."

## Do not say

- Never claim the $2.19M or the $3.22M was caused by marketing. "Supported" and "touched" only.
- Never imply the $17.65M open pipeline was marketing-generated.
- Leyad is CRM-field evidence, not a traced journey. Weissman is the traced one.
- No rank claims beyond the one live SERP pulled today. No revenue forecast for Empeon.
- Nothing was submitted, published, or changed on empeon.com.
