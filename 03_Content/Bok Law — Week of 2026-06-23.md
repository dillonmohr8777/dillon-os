## Wednesday Wisdom — June 25

**Topic:** Co-parenting schedules when summer plans collide

Summer is here, and for separated parents, that often means two households, two vacation calendars, and one shared goal: your children feeling secure.

When camp weeks, family reunions, and travel plans overlap, tension is normal. What helps is treating the schedule like a business agreement — written, specific, and focused on the kids' experience rather than winning a calendar battle.

A few practical reminders:

- Confirm pickup and drop-off times in writing, even for small changes.
- Build in buffer days between transitions when possible — kids need decompression time.
- If a conflict arises, lead with "What works best for [child's name]?" instead of "That's not fair."

Mediation exists for exactly these moments. You don't have to solve every summer clash alone.
