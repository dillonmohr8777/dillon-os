# SEO Keyword Targets

This is the cross-workspace control surface for source-backed keyword and topic
opportunities. Store the detailed brief in the relevant client, brand, or
campaign folder and link it here. Never blend client query data or performance.

## Intake requirements

Every target entering the queue needs:

- exact client, brand, account, market, and service;
- raw query or customer-language evidence with observation date;
- source label: Search Console, Ads search terms, CRM, calls, forms, Trends,
  Keyword Planner, reviews, sales notes, competitor evidence, or hypothesis;
- intent, funnel stage, entity, geography, and exclusions;
- current URL and proposed URL decision;
- conversion path and qualified business outcome;
- opportunity score with risks; and
- an owner, status, and next review date.

Use [[_templates/Keyword Research Brief|Keyword Research Brief]] for the full
research object and [[_templates/Content Cluster Brief|Content Cluster Brief]]
when a cluster moves into production.

## Opportunity decision

One cluster must produce one explicit decision:

1. update an existing canonical page;
2. create a new primary page;
3. add a supporting article, comparison, case study, glossary, calculator, FAQ,
   video, or local page;
4. create or refine a paid-search target or verified negative; or
5. reject the opportunity because the business cannot serve or prove it.

Score the cluster using business fit, intent strength, evidence strength,
winnability, existing authority, reuse, timing, and a risk penalty. Search
volume alone never determines priority.

## Weekly review

- Reconcile new first-party queries and customer language.
- Separate branded, non-branded, local, informational, transactional,
  employment, DIY, supplier, competitor, and wrong-company demand.
- Check cannibalization and page-query mismatch.
- Promote accepted language into pages, ads, sales enablement, and AI-answer
  monitoring where appropriate.
- Tie outcomes to qualified opportunities, appointments, estimates, contracts,
  revenue, or another explicitly defined result.
- Update [[12_Brain/03_Concepts/Keyword Research and Search Demand|the canonical
  keyword system]] when a reusable lesson is proven.
