## Wednesday Wisdom — July 2

**Topic:** When kids ask hard questions about separation

Children notice more than adults assume. A calendar change, a new bedroom, or a parent who seems quieter can spark questions that feel too big for a Tuesday afternoon.

You don't need perfect answers. What helps is honesty at their level: "We're figuring some things out as a family, and you are safe and loved." Avoid blaming the other parent. Avoid promising outcomes you can't control.

If the questions keep coming, a family therapist or mediator can give you language that protects the child while respecting both households. Asking for help is not failure — it's parenting.

## Turn the Page Thursday — July 3

**Topic:** Independence that doesn't mean isolation

Independence Day weekend is a reminder that freedom and connection can coexist. After a major life change, it's easy to equate "strong" with "alone."

Turning the page doesn't require cutting everyone off. It can mean choosing who gets access to your energy, rebuilding rituals on your terms, and accepting that some friendships will shift while new ones form.

This weekend, one small act of connection — a walk with a friend, a call to someone who listens without fixing — counts as progress.

## Family Fridays — July 4

**Topic:** Pittsburgh summer traditions, reimagined

Fireworks over the Point, neighborhood parades, backyard grills — Pittsburgh summers are built for gathering. If your family looks different this year, you don't have to force last year's script.

A new spot for fireworks, a potluck with chosen family, or a quiet morning before the crowds can become traditions that fit who you are now.

Here's to a safe, warm holiday weekend — however you choose to spend it.
