## Wednesday Wisdom — July 23

**Topic:** School-year custody transitions

August is coming. For co-parents, that means new schedules, new teachers, and new pickup logistics.

Start the conversation now — not the night before the first day:

• Share school supply lists and who is buying what.
• Confirm which address is on file for emergency contacts.
• Agree on who attends orientation or parent night if you can't both go.

Kids pick up on tension fast. A calm, coordinated start to the school year sets the tone for the months ahead.

If logistics feel impossible to align, mediation can help you build a plan before small disagreements become big ones.

## Turn the Page Thursday — July 24

**Topic:** Letting go of the "what if" spiral

"What if I had stayed?" "What if I had left sooner?" "What if the kids would have been better off?"

The what-if spiral keeps you stuck in a version of the past you can't change. It steals energy from the life you're building now.

You made the best decision you could with what you knew at the time. That is enough.

Turning the page doesn't mean pretending the hard parts didn't happen. It means choosing to invest in what comes next instead of rehearsing what might have been.

## Family Fridays — July 25

**Topic:** Back-to-school nerves in split households

Some kids are excited. Some are anxious. Some are both.

When your child moves between homes, back-to-school season can feel like starting over twice — new routines at each house, different rules, different bedtimes.

Help them feel grounded:

• Pack a consistent "school kit" that travels with them (backpack, lunchbox, charger).
• Keep morning routines similar at both homes when possible.
• Ask one simple question each day: "What was the best part of school today?"

Your steadiness matters more than perfect coordination. Pittsburgh families, you've got this.
