## Wednesday Wisdom — July 2

**Topic:** When adult children get involved in a parent's divorce

Adult children are often pulled into their parents' divorce in ways nobody planned. One parent vents. The other asks them to take sides. Siblings stop talking. Family gatherings get awkward fast.

If you're a parent going through divorce: your kids love you both. Keep adult children out of legal strategy, financial details, and conflict. They should not be your therapist or your messenger.

If you're an adult child watching your parents separate: you are allowed to set boundaries. You can love both parents and still refuse to referee their relationship.

Mediation gives families a structured way to separate without turning children into collateral damage, no matter their age.

## Turn the Page Thursday — July 3

**Topic:** Independence beyond the holiday

July Fourth celebrates independence. For many people going through divorce or custody changes, independence means something quieter: choosing peace over winning, clarity over chaos, and a future that fits who you are now.

You do not need anyone's permission to start over. You do need support, a plan, and sometimes a guide who has walked families through this before.

This week, notice one small decision you made for yourself. That counts.

## Family Fridays — July 4

**Topic:** Fourth of July and blended families

Fireworks, cookouts, and long weekends can be joyful or complicated depending on your family structure. Blended families, shared custody, and new partners all add layers.

A few gentle reminders:

• Confirm pickup and drop-off times before the holiday, not during it.
• Let kids enjoy the day without guilt about who they are with.
• New partners: support the kids' relationship with both parents. You're part of the team, not a replacement.
