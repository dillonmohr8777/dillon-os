Payroll isn't just a back-office function. It's the trust layer between your people and your leadership.

When payroll errors stack up — wrong deductions, late deposits, managers approving time they don't understand — employees don't file a ticket. They lose confidence.

Three payroll hygiene habits I recommend every mid-market HR leader audit quarterly:

• **Time-entry ownership** — managers trained, not just given access.
• **Exception workflows** — documented paths for corrections, not ad-hoc Slack fixes.
• **Pre-run reconciliation** — one person signs off before funds move.

Clean payroll is quiet. Messy payroll is loud in all the wrong channels.
