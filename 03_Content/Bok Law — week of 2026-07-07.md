## Wednesday Wisdom — July 9

**Topic:** Mediation vs. litigation: what families should know

Many families assume divorce means a courtroom. It doesn't have to.

**Mediation** puts you and your spouse in the same room (or virtual room) with a neutral mediator who helps you reach agreements on custody, support, and property. You keep control. You move faster. Costs are typically lower.

**Litigation** means a judge decides. That path makes sense when safety is at risk, one party won't negotiate in good faith, or complex assets need court oversight.

Neither path is "winning." The goal is a stable outcome for your family. If you're unsure which fits your situation, a consultation can clarify options before you commit.

## Turn the Page Thursday — July 10

**Topic:** The paperwork nobody warns you about

Divorce isn't only emotional. It's administrative. Bank accounts. Insurance policies. School records. Beneficiary forms you forgot you filled out ten years ago.

Give yourself grace. Make a checklist. Tackle one item per day if that's all you have energy for.

Turning the page includes the boring stuff. Every form you complete is one less loose end holding you back.

## Family Fridays — July 11

**Topic:** Back-to-school prep for co-parents

School supplies lists are already showing up. For co-parents, August planning starts now.

• Agree on who buys what before the Target run gets political.
• Share the school calendar early: open houses, parent nights, sports schedules.
• Tell teachers both parents' contact info if you want equal communication.

A smooth school year starts with a calm July conversation. Your kids will feel the difference.
