## Wed Wisdom — 2026-09-03

**Theme:** Back-to-school custody schedule changes  
**Post (Facebook):**  
When the school year starts, custody schedules often need a reset—not because anyone did something wrong, but because routines change. If you're navigating a parenting plan in Western Pennsylvania, start with what the order actually says about school-year vs. summer time. Write down the pickup/drop-off details that keep getting argued about, then ask your attorney what a reasonable modification request looks like before you send anything to the other parent. Small clarity beats a heated text thread.

## Turn the Page Thursday — 2026-09-04

**Theme:** Divorce paperwork overwhelm  
**Post (Facebook):**  
"Turn the Page" isn't about pretending divorce is easy. It's about not letting a pile of forms become the story of your week. If you're staring at financial disclosures or confused about what "served" really means, you're not behind—you're normal. Gather documents in one folder (pay stubs, tax returns, mortgage statements), note what's missing, and bring questions—not shame—to your first consultation.

**Guardrail:** No outcome promises; Western PA geography only.

## Family Friday — 2026-09-05

**Theme:** Supporting kids during separation  
**Post (Facebook):**  
Kids don't need perfect parents during separation—they need predictable adults. Keep school, activities, and bedtime as steady as you can. Avoid using children as messengers about money or schedules. If you're unsure how to explain changes age-appropriately, that's a good question for your attorney—not the other parent's lawyer.
