## Friday — Moe El-Abdallah

**Format:** Native text post  
**Author:** Moe El-Abdallah

**Post copy:**

Configuration tip that saves headaches: document your security role matrix before you scale user access.

I've audited enough UKG and Workday environments to know the pattern. Go-live happens fast. Roles get copied from a template. Six months later, half your managers can see compensation data they shouldn't.

Before you add 200 users for open enrollment:

• Map each role to actual job functions, not job titles
• Test with two real managers before bulk provisioning
• Schedule a quarterly access review (put it on the calendar now)

Security isn't a launch-week task. It's a lifecycle discipline.
