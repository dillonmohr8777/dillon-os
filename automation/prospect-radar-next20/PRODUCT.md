# Prospect Radar Next 20 Factory

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Existing Dillon OS static HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Node, FFmpeg, and Playwright site factory.

## Users

Dillon reviews private, mobile-first concept websites for qualified Momentum 360 prospects. A prospect may later review an approved concept, but these local builds are not outreach or publication.

## Product Purpose

Select exactly 20 untouched Prospect Radar rebuild opportunities per run, prove that each one is a new build, and produce a complete private concept with verified identity assets and review evidence.

## Positioning

The factory binds each concept to current Radar evidence, a verified first-party logo, verified first-party visual references, global prior-build exclusion, and per-asset provenance before it permits a build.

## Operating Context

The current Prospect Radar registry and prior batch evidence are the sources of truth. A hidden Windows task runs once daily. Every run is resumable and local only.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Select 20 current `rebuild` prospects with a reachable official source, a verified exact logo, and usable first-party visual reference.
- Exclude domains and slugs found in every prior Radar, prospect-site, and site-factory artifact available on this machine.
- Preserve exact logos. Never redraw or synthesize a prospect logo.
- Keep `noindex`, `nofollow`, `mail_ready=hold`, and `qa_ready=hold` until factual and outreach gates pass.
- Never send, publish, deploy, mutate CRM, or write the canonical Marketing Chief queue.
- No scroll lab, WebGL lab, or experimental lab route belongs in this lane.

## Brand Commitments

Mobile is the primary canvas. Each site inherits the prospect's verified logo and visual cues, uses self-hosted expressive typography, and ends with a large exact-logo reveal inspired by the IMMOHRTAL ink-soak interaction. Recent Prospect Radar cinematic pacing is a craft reference, not a reusable costume.

## Evidence on Hand

- `12_Brain/state/radar/registry.json`
- `Daily-Briefs/prospect-radar.html`
- Prior batch manifests, target files, selection evidence, and asset provenance under Dillon OS and dated Prospect Radar workspaces
- `immohrtal-site/src/components/Hero.tsx` and `immohrtal-site/src/index.css` for the ink-logo reference
- Verified first-party source pages and assets captured per run

No prospect performance claims, testimonials, prices, outcomes, or operational facts may be invented.

## Product Principles

- New means globally untouched, not merely absent from the current batch.
- Exact identity beats generated approximation.
- Mobile composition is designed first and desktop is the expansion.
- A source gap becomes a hold, never a guess.
- Every completion claim is backed by deterministic receipts and rendered QA.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Keyboard access, visible focus, reduced motion, responsive type, 44 pixel touch targets, semantic structure, zero horizontal overflow at 320 pixels, and meaningful alternative text are required.
