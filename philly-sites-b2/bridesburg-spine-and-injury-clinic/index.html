Early intervention changes the outcome

Auto injuries, whiplash, headaches, neck pain, back pain, shoulder pain, jaw pain, radicular pain, dizziness and vertigo. The list is long because the delay between the injury and the first visit is what turns a short problem into a long one. Soft tissue that heals in a shortened position stays shortened, and by the time most people book, the body has already built its compensation. Getting seen in the first week changes what the treatment is even trying to undo.
