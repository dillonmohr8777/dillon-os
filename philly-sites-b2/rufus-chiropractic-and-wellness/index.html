Chiropractic care

Adjustment and ongoing care built around the health goals you came in with, rather than a fixed programme sold up front.

Neck pain and sciatica

The two complaints that bring most people through the door, and the two that most reward finding the actual cause.

Corrective exercises

Exercises that hold the correction between appointments, which is where most of the progress is really made or lost.

Massage therapy

Soft tissue work alongside the adjustment, addressing the muscle that keeps returning the joint to where it started.

Cupping and physiotherapy

Cupping, physiotherapy and ultrasound used where they genuinely help rather than added to fill out a treatment plan.

Red light therapy

About our office

About our office

We provide excellent chiropractic care to help our patients reach and exceed their health goals. We assess the patient as a whole. We want to know what is happening in the rest of your life, because it is usually connected to what brought you in. The intake here takes longer than most people expect for that reason, and it is regularly the part of the visit that turns up the thing nobody had connected to the pain.
