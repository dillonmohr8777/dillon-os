What we offer

Care and education, treated as the same job.

01

Chiropractic techniques

Adjustment methods and the tools that go with them, explained before they are used rather than after you ask what that was.

02

Sports injuries

Injuries from the field and the gym treated with return to play in mind, not just the pain gone by Friday.

03

Coping with pain

Self care you can actually do between appointments, which is what determines whether progress holds or resets each week.

04

Types of therapies

A range of therapies matched to the condition in front of us rather than to whatever the schedule makes easiest.

05

Exercise and prevention

General prevention guidance and exercise programming aimed at the injury you have not had yet.

06

Anatomy education

Key body structures and terms laid out plainly, so you can follow your own case instead of nodding along to it.

Patient education is part of the treatment

Anatomy, exercise, health tips, general prevention, key body structures and terms, treatments, chiropractic techniques and tools, coping with pain, and types of therapies. Patients who understand what is happening get better outcomes, and that is not a slogan. Someone who knows why their neck is doing this will change the thing at work that is causing it. Someone who does not will be back in six weeks with the same complaint and the same setup waiting at home.
