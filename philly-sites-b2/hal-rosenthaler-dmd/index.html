The practice

Our commitment to you

Dr. Rosenthaler provides dentistry that looks good, feels good, lasts a long time, and fits within your means. Specializing in prosthetic, restorative, cosmetic and implant dentistry, his gentle techniques combine with the caring nature of our team. That last constraint is deliberate and it is stated up front: the best plan is the one you will actually complete, so treatment gets sequenced around what is realistic rather than presented as a single number to accept or decline.
