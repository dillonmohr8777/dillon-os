Services

What we do

01

Family foot care

Quality foot and ankle care for every age in the house, from the child growing out of shoes to the grandparent who needs watching.

02

Heel and arch pain

The pain that announces itself on the first step of the morning, which is a specific pattern with a specific cause.

03

Diabetic foot care

Regular monitoring for feet that cannot afford a missed problem, on a schedule rather than when something is already wrong.

04

Nail and skin conditions

Ingrown nails, fungus, corns and calluses, treated in office and usually far more easily than people expect.

05

Sports and activity injuries

Injuries treated with getting you back out there as the point, not simply waiting for the pain to fade.

06

Pediatric podiatry

Children's feet checked by people who see them all day and know which things are worth watching and which resolve on their own.

About

Why Northeast Family Foot Care

At Northeast Family Foot Care we believe that a visit to the doctor does not have to be a chore. We want you to feel relaxed and at home. In fact, in our office, you are not just a patient, you are part of the practice. Podiatry sees a lot of people who put the visit off for a year, and the tone of the office is a deliberate response to that, because the appointment somebody dreads is the appointment they postpone until it is harder to fix.
