About

Why Oxford Rehabilitation Center

Top rated chiropractic rehab center in Philadelphia, serving the community for over 30 years. Rehab services include chiropractic therapy, physical therapy, hands-on techniques and aquatic therapy. Motor vehicle accidents and work related injuries are a large part of what we handle. Having all four disciplines in one building is what lets a patient move between them as they recover, starting in the water and finishing on the floor without ever being referred out and starting over.
