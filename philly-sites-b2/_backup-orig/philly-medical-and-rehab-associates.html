About

Why Philly Medical and Rehab Associates

People who suffer slip and fall injuries can experience trauma to bones, joints, muscles and ligaments on nearly any place of the body. When these injuries are severe and a chronic source of pain you may encounter limitations in your daily life. Those limitations are usually what finally brings someone in, long after the injury itself, and by then the task is not only treating the damage but undoing everything the body has learned to do around it.
