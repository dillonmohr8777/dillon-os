Our philosophy

PTinPhilly is a cash based physical therapy and personal training practice founded by professionals with knowledge and experience in the fitness and wellness industry. We never book multiple patients in one hour, which allows you to have the full attention of a licensed Doctor of Physical Therapy. Anyone who has done therapy at a busy clinic knows the alternative: fifteen minutes of hands on work and forty five minutes on a table with a heat pack while the clinician runs three other rooms.

Why the industry is going cash based

Private pay lets the clinician spend the hour on you instead of on a billing code, which is the actual reason so much of the profession is moving this way. Every one of our trainers is an experienced certified professional delivering programs proven to get results.
