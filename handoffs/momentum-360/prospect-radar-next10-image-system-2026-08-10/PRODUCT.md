# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The primary users are Fabled 5 / Opus 5 builders assembling private Prospect Radar homepage concepts for Momentum 360, plus Dillon and the Fabled 5 reviewers judging whether each concept is visually compelling enough to earn a client conversation. The secondary audience is the local business owner who should immediately recognize their trade, place, and material world without mistaking synthetic concept imagery for real business photography.

## Product Purpose

Create a compact, reusable image system for ten Philadelphia-area prospect homepages. Success means every homepage can open with the cinematic depth, object-study composition, and material specificity of the Golden Eagle Jewelers reference while retaining business-specific visual identity, readable fallbacks, and honest generated-imagery disclosure. The package prioritizes thirty strong image plates over a full site implementation so Opus 5 / Fabled 5 can build the pages efficiently.

## Brand Personality

Cinematic, tactile, exacting. The work should feel like a dark gallery installation built from the physical materials of each trade: polished mineral, porcelain, steel, lacquer, rubber, paper, copper, or food texture. It should create intrigue without becoming vague, gothic, or illegible.

## Anti-references

- The current Next 10 batch's single generic hero per prospect, repeated section anatomy, and category-default art direction.
- Generic stock-photo offices, smiling teams, staged handshakes, fake storefronts, and literal before/after collages.
- WebGL-only scenes that become blank or materially sparse in constrained rendering.
- AI-generated lettering, fake logos, watermarks, readable paperwork, signage, or unsupported business claims inside imagery.
- Recolored copies of one master composition.

## Design Principles

1. Start from a physical object, not a category stereotype.
2. Give each prospect one transformation story and three complementary focal lengths.
3. Keep typography, overlays, labels, logos, and diagrams code-native; images remain clean cinematic plates.
4. Preserve honest boundaries: synthetic concept imagery is disclosed and never presented as business photography.
5. Spend generation effort on assets that can carry multiple sections and crops, not decorative filler.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

All image plates must support useful alt text and must not contain essential copy. Pages must keep readable non-WebGL fallbacks, visible content when JavaScript is unavailable, strong text contrast over imagery, and reduced-motion behavior. No faces are required, and hands may appear only when process clarity benefits.
