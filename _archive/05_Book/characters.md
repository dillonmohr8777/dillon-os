# Characters

## Darnell Covington
Math prodigy. East Atlanta upbringing, Harvard education. Central protagonist.

## Javon Whitfield
Harvard quarterback, Rosedale MS. Darnell's counterweight and foil.

## Dijon Garnier
French billionaire antagonist. Drives the novel's political and financial machinery.

## Alec Daheim
CIA operative. Bridges the geopolitical thriller layer.
