# Brand Guidelines

## Tone
Trustworthy, local, no-nonsense, reliable. Emphasize emergency readiness, quick response, and long-term comfort. Never salesy.

## Content pillars
- Seasonal HVAC readiness (furnace tune-up, AC prep, heat pump seasonal swing).
- Emergency repair and same-day service positioning.
- System replacement and efficiency upgrades.
- Community / neighborhood shoutouts across Hampshire IL and surrounding service areas.
