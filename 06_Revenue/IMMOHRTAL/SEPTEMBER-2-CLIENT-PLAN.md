# IMMOHRTAL Two Client Operating Plan

**Target:** Two verified new IMMOHRTAL customers by 2026-09-15
**Start:** 2026-08-25
**Window:** 22 calendar dates, 16 weekdays, or 15 operating days when Labor Day is held out
**Status:** Internal operating target, not a forecast or guarantee

## Definition of success

A customer counts only when the exact legal customer and scope are resolved, the agreement or order form is signed, the required initial payment or written authorization is verified, and a delivery slot is reserved. Interest, a reply, a call, a proposal, or a verbal yes does not count.

## Planning funnel

This is capacity math built from explicit assumptions. It is not a market benchmark.

| Stage | Target count | Planning assumption |
|---|---:|---|
| Evidence-screened accounts | 200 | About 13 to 14 per operating day |
| Source-qualified, conflict-cleared accounts | 160 | About 10 to 11 per operating day |
| Exact first touches approved and sent | 120 | 8 per operating day after exact batch approval |
| Positive replies | 12 | 10% planning response assumption |
| Verified meetings booked | 8 | Two thirds of positive replies select a real slot |
| Verified customers | 2 | 25% of completed qualified meetings close |

The funnel should be recalculated from actual results after every 30 verified sends or every five completed meetings, whichever happens first. Missing data stays unknown. A low reply count does not justify lowering evidence, privacy, or source-quality controls.

## Source order

1. Current inbound or prior business-owner conversations in Dillon's authorized Gmail account, after full-thread and client-route review.
2. Dillon-owned Mohr Media or IMMOHRTAL contact sources in Google Drive and Sheets, after purpose, ownership, freshness, duplicate, and suppression checks.
3. Current public business sites and verified business profiles for narrow service-business research.
4. Public LinkedIn company or owner evidence only when the identity is current and the information is visible without bypassing access controls.

Never move client-owned contacts, employer contacts, job-search contacts, purchased lists without a verified use basis, private profile data, or inferred email addresses into the IMMOHRTAL prospect pool.

## First wedge

Prioritize established service businesses with a live public business identity and one reproducible website, search-access, answer-visibility, or paid-media-management problem. A current site or source must support the observation. Start with companies for which Dillon already has a verified public-safe homepage concept because the first touch can lead with a useful artifact rather than a generic pitch.

## Offer routing

Use the smallest sufficient approved offer:

- Technical SEO at $700 per month
- AEO at $700 per month
- GEO at $700 per month
- SEO + AEO + GEO at $1,500 per month
- Google Ads management at $400 per month
- Meta Ads management at $400 per month
- Google + Meta Ads management at $650 per month
- Custom-scoped website, optimization, content, CRM, or business-agent work only after discovery

Do not lead with every service. Route one observable problem to one plausible offer, then let buyer-supplied facts change the scope.

## Booking state

The primary Google Calendar identity is live and verified. A dedicated IMMOHRTAL booking page is not currently verified. The booking lane therefore remains `BLOCKED_CONFIGURATION` until Dillon's availability, meeting length, notice, buffer, cap, fields, privacy language, confirmation copy, and test receipts are approved.

Until then, the system may read live availability and prepare a reply with two or three open times after a positive prospect response. It may not create the event or send an invitation without the applicable approval and confirmed prospect selection.

## Non-negotiable controls

- No email, LinkedIn message, connection request, follow-up, proposal, or calendar invite is sent without the required exact approval.
- No em dash appears in outreach copy. Rendered email follows the stricter no dash punctuation rule.
- Every public email address retains its exact first-party source locator.
- Every opt-out, client conflict, employer conflict, active-conversation conflict, invalid identity, and delivery failure enters suppression.
- Every stage transition has a timestamped receipt.
- The daily report distinguishes drafted, approved, sent, replied, booked, proposed, signed, and paid.
