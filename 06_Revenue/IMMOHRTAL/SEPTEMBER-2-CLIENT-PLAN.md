## Definition of success

A customer counts only when the exact legal customer and scope are resolved, the agreement or order form is signed, the required initial payment or written authorization is verified, and a delivery slot is reserved. Interest, a reply, a call, a proposal, or a verbal yes does not count.

## Non-negotiable controls

- No email, LinkedIn message, connection request, follow-up, proposal, or calendar invite is sent without the required exact approval.
- No em dash appears in outreach copy. Rendered email follows the stricter no dash punctuation rule.
- Every public email address retains its exact first-party source locator.
- Every opt-out, client conflict, employer conflict, active-conversation conflict, invalid identity, and delivery failure enters suppression.
- Every stage transition has a timestamped receipt.
- The daily report distinguishes drafted, approved, sent, replied, booked, proposed, signed, and paid.
