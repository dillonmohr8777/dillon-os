# Evidence and Source Rules

## Source hierarchy

1. Current public company website for identity and observable public condition.
2. Buyer-supplied evidence with explicit scope and source date.
3. Authorized private evidence routed to the exact business and environment.
4. Local research artifacts only as a pointer to their named source, never as
   proof of a later live or commercial event.

Search snippets, model output, concept pages, old drafts, and agent assertions
cannot independently prove account identity, buyer intent, rankings, traffic,
lead volume, conversion, budget, decision authority, or commercial progress.

## Required evidence packet

Every account needs:

- stable account identity and canonical public domain;
- source URL, source kind, observed date, and refresh due date;
- one reproducible observation stated without inferred private impact;
- direct service-lane relevance and explicit unknowns;
- confidence and truth state;
- duplicate, suppression, prior-touch, and client-conflict disposition before
  qualification;
- local artifact locator and source row rank;
- independent QA before crossing a commercial boundary.

## Freshness

- Day 1 website observations receive a 14-day review window.
- Reproduce evidence sooner if the page changes, redirects, becomes unavailable,
  or a new source contradicts it.
- Stale evidence may remain historical but cannot support stage advancement.
- `observed_date` is the source observation date; `updated_at` is record hygiene.

## Truth states

- `confirmed_current`: reproduced from the named current source within the
  freshness window.
- `provisional`: plausible but missing a required identity or source check.
- `unknown`: not evidenced and not safely inferable.
- `blocked`: evidence conflict, access restriction, suppression, or policy gate
  prevents use.

## Privacy and minimization

- This foundation stores company-level public facts only.
- Do not import email addresses, phone numbers, named-recipient data, message
  subjects, message bodies, private communications, cookies, tokens, or
  credentials.
- Contact-channel discovery, if later authorized, belongs in a separately
  permissioned table with purpose, source, suppression, and retention controls.
- Client-owned prospect or CRM data cannot be reused for IMMOHRTAL without
  exact authority and client isolation.

## Commercial claim rules

- Public site friction does not prove lost leads or revenue.
- A concept proves preparation only.
- A message is sent only with an external delivery receipt.
- A reply, meeting, proposal, decision, contract, payment, or revenue event
  requires its own exact receipt.
- Missing business metrics remain null, not zero.
- Forecasting and pipeline value remain excluded until source-backed scope and
  amounts exist.

## Owner handshake

Demand Intelligence signs the evidence fields. Revenue Pipeline Manager either
accepts the packet for qualification or returns a specific defect. The Revenue
Pipeline Manager cannot overwrite uncertainty to advance a stage. The auditor
may fail a record but may not silently repair it.
