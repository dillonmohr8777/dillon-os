# AI Design (Site Factory)

Need Momentum’s design offer: a real site that looks like the business, not a Midjourney moodboard.

## Description

[[12_Brain/entities/Website Factory|Website Factory]] plus taste. Cold outreach gets a noindex demo harvested from their site and socials. After they engage: Tier A (shared architecture, their brand) or Tier B (bespoke, Kimi-pilot style). AI is the production system. The homepage never says “AI-designed.”

## Ideal Client

Dated site, can pay, vertical matches an industry page. `/site-grade` rebuild verdicts only. Ads/SEO-grade sites get a traffic offer instead.

## Deliverables

- Harvest (screenshots, copy, palette) before brief
- Demo or rebuild with QA (responsive, contrast, unique images, facts sourced)
- Maps/NAP/contact that are true or blank
- Handoff: repo, CMS path, noindex until they own it

## Pricing

Automaton publishes $5–25k web/ops projects and $2–5k sprints (seller, 2026-05-08). Factory should beat traditional web on cycle time, not race $997 “AI website” shops on price. Mac sets the number. Tier B only after a bite.

## Objection Handling

- “Is this a template?” Tier A shares a skeleton; tokens, photos, and copy are theirs. Tier B does not share a skeleton.
- “Will you index a demo of my business?” No. Private noindex until they hire us.
- “Can you just generate it in an afternoon?” Fast is the factory. Taste is the gate.

## Sales Assets

- Review hub (one URL per batch) + 5-min Loom
- Philly 25 + three Kimi pilots as internal proof, not a public portfolio of businesses we don’t work for
