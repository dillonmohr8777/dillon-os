# IMMOHRTAL Outreach and Booking Standard

**Status:** Internal operating standard
**As of:** 2026-08-25
**External sending authority:** Exact approved batch only
**Automatic calendar booking:** Blocked pending a dedicated IMMOHRTAL booking page and approved availability rules

## Outreach writing rules

1. Resolve the exact company, person, source, and relationship before drafting.
2. Keep every client-owned, employer-owned, job-search, purchased, and suppressed list out of the IMMOHRTAL prospect pool unless a current source explicitly authorizes that use.
3. Use one current, reproducible observation about the prospect's real site or public business presence.
4. Make one truthful connection to an IMMOHRTAL service. Do not invent results, familiarity, urgency, or a referral.
5. Use no em dash in any outreach copy. Rendered email bodies also follow Dillon's stricter email rule: no hyphen, en dash, em dash, or dash-style punctuation.
6. Use short sentences, ordinary buyer language, and one clear next action.
7. Never infer a private email address. Record the public source for any business address used.
8. Prepare drafts and approval previews first. Sending, posting, connection requests, and follow-ups require the existing exact-delivery approval.

## Lead-to-meeting state machine

`RESEARCH_ONLY -> EVIDENCE_READY -> QUALIFICATION_PENDING -> QUALIFIED -> OUTREACH_DRAFTED -> OUTREACH_APPROVED -> OUTREACH_SENT_VERIFIED -> REPLY_VERIFIED -> BOOKING_CONSENT_VERIFIED -> MEETING_BOOKED_VERIFIED`

Research, a draft, a suggested time, or a booking link is never a booked meeting. Each transition requires a current source locator and a receipt.

## Automatic booking contract

Automatic booking may be enabled only after all of these controls are verified:

- A dedicated IMMOHRTAL booking URL owned by Dillon, not a client or employer booking page
- The exact calendar identity and destination calendar
- Meeting duration, recurring availability, timezone, notice window, buffer, and daily cap
- Required prospect fields and a privacy notice that matches the actual collection
- Duplicate, conflict, cancellation, and reschedule handling
- A confirmation message with no unsupported promise
- A test booking, calendar event readback, invite routing readback, and cancellation test
- A shutdown control and an owner for failed or suspicious bookings

Current live evidence confirms Dillon controls the primary Google Calendar account. No dedicated IMMOHRTAL booking page was found in the authorized Gmail search on 2026-08-25. The visible Calendly URL found in business threads belongs to Momentum 360 and must not be reused for IMMOHRTAL.

Until the contract passes, a positive reply may receive an approval-ready draft with proposed times based on live calendar availability. No event is created and no invitation is sent automatically.
