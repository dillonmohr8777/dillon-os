# IMMOHRTAL Qualification and Disqualification

**Workflow:** `IMMOHRTAL-DAY1-20260825`
**Step:** `OFFERS`
**Status:** Internal Day 1 sales gate

Use this gate before a custom proposal, calendar commitment, private-system access request, or pricing exception. Passing qualification permits a scoped sales conversation. It does not authorize outreach delivery, a contract, account access, publishing, or implementation.

## Universal qualification floor

Proceed only when all are true:

1. The prospect is an established service business or a closely related expert business with a real website, visibility, content, CRM, or repeated-workflow need.
2. The problem is supported by a current public observation, information the prospect supplied, or authorized private evidence. Private impact is not inferred from a public symptom.
3. One IMMOHRTAL service lane directly fits the problem: Web Design, Website Optimization, Technical SEO, AEO/GEO, Content and Schema, Business Agents, or HubSpot and CRM Agents.
4. A named person owns or influences the decision, and one person can consolidate approval.
5. The buyer accepts a bounded first engagement with written inputs, exclusions, acceptance checks, and approval gates.
6. Budget is at or above the applicable internal launch floor, or scope can be reduced without weakening QA or safety.
7. Required source material and access can be provided through an approved route without exposing credentials in email, chat, or project files.
8. The delivery calendar has capacity under `DAY-1-PRICE-BOOK.md`.
9. The opportunity does not conflict with an existing client, partner, active conversation, opt-out, or do-not-contact record.
10. The buyer understands that search, AI-answer, traffic, lead, revenue, and third-party platform outcomes are not guaranteed.

## Qualification scorecard

Score each dimension 0, 1, or 2.

| Dimension | 0 | 1 | 2 |
|---|---|---|---|
| Problem evidence | Vague or inferred | Plausible with partial evidence | Reproducible or buyer-confirmed |
| Service fit | Paid-media-only or outside offer | Adjacent fit | Direct fit to a live service lane |
| Decision ownership | Unknown | Influencer identified | Authorized decision maker engaged |
| Scope readiness | “Do everything” | First boundary emerging | One clear next decision or workflow |
| Inputs/access | Missing or unsafe | Obtainable with conditions | Ready through approved routes |
| Budget fit | Below floor | Unconfirmed but plausible | Floor or range acknowledged |
| Timing/capacity | Artificial emergency or impossible | Flexible | Fits current queue and dependencies |
| Evidence culture | Requests fabricated certainty | Needs expectation setting | Accepts source-backed and testable work |

- **13–16:** Qualified. Prepare the smallest fitting proposal.
- **9–12:** Conditional. Run a paid diagnostic or resolve the specific missing condition.
- **0–8:** Disqualify or nurture. Do not produce a custom implementation proposal.

A hard disqualifier below overrides the numeric score.

## Offer-specific qualification

### Live Website Decision Diagnostic — $950

Qualify when:

- A live website and one primary business decision are available.
- The buyer can identify a key service, customer path, or repeated handoff to inspect.
- The decision maker wants an evidence-based repair/rebuild/visibility/workflow recommendation rather than free speculative concepts.
- The site is accessible for ordinary review or the buyer can provide approved access.

Disqualify when:

- The request is a free audit, free mockup, unpaid strategy session, or exhaustive full-site crawl.
- The buyer expects production changes inside the diagnostic.
- The main ask is paid-media performance, legal compliance certification, or a guaranteed ranking/citation.
- No live/staging evidence or decision owner exists.

### Website Optimization Sprint — $4,500 to $7,500

Qualify when:

- The visual/technical foundation is worth preserving.
- Five or fewer priority repairs can create a meaningful release across a bounded route/template set.
- Repository/CMS, hosting, and a recovery path can be provided.
- One approver can return consolidated feedback within two business days.

Disqualify or redirect to a website system when:

- Navigation, page architecture, visual language, content hierarchy, and responsive system all need coordinated replacement.
- The stack is inaccessible, unsupported, or cannot be safely released within the sprint.
- The buyer requests unlimited pages, daily piecemeal changes, or multiple stakeholder revision streams.
- The sprint depends on an unapproved migration, new CRM, full content program, ecommerce build, or application logic.

### Distinctive Website System — $18,000 to $32,000

Qualify when:

- The business has a clear primary audience, offer, and accountable decision maker.
- The current site needs coordinated structural and visual change.
- Approved factual inputs, brand assets, image rights, and public proof can be assembled.
- The buyer accepts an eight-to-12-week process, staged decisions, consolidated feedback, and milestone payments.
- The launch can be bounded by a signed page/template matrix.

Disqualify or pause when:

- The buyer will not choose one authorized approver.
- Source material, service facts, ownership rights, or domain/hosting control cannot be resolved.
- The requested launch date makes source review, responsive build, QA, or migration safety impossible.
- The budget is below the floor while scope still requires a custom system.
- Success is defined only as a promised rank, lead volume, revenue result, or imitation of another brand.
- A second brand, domain, language, ecommerce system, or business unit is hidden inside the starting scope.

### Search and Answer Visibility Program — $2,000 foundation plus $3,500 to $6,500 monthly

Qualify when:

- The website is technically usable or the buyer agrees to repair the foundation first.
- One service/topic cluster and a real subject-matter reviewer are available.
- The business can supply accurate facts, evidence, and source material.
- Search Console/analytics access can be provided when measurement is expected.
- The buyer accepts a six-month initial learning/production horizon and output-based acceptance without guaranteed exposure.

Disqualify or redirect when:

- The buyer wants mass AI articles, purchased links, fabricated expertise, review manipulation, or unsupported schema.
- The core site is inaccessible, broken, or too thin to support the proposed program and the buyer refuses remediation.
- No subject-matter reviewer or authoritative source material is available.
- The buyer requires guaranteed rankings, indexing, AI citations, traffic, leads, or revenue.
- The requested market requires language, legal, or subject expertise not available in the approved delivery team.

### Bounded Business Agent Implementation — $7,500 to $15,000

Qualify when:

- One repeated task has a clear trigger, owner, source set, output, reviewer, and stop condition.
- Representative non-sensitive examples and at least one failure case are available.
- A read-only, inspection, classification, or draft-mode first release is useful.
- One environment and one primary integration/data source can define the starting boundary.
- The team will review receipts and expand permissions only after evidence supports it.

Disqualify or redesign when:

- The first release requires autonomous outreach, public publishing, spend, deletion, access changes, or ambiguous CRM mutation.
- The buyer asks the worker to bypass MFA, CAPTCHA, passkeys, consent, or platform controls.
- The task depends on copying credentials into prompts, repositories, logs, or project documents.
- There is no stable process, no owner, no reviewable output, or no way to recognize failure.
- The buyer expects guaranteed headcount reduction or labor savings without a measured baseline.
- The request actually contains multiple workflows, clients, portals, brands, or environments.

## Hard disqualifiers for every offer

- Opted out, do-not-contact, bounced, invalid, risky, or client-conflicted prospect.
- Personal contact data with no clear business relationship or public business purpose.
- Need for an invented compliment, metric, testimonial, customer outcome, competitive claim, urgency, or private-system assumption.
- Unclear client, account, domain, repository, portal, owner, or publication target.
- Request to misrepresent work, ownership, authorship, evidence, platform affiliation, or AI capability.
- Request to publish unsupported results, use deceptive outreach, buy or fabricate reviews, or evade platform/legal requirements.
- Requirement to expose secrets or cross client/account boundaries.
- Refusal to accept change control, milestone payment, QA, source verification, or consequential-action approval.
- Abuse, harassment, discriminatory targeting, illegal purpose, or a business category Dillon does not wish to represent.
- Timeline or workload that would exceed the launch capacity rules without qualified added delivery capacity.

## Redirect rules

| Observed state | Smallest right offer |
|---|---|
| The buyer is unsure whether to repair, rebuild, or change visibility/content | Live Website Decision Diagnostic |
| The existing site is credible and the problem is bounded | Website Optimization Sprint |
| Structure, visual language, content hierarchy, and responsive system need coordinated change | Distinctive Website System |
| The public site is usable but a service/topic footprint needs sustained source and measurement work | Search and Answer Visibility Program |
| One repeated, evidence-rich task has a clear owner and safe first authority | Bounded Business Agent Implementation |
| The request is mainly a CRM handoff problem but the form/page is weak | Optimize the public handoff first, then qualify the agent/CRM workflow |
| The request is paid-media-only | Do not force-fit; refer or decline unless Dillon explicitly expands the offer |

## Discovery questions

Ask only what is needed to route the opportunity:

1. What is the live website or exact workflow?
2. What should a customer or team member be able to understand or do more easily?
3. Which page, handoff, or repeated task feels most limiting today?
4. What evidence confirms that problem, and what remains an assumption?
5. Who owns the decision and who supplies factual/source approval?
6. Which systems, domains, repositories, CMS instances, or CRM portals are in scope?
7. What cannot change?
8. What would count as an accepted artifact or release independent of rankings, traffic, leads, or revenue?
9. What budget range and decision date are real?
10. Which launch-capacity slot would this use?

## Proposal gate

Before a proposal is marked ready, record:

- Qualification score and any hard-disqualifier check.
- Chosen offer and why it is the smallest sufficient first move.
- Exact client, website/domain, repository/CMS, CRM portal/environment, and authorized owner where applicable.
- Included routes, templates, page units, workflow, integrations, revision rounds, and support window.
- Inputs, exclusions, acceptance criteria, payment milestones, delivery window, and capacity slot.
- Claims/evidence boundary and explicit no-guarantee language.
- Current state: draft, reviewed, approved, sent, accepted, declined, paused, or blocked.

No proposal or message is sent merely because this gate passes. External delivery retains the exact approval required by the operating policy.
