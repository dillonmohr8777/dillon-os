## Commercial assumptions

- IMMOHRTAL is a founder-led website and AI implementation studio, not a broad paid-media agency.
- The internal delivery-cost model values delivery labor at **$70 per hour** and adds direct project expenses separately. This is a planning assumption, not payroll or a customer-facing hourly rate.
- Gross margin estimates below are planning ranges calculated as `(price - assumed delivery cost - direct expenses) / price`. They exclude sales time, general overhead, taxes, and founder profit distributions.
- Fixed-fee scope is controlled by named routes, templates, systems, workflows, and revision rounds. Unbounded page counts, stakeholder rounds, integrations, migrations, and content production require a change order.
- A timeline begins only after the first payment, required access, source material, and one authorized decision maker are available.
- Customer-caused delays move the delivery calendar. A project paused more than 10 business days may require a restart date and fee.
- IMMOHRTAL can improve access, clarity, usefulness, measurement, and release quality. It does not guarantee rankings, indexing, AI citations, traffic, leads, revenue, or third-party platform behavior.

## 1. Live Website Decision Diagnostic

**Role in the ladder:** Founder-led front door
**Internal launch price:** **$950 fixed**
**Website truth:** Website optimization, technical SEO, web design, AEO/GEO, content/schema, and CRM handoff are assessed only where visible evidence supports them.

### Best for

An established service business that can point to a live site and a decision it needs to make: repair the current site, redesign it, strengthen a core service path, improve search access, or fix a handoff.

### Included

- Kickoff questionnaire and one 30-minute decision call.
- Review of the homepage, one primary service path, contact path, and representative mobile behavior.
- Reproducible observations across clarity, hierarchy, conversion path, responsive behavior, accessibility signals, crawlability, metadata/internal links, visible evidence, and form handoff.
- A concise decision brief with the highest-priority three to five findings, source URLs or screenshots, risk/impact reasoning, and a sequenced recommendation.
- One 30-minute readout and one written clarification round within five business days.

### Excluded

- Production edits, design concepts, page copy, a full crawl of a large site, analytics or CRM configuration, paid-media review, legal/accessibility certification, keyword guarantees, competitor surveillance, or unlimited consulting.

### Delivery window

Five business days after payment and receipt of the live URL, primary goal, known constraints, and authorized access required for any private evidence.

### Client inputs

- Live website and any safe staging URL.
- Primary service or decision path.
- One business goal and one known constraint.
- One decision maker for the readout.
- Optional read-only analytics or Search Console access when measurement is in scope.

### Acceptance criteria

- The brief names every inspected URL.
- Each material finding is reproducible or explicitly labeled as a hypothesis.
- Findings are prioritized rather than exported as an unranked warning list.
- The recommendation distinguishes repair, rebuild, visibility, content, CRM, and workflow work.
- No unsupported performance claim or ranking/citation promise appears.

### Payment and economics

- 100% due to reserve the diagnostic.
- Assumption: 7 delivery hours plus up to $50 direct expense.
- Planning delivery cost: $540.
- Planning gross margin: approximately 43%.
- Founder capacity: 7 hours total; do not let the diagnostic become unpaid implementation.

### Natural next step

Optimization Sprint for a sound site with bounded friction; Full Website System when structure and visual language need coordinated replacement; Visibility Program when source pages and measurement are the primary gap; Agent Implementation when the repeated operational handoff is the real constraint.

## 2. Website Optimization Sprint

**Role in the ladder:** Focused implementation
**Internal launch range:** **$4,500 to $7,500**
**Typical starting scope:** **$4,500**
**Website truth:** Performance, mobile usability, accessibility, content clarity, conversion paths, technical SEO, analytics, and release QA for an existing website.

### Best for

A credible existing website whose design foundation can remain while a bounded set of high-value problems is repaired in the real build.

### Included at $4,500

- Confirmed baseline and prioritized sprint backlog.
- Up to five agreed repairs across no more than five representative public routes or one shared template family.
- Repairs may address responsive layout, content hierarchy, calls to action, accessibility, image/script/font waste, crawl paths, metadata, internal links, accurate schema, or one contact/form experience.
- One staging review, one consolidated revision round, production release to the verified existing host, and desktop/mobile/live verification.
- Before-and-after evidence tied to the agreed issue list.

### Range drivers

Price moves toward $7,500 for a difficult legacy stack, multiple templates, content restructuring, analytics event repair, complex deployment, third-party form behavior, or more extensive accessibility remediation. Scope beyond that becomes a second sprint or website-system proposal.

### Excluded

- New brand identity, complete redesign, unlimited pages, a new CMS, full copywriting, ecommerce, multilingual work, custom application logic, CRM rebuild, paid media, ongoing SEO, or third-party subscription fees.

### Delivery window

10 business days for the starting scope; up to 15 business days at the top of the range. The clock begins after access, backup/recovery path, approved backlog, and content inputs are ready.

### Client inputs

- Repository/CMS and hosting access through an approved method.
- Current brand assets and source copy.
- Analytics definitions if measurement is included.
- One authorized approver who returns consolidated feedback within two business days.

### Acceptance criteria

- Every agreed repair has a pass/fail check and before-state locator.
- The build passes its project tests and production build where available.
- Agreed desktop and mobile routes are inspected for overflow, interaction, and console errors.
- Keyboard/focus and reduced-motion behavior are checked where the changed component requires them.
- Live URLs, response behavior, metadata, links, and forms affected by the sprint are verified after release.
- Any remaining issue is documented as deferred, blocked, or out of scope rather than silently omitted.

### Payment and economics

- 60% at booking; 40% before production release.
- Assumption: 32 to 50 delivery hours plus $150 to $300 direct expense.
- Planning delivery cost: $2,390 to $3,800.
- Planning gross margin: approximately 47% to 49% when priced to complexity.
- Founder capacity: one kickoff, one staging review, one QA/release block; no piecemeal daily feedback.

### Natural next step

A second tightly defined sprint, Full Website System if patching reveals structural debt, or Visibility Program once the public experience and crawl path are sound.

## 3. Distinctive Website System

**Role in the ladder:** Full website design and build
**Internal launch range:** **$18,000 to $32,000**
**Typical starting scope:** **$18,000**
**Website truth:** Custom art direction, responsive page system, accessible interaction, production code, service architecture, crawlable content, accurate structured information, contact path, and verified release.

### Best for

An established service business whose current structure, design language, and key content need to change together, and which has an accountable approver and usable source material.

### Included at $18,000

- Product/positioning brief for one primary audience and decision path.
- One committed visual direction and documented design system.
- Up to eight core public pages using up to four reusable page templates, typically homepage, services hub, up to three service pages, about, work/proof, and contact.
- Responsive production build in the agreed existing or selected static web stack.
- Client-supplied copy edited and structured for clarity; net-new copy for the included core pages within the approved source record.
- Metadata, canonicals, sitemap, robots controls, internal links, visible author/organization facts where appropriate, and accurate supported schema.
- One contact path, baseline analytics event plan, migration/redirect map for included routes, and verified production release.
- Two consolidated review rounds: direction and staging.

### Range drivers

Price moves toward $32,000 for more page templates, deeper content production, migration complexity, custom interaction/3D, integrations, location systems, case-study libraries, CMS authoring, ecommerce, multilingual requirements, or unusual compliance/accessibility constraints. These drivers must be quoted explicitly.

### Excluded

- Unlimited pages or revisions, brand strategy beyond the web system, logo design unless named, photography/video production, ecommerce or membership logic, complex web applications, third-party fees, legal/privacy drafting, ongoing content, guaranteed WCAG certification, rankings, AI citations, or lead volume.

### Delivery window

Eight to 12 weeks for the starting scope, subject to content/access readiness and feedback within two business days. Additional complexity receives a dated schedule before signature.

### Client inputs

- One authorized decision maker and one source-of-truth content owner.
- Approved logo/brand assets, factual service information, people/location details, project proof, policies, and imagery rights.
- Existing domain, hosting, analytics, repository/CMS, and redirect context.
- Consolidated approval at the direction and staging gates.

### Acceptance criteria

- Included routes and templates match the signed page matrix.
- Approved content and assets are present with no invented claims.
- Core content is available in meaningful HTML and important routes have consistent metadata/canonical signals.
- Agreed desktop/mobile widths, keyboard/focus, reduced motion, overflow, media alternatives, forms, and console output pass the release checklist.
- Redirects and affected live routes are verified on the mapped production host.
- Known exclusions, third-party limitations, and post-launch observations are recorded in the launch receipt.

### Payment and economics

- 40% at booking, 30% at approved visual direction, 20% at staging acceptance, 10% before production release.
- Assumption: 120 to 210 delivery hours plus $800 to $1,500 direct expense.
- Planning delivery cost: $9,200 to $16,200.
- Planning gross margin: approximately 49% at both ends when scope and price rise together.
- Founder capacity: one active full build; no new active website starts until its staging gate is scheduled.

### Natural next step

Website Optimization Sprint for post-launch evidence, Visibility Program for ongoing search/answer work, or Agent Implementation for the contact-to-operations handoff.

## 4. Search and Answer Visibility Program

**Role in the ladder:** Recurring AEO, GEO, technical search, content, and measurement
**Internal launch price:** **$2,000 foundation fee plus $3,500 to $6,500 per month**
**Initial term:** **Six months**
**Website truth:** Crawlable source pages, entity relationships, direct answers, evidence, internal architecture, accurate schema, and dated visibility observation for Google and AI answer systems.

### Best for

A service business with a technically usable website, approved subject-matter access, and a need to build or improve a specific service/topic footprint over time.

### Foundation month

- Baseline crawl and indexability review for the agreed public route set.
- Priority service, question, entity, and evidence map.
- Measurement definitions for queries, landing pages, citations/answer appearances when observable, qualified visits, and CRM outcomes when available.
- Six-month editorial and repair backlog tied to existing or planned source pages.

### Monthly scope at $3,500

- One focused service/topic cluster.
- Up to two substantial new or revised source pages, or one source page plus two bounded technical/content repairs.
- Internal-link and supported-schema updates for the changed route set.
- One dated visibility observation and a plain-language monthly decision memo.
- One monthly working session and one consolidated revision round.

The $6,500 tier may cover up to four substantial pages or a broader route set, provided subject-matter review and production capacity are available. Exact units belong in the order form.

### Excluded

- Guaranteed rankings, indexing, traffic, AI inclusion/citation, PR/link buying, review generation, mass commodity articles, unsupported schema, paid media, social publishing, unlimited technical remediation, or a website rebuild.

### Delivery window

Foundation delivered within 15 business days of access and source receipt. Monthly production follows a mutually agreed calendar. Missed client source/approval dates shift the release month rather than compressing QA.

### Client inputs

- Website/repository/CMS access and Search Console/analytics access when measurement is expected.
- Subject-matter reviewer and source documents for consequential claims.
- Accurate organization, service, people, location, credentials, and proof facts.
- Approval within three business days for monthly releases.

### Acceptance criteria

- Every changed page has one documented job and target question set.
- Claims are supported by client-approved or authoritative sources; uncertainties are labeled.
- Changed pages have crawlable content, intentional internal links, accurate visible facts, and only supported structured data.
- The monthly memo distinguishes outputs, observations, and business outcomes; it never presents a transient search result as a guarantee.
- Production routes and changed technical signals are verified after release.

### Payment and economics

- Foundation fee and first month due at booking; each later month billed in advance.
- Assumption at $3,500: 25 delivery hours plus $200 direct expense; planning delivery cost $1,950; planning gross margin 44%.
- Assumption at $6,500: 45 delivery hours plus $300 direct expense; planning delivery cost $3,450; planning gross margin 47%.
- Founder capacity: maximum three launch programs; monthly units do not roll over and emergency work displaces planned production only through a signed change.

### Natural next step

Optimization Sprint for technical debt outside the monthly ceiling, Website System when source-page improvements expose a broken experience, or Agent Implementation for repeatable research, review, or CRM preparation.

## 5. Bounded Business Agent Implementation

**Role in the ladder:** Practical AI worker for one repeated job
**Internal launch range:** **$7,500 to $15,000**
**Typical starting scope:** **$7,500**
**Website truth:** Research, drafting, inspection, organization, quality checks, receipts, exact routing, and explicit approval gates for consequential actions.

### Best for

A team with one repetitive, evidence-rich workflow, a clear owner, a reviewable output, and a low-risk read-only or draft-mode first release. Good starting examples include a website health check, prospect-site audit brief, content refresh monitor, CRM enrichment queue, or prepublication quality gate.

### Included at $7,500

- Workflow discovery and a written worker contract covering trigger, client/account scope, sources, allowed actions, output, stop conditions, approval gates, and receipt.
- One workflow in one environment with one authoritative owner and one primary integration or data source.
- Read-only, classification, inspection, or draft/staging behavior first.
- Deterministic validation where possible, duplicate/retry handling, failure states, logging/receipts, and a documented human review path.
- Test fixtures, acceptance run, operator guide, and one training session.
- Thirty calendar days of defect support for the agreed workflow.

### Range drivers

Price moves toward $15,000 for multiple APIs, complex identity resolution, CRM sandbox work, scheduled execution, higher evidence requirements, migration of an existing prototype, or a more demanding review interface. A second workflow is a separate implementation.

### Excluded

- Autonomous sending, public publishing, spend, destructive actions, ambiguous CRM writes, credentials copied into project files, bypassing MFA/CAPTCHA/passkeys, broad multi-agent transformation, 24/7 managed operations, model/API usage fees, unsupported integrations, or guaranteed labor savings.

### Delivery window

Four to six weeks for the starting scope, including discovery, contract approval, build, safe-mode run, acceptance, and operator handoff.

### Client inputs

- Named workflow owner and reviewer.
- Current process, representative non-sensitive examples, expected output, failure examples, and baseline review time if available.
- Approved account/environment, least-privilege access route, retention rules, and escalation contact.
- Timely review of staged outputs; no production authority is inferred from access.

### Acceptance criteria

- The worker executes only the agreed trigger, account/client boundary, sources, and actions.
- At least three representative fixtures and one failure-path fixture produce the documented status and receipt.
- Duplicate/retry behavior is deterministic for the accepted cases.
- Missing access, ambiguous routing, unsupported claims, and consequential actions fail closed or pause for approval.
- The operator can inspect what the worker used, prepared, changed, or refused.
- Production activation, if any, occurs only at the signed authority level; draft/read-only acceptance is a valid completion state.

### Payment and economics

- 50% at booking, 30% at safe-mode demonstration, 20% at acceptance.
- Assumption: 60 to 115 delivery hours plus $300 to $600 direct expense.
- Planning delivery cost: $4,500 to $8,650.
- Planning gross margin: approximately 40% to 42% when scope and price rise together.
- Founder capacity: one active implementation; one workflow only; consequential authority is never bundled as a convenience.

### Natural next step

Optional managed reliability and iteration support at **$1,500 to $3,000 per month**, scoped by run volume, integration count, review load, and support window. Additional workers are separately qualified and priced after the first worker has reliable receipts.

## Proposal rules

1. Sell the smallest offer that resolves the prospect's next decision.
2. Never present a diagnostic observation as proof of private business impact.
3. Do not discount below the price book by removing QA, source controls, accessibility checks, or approval gates.
4. Prefer reducing route count, page units, integrations, revision rounds, or support duration to cutting price without cutting scope.
5. Require a new scope and price when a project adds a second brand, domain, CRM portal, business unit, language, ecommerce system, agent workflow, or authoritative approver.
6. No custom proposal is sent until the prospect passes `QUALIFICATION.md` and capacity exists on the calendar.
7. The final proposal must name the client, exact public/private systems, deliverables, exclusions, client inputs, acceptance checks, price, payment dates, and current approval state.
