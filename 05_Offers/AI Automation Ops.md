# AI Automation (Ops)

Need Momentum’s ops offer: missed follow-up becomes a booked conversation, in the client’s CRM.

## Description

The live page already exists. Tighten it to the Kind Insurance pattern: chatbot intake → client-owned CRM → routing → UTMs → human approval. 3–6 weeks. Disclose that the bot is AI. Do not sell a 7-day “AI employee” with no roster.

## Ideal Client

Steady inbound, slow follow-up, a CRM they already pay for. Insurance, home services, medical, real estate. Not a greenfield “we’ll host your leads forever” landlord deal.

## Deliverables

- Funnel map and stack audit
- Chat / SMS / email workflows in **their** tools
- QA: consent, tagging, every path, reports
- Launch watch + iteration
- Named human who owns breakage

## Pricing

Live FAQ: $1,500–$3,000 depending on tier and industry, plus setup. Ecommerce Starter $2000 / Growth $1500 / Enterprise $2000 on the page — Growth below Starter is a rewrite bug until Mac says otherwise. Real-estate packages on that page have no prices. Automaton sprints $2–5k are the adjacent analog.

## Objection Handling

- “Will this replace my staff?” No. It does the follow-up they skip.
- “Do you keep our leads?” No. CRM stays theirs.
- “Is the chatbot a person?” Disclose AI at first interaction.
