# AI Marketing (Creative + Ads)

Need Momentum’s marketing offer: more tests, same brand, a human still ships the ad.

## Description

AI drafts research, angles, and variants. Account managers keep taste, tracking, and spend control. This is not Meta Advantage+ on autopilot and not “AI-powered ads” on the client’s landing page. 60% of US consumers in an April 2026 survey called “AI” in brand messaging a turnoff.

## Ideal Client

Already in Google/Meta with Need Momentum, or a site-factory close that needs traffic. Home services and medical first.

## Deliverables

- Offer-angle matrix and creative variants (human-edited)
- Conversion tracking that is actually firing
- Weekly test log, not an “AI insights” dashboard
- Disclosure checklist when creative uses a generated person (NY rule in force 2026-06-09) or photoreal media (platform labels)
- Optional: AI video outbound (“we made you this”) as Mac’s third bucket — still human-approved, not autonomous DMs

## Pricing

Addon to the ads retainer. Do not invent a new $5k “AI marketing” SKU that is the same AM hours with a new label. Mac sets the delta.

## Objection Handling

- “Can’t we just turn on Advantage+?” We can test it. We do not hand the brand to a default-on generator.
- “Will this replace our AM?” No. It makes the AM ship more tests.
- “Put AI in the headline.” No. Put the offer in the headline.

## Named stack

Google Ads, Meta Ads, existing conversion tracking, Claude for draft variants, AM for spend and publish. `ads-audit` skill is capability-gated: **no spend** from the skill. Creative disclosure checklist when generated people or photoreal media appear.

## Human checkpoints

• AM edits every variant before it runs.
• Spend changes happen in the ads UI, by a person.
• NY synthetic-performer and platform labels when the creative needs them.
• Client brand and banned-term lists (Bar Crawl: pre-approved copy only).

## Sample SOW bullets (counsel still reviews)

• Addon to the existing ads retainer. More tests, same account.
• AI drafts. Humans ship. Tracking events you can defend in a report.
• Weekly test log. No "AI insights" dashboard as the deliverable.
• Ads don't lead with the word AI.

## First meeting (15 min)

Open last month's account. Show the next ten tests. Don't tour models.

• Last-week brief + redlined variants (the buyer ask)
• `claude-ads` upstream as an internal audit skill, capability-gated, no spend from the skill
