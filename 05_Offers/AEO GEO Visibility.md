# AEO / GEO Visibility

Need Momentum’s AI-search offer: make the business the cited answer without selling hacks or listicles.

## Description

Paid [[12_Brain/projects/Prospect Radar V2|Prospect Radar]] diagnostic, then a 90-day program that ships people-first pages, GBP, and citation measurement on top of existing SEO. Google’s 2026-05-15 guide: generative features are RAG over the Search index. Ignore llms.txt, chunking, fake mentions.

## Ideal Client

Local / multi-location SMB already paying for SEO or willing to. Home services, medical, legal, franchise. Not a funded SaaS buying WebFX OmniSEO.

## Deliverables

- Named prompt list (the questions customers actually ask)
- Radar audit: site, GBP, schema-for-rich-results, competitor citations
- 90-day content + entity cleanup (non-commodity pages, not one URL per fan-out query)
- Measurement: Search Console generative AI report, GA AI referrals, monthly prompt re-test
- Human QA before anything publishes

## Pricing

Internal posture only until Mac confirms. WebFX Bronze is $3,000/mo software+service (6-month, seller card). First Page Sage’s $2–12k GEO tiers are listicle/PR — do not copy. Seer starts many clients on a diagnostic. Charge for Radar. Do not give it away.

## Objection Handling

- “Isn’t this just SEO?” Yes, plus a second scoreboard. We will not invoice a third acronym.
- “Can you guarantee ChatGPT cites us?” No. FTC substantiation. We measure, we don’t promise.
- “We need llms.txt.” Google does not use it.
