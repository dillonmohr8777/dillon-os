## What to build from this

1. **The anatomy piece is the first video.** Take one Momentum thing — the
   lead path, the reporting pipeline, the AI division's four lanes — and take
   it apart in paper, layer by layer, the way `@MSchwaibold` takes a component
   apart. One indigo accent marks the signal path, exactly as in
   `ref-06-thread-circuit-brain.png`.
2. **Stop-motion weight, not motion-graphics easing.** The paper frames demand
   it and `@charlierguo`'s post is the proof it is now cheap.
3. **Feed the eight reference frames in as reference**, per the
   `@Miguel07Code` route, rather than describing them in a prompt.
4. **Watch the `@adilinthewild` tutorial first.** It is the owned stack, and
   it names the price range clients already pay.

## Caveats

- The 20 "craft" hits come from a keyword filter over post text; a visually
  perfect but text-light post can slip through. 113 of 148 likes carry video,
  so the corpus is bigger than the shortlist.
- The scroll stopped at 148. There are more likes further back.
- Recreating **technique and production quality** is the goal. Do not reproduce
  another creator's specific piece as Momentum's own work.
