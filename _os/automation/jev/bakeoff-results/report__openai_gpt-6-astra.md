### What happened

We spent $1,113 on three search terms that generated zero recorded conversions, with cost per click ranging from $39 to $54. That is expensive traffic with no measured return from those terms.

“timberline landscaping,” a competitor’s brand, was the top search term by clicks and accounted for 6 of 17 total recorded conversions. Competitor searches contributed to reported results, but those conversions alone do not establish lead quality.

The biggest measurement problem is that call tracking is not installed, even though “Phone call lead” is the primary conversion on 9 of 9 campaigns. That leaves a critical gap in verifying phone leads and assessing performance. We have clear evidence of spending without recorded conversions, but incomplete visibility into the outcome every campaign is prioritizing.
