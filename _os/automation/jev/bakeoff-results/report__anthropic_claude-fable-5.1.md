**What happened**

Three search terms consumed $1,113 this week and produced 0 conversions. Clicks on those terms cost between $39 and $54 each. That is money spent with nothing to show for it.

The term that drove the most clicks was "timberline landscaping," a competitor's brand name. It also accounted for 6 of the 17 conversions recorded across the account. Some of those searchers were likely looking for Timberline specifically, not for you. Picking up a few of them is fine, but a competitor's name should not be the top performing term in a campaign built to sell your services.

There is a bigger issue underneath both points. "Phone call lead" is the primary conversion on 9 of 9 campaigns, and call tracking is not installed. That means we cannot confirm that any of the 17 recorded conversions was an actual phone call from a real prospect, and we cannot tell which terms or ads generated the calls that did come in. The conversion data we are reporting against is not measuring what the campaigns are set up to optimize toward.

In plain terms: $1,113 went to terms with no return, the best measured term is a competitor's name, and the account's main success signal is not being tracked. Fixing the tracking gap comes first. Until it is in place, every optimization decision, including pausing the three dead terms, is being made on incomplete information.
