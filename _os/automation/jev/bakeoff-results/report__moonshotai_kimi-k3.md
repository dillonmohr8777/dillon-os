**What happened**

This week the account spent $1,113 on three search terms that produced zero conversions. Clicks on those terms cost between $39 and $54 each, so the budget was absorbed quickly with nothing to show for it.

The strongest term by clicks was "timberline landscaping," which is a competitor's brand name. It generated 6 of the 17 total conversions this week. That means more than a third of reported conversions came from people searching for a competitor, not for your business. That traffic is fragile. Competitor clicks tend to be expensive, the intent is weaker, and the competitor can push back or outbid us at any time.

The bigger issue is measurement. Call tracking is not installed on the account, yet "phone call lead" is set as the primary conversion on all 9 of 9 campaigns. We cannot confirm which calls actually happened, where they came from, or whether they were real leads. Every conversion figure in this report, including the 6 attributed to the competitor term, is built on a signal we cannot verify.

Bottom line: the account is spending heavily on terms that do not convert, and the conversions it does report cannot be proven. Installing call tracking is the necessary first step before we can judge performance honestly or decide where the budget should go.
