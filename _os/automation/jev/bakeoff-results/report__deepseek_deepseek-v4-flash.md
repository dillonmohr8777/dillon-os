What happened

This week, we spent $1,113 on three search terms that produced zero conversions. Those terms had a cost per click between $39 and $54, meaning we paid a premium for clicks that did nothing. That spend is a direct loss.

The top term by clicks was "timberline landscaping," which is a competitor's brand name. That term drove 6 of our 17 total conversions. While that is helpful, it shows we are reliant on a competitor term to generate results. Our own targeted terms are failing.

The core issue is measurement. Phone call lead is the primary conversion action on all 9 campaigns. Call tracking is not installed. We cannot verify which campaigns or keywords actually generate phone calls. The 17 conversions we see may only represent a fraction of real leads, or they may be misattributed. Without call tracking, we cannot see which terms drive calls. The three dead terms may have generated calls that we cannot see, but the current data says they produced nothing. We must stop spending on those three terms immediately and install call tracking to get accurate conversion data. The $1,113 is unrecoverable, but we can prevent future waste with this fix.
