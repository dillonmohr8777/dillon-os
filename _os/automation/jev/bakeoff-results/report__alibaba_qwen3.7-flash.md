What Happened

We spent $1,113 across three search terms this week and generated zero conversions from those keywords. Those terms carried a cost per click ranging from $39 to $54. Meanwhile, the highest clicking term was timberline landscaping, which delivered six of the seventeen total conversions despite being a competitor brand. We are wasting premium budget on traffic intended for another company instead of capturing new landscaping clients.

The core issue runs deeper than keyword selection. Phone call lead is the primary conversion on 9 of 9 campaigns, yet call tracking is not installed. Without this foundation, we cannot record call volume or connect advertising activity to actual customer inquiries. We are feeding expensive unbranded and competitor queries with no return while remaining blind to our most important metric.

We must pause spending on the three zero conversion terms immediately and shift funds toward tightly targeted landscape services. We also need to install call tracking without delay so every campaign can be evaluated against real phone leads. Optimization halts until these critical gaps close.
