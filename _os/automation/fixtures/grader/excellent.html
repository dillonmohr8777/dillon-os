What we do

Harbor HVAC has kept Fishtown and Northern Liberties warm since 1994. We install and service gas boilers, forced-air furnaces, ductless mini splits, and central air. Two generations of the Kowalczyk family have run this shop out of the same garage on Frankford Avenue, and most of our work still comes from neighbors telling neighbors. We are licensed, insured, and EPA 608 certified, and every technician who shows up at your door has been with us at least three years.

Repairs get a flat-rate quote before any wrench comes out, so the number you hear on the phone is the number on the invoice. Installations come with a written load calculation, because a system sized by guesswork costs you every month it runs. If your building is a Philadelphia rowhome with a hundred-year-old chimney and no returns upstairs, we have already solved that exact problem a few hundred times.
