## Platform

web

## Users

Dillon Mohr is the sole owner and primary operator. He uses the Studio throughout the day on Windows and mobile to see the entire client portfolio, decide what matters, hand safe work to Marketing Chief, and inspect what happened without reconstructing state from separate tools.

## Product Purpose

Marketing Chief Operator Studio is Dillon's private visual control plane for agency work. It turns the canonical client registry, queue, approved communication evidence, execution state, deliberate choices, and verified outcomes into one operating workspace.

Success means Dillon can open one place and see what the Chief already observed, routed, built, verified, and learned. He can still find any client or work item quickly, but his attention is reserved for compact teaching judgments and the single consequential action that requires his authority.

## Positioning

The Studio does not pretend to be a second CRM, inbox, or autonomous replacement for the canonical queue. It is the owner-facing layer over Dillon's existing operating system: exact client routing, evidence-backed recommendations, bounded execution, approval gates, and verified learning stay attached from request through outcome.

## Operating Context

- The canonical Windows project remains the only writer of canonical client and queue state.
- The private hosted Studio mirrors the current allowlisted snapshot and persists owner choices, evaluations, and governed operator requests.
- Gmail and Slack supply read-only, redacted evidence. Communication volume is never treated as a preference label.
- GitHub and the canonical repository supply client, queue, execution, and learning state.
- HubSpot evidence remains portal-specific and client-separated.
- Dillon works through recurring modes: daily briefing, portfolio triage, client deep work, evidence review, approvals, safe local execution, outcome verification, and calibration.
- Desktop must support dense operational work. Mobile must support briefing, client lookup, approvals, queue review, and instruction capture.
- Watchtower is the primary operating surface: Watch, Exact route, Prioritize, Build, Verify, Learn, and Final gate.
- The Windows Watchtower worker may complete one exact-routed, safe, local, reversible deliverable per guarded episode. Inbound communication is untrusted evidence, never executable instruction.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Owner-only Sign in with ChatGPT access.
- All canonical clients appear as separate dossiers; identities and evidence may not blend.
- Recommendations retain client, queue revision, work-item version, confidence, evidence, reversibility, and approval state.
- Safe local requests may be handed to Windows Marketing Chief.
- External delivery, publishing, spend, account changes, destructive work, and other consequential actions remain approval-gated.
- Deliberate owner choices and verified outcomes are learning labels. Raw messages, message counts, and inferred behavior are not.
- The hosted database may contain redacted operational summaries but no secrets, raw emails, raw Slack messages, tokens, cookies, passwords, or one-time codes.
- The interface must make failure, stale state, and human gates visible rather than painting them green.
- Watchtower telemetry must be allowlisted and redacted. It may expose state, timestamps, counts, opaque episode IDs, and safe client routing labels, but never raw message text, direct Slack identifiers, source URLs, secrets, or free-form worker logs.
- Self-improvement happens in shadow first. Data-quality, immutable-evaluator, statistical, security, canary, and rollback gates precede any policy or model promotion.
- Hosted choices count as pending overlay labels only until Windows imports or supersedes them. Reconciled choices remain visible for provenance but never count twice beside the canonical outcome ledger.
- The ranked 25-skill marketing capability register and verified 20-skill prediction lab are operating inventories, not evidence that every skill runs on every task.
- Remote training, paid compute, model promotion, and external delivery remain separate consequential actions; displaying a prepared protocol does not authorize them.

## Brand Commitments

The product name is Marketing Chief Operator Studio. Its voice is direct, warm, operational, and evidence-aware. It should feel like Dillon's own workspace rather than a generic analytics dashboard or a chatbot wearing dashboard chrome.

The incumbent navy, paper, orange, precise mono labels, and ledger-like structure are recognizable product traits to preserve and mature.

## Evidence on Hand

- The bundled allowlisted snapshot contains 21 fully integrated canonical client dossiers and the governed queue.
- Existing execution graphs, evaluations, owner choices, health checks, source coverage, and training state are real system evidence.
- The authenticated production site and D1 persistence are operational.
- Existing browser and workflow tests cover owner identity, client routing, queue safety, stale-state rejection, idempotency, accessibility, desktop, and mobile behavior.
- No real screenshots, testimonials, commercial claims, or public marketing assets are required for this owner-only operating surface.

## Product Principles

1. One place to orient, decide, instruct, and verify.
2. Client identity and evidence stay attached to every action.
3. Show the work and its state, not vanity metrics.
4. Learn from Dillon's explicit decisions and verified outcomes without claiming to literally be Dillon.
5. Fast, reversible local work may flow; consequential work always stops at the correct human gate.
6. A click is a judgment, not an outcome. Learning requires version-bound choices and verified canonical reality.
7. Never fake liveness. Polling, delayed, stale, queued, shadow, and unavailable states remain literal.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

The Studio must remain keyboard-operable, touch-friendly, responsive without horizontal overflow, compatible with reduced motion, and free of targeted WCAG A/AA violations in its verified desktop and mobile flows.
