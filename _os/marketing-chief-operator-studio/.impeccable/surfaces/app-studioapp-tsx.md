## Scope

Primary authenticated Studio application. Mode: Operate.

## Audience and Job

Dillon uses this surface throughout the day to orient across the portfolio, find a client or work item, issue a governed instruction, make approvals and training choices, and inspect verified results.

## Task and Proof

The surface must support briefing, client 360, portfolio work, evidence review, approvals, command capture, execution traces, learning, and health. It proves state through the canonical queue revision, client routing, source coverage, execution graphs, D1 records, and verified outcomes.

## Constraints

Owner-only. No raw communications or secrets. Hosted actions may persist intent and evidence but may not bypass Windows canonical scripts or external-action approval gates. Desktop supports dense work; mobile supports the core daily workflows without horizontal overflow.

## Direction and Memorable Moment

Extend The Operating Ledger. The memorable interaction is the universal owner command: Dillon can state what he wants done, attach the exact client and operating mode, and see that instruction become a visible governed handoff rather than disappearing into chat.

## Unresolved Decisions

None blocking. Preserve the incumbent identity while expanding its interaction and information architecture.
